## Quest Items

| Item | Stats | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Tengu Gland** | Quest item | Dropped by Tengu in BDC. Collect 10 and bring to Canniblas for the Tengu Skill Progression quest. |
| **Hermit Scroll** | Quest item | Dropped by the Hermit (lair drop in BDC Forest). Right-click to read and advance to skill 39. Used for the Hermit Skill Progression quest. Looks like a statue in inventory. |
| **Spectre Statue** (x4) | Quest item | Dropped by the 4 Spectres in BDC Castle. Bring all 4 to the Archaeologist to unlock Crypt access. |
| **Black Vial** | Quest item | Received from Canniblas NPC after turning in 10 Tengu Glands. Brew with 1 Acid Pot to create an Ink Pot. |
| **Ink Pot** | Quest item | Created by brewing a Black Vial with 1 Acid Pot. Drink to advance past skill 35 (to 37). Turn in at the same NPC (Canniblas). |
