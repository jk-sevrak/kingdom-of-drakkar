# BDC Quests

## Crypt Access

**Purpose**: Unlock access to the Crypt dungeon beneath BDC Castle, where the Acidron Alcor and Black Dragon Consort lairs are located.

**Prerequisites**: You must first complete the Trapper climbup in GDH2.

**Steps**:
1. Complete the **Trapper climbup** in GDH2 (Green Dragon Hatcheries, level 2)
2. Enter BDC via the Arbiter NPC in the southwest corner of GDH2
3. Find and talk to the **Trapper** in the BDC forest to activate the Archaeologist quest
4. Travel to the **BDC Castle**
5. Kill the **4 Spectres** inside BDC Castle -- each drops a statue
6. Collect all **4 Spectre statues**
7. Take the statues to the **Archaeologist** NPC in the BDC forest (located in the southeast area)
8. **Reward**: Access to the stairs leading down to the Crypt in BDC Castle

## Tengu Skill Progression (Skill 35 to 37)

**Purpose**: Advance past skill 35 (to skill 37). This quest can be skipped in favor of the Hermit Skill Progression quest if preferred.

**Steps**:
1. Hunt **Tengu** creatures in BDC and collect **10 Tengu Glands** (black gland items)
2. Bring the 10 glands to the **Canniblas** NPC in the BDC forest (located in the northwest area)
3. Hand in the 10 glands to receive a **Black Vial**
4. Obtain **1 Acid Pot** (dropped by Leeches in GDH)
5. **Brew** the Black Vial with the Acid Pot to create an **Ink Pot**
6. Return to the **same NPC** (Canniblas) with the Ink Pot
7. Hold the Ink Pot in your right hand and click on Canniblas
8. Follow his dialog, telling him the words in **ALL UPPERCASE LETTERS**
9. **Reward**: Advance past skill 35 to skill 37

---

## Hermit Skill Progression (Skill 37 to 39)

**Purpose**: Advance past skill 37 (to skill 39). This quest can also be used as an alternative to the Tengu Skill Progression quest to skip directly ahead.

**Steps**:
1. Travel to the **southwest corner** of the BDC forest area
2. Locate and engage the **Hermit** lair boss
   - **Warning**: The Hermit can stun and hits hard. Come prepared.
3. Slay the Hermit
4. Pick up the **Hermit Scroll** (it looks like a statue in your inventory)
5. **Right-click** the scroll while holding it to read it
6. **Reward**: Advance to skill 39
