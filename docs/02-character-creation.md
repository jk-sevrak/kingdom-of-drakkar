# Character Creation

## Attributes

Characters in Kingdom of Drakkar have 8 primary attributes:

| Attribute | Abbreviation | Effect |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **Strength** | STR | Physical power, melee damage, carrying capacity. Rumored to affect HP gains per level. Barbarians get HP bonuses from STR. |
| **Intelligence** | INT | Mental ability, Mentalist spell power. Determines starting EP and EP gains per level for Mentalists. |
| **Wisdom** | WIS | Divine/healing power. Determines starting EP and EP gains per level for Healers. |
| **Constitution** | CON | Hit points/health. Affects Hit Doctor costs (higher CON = cheaper). Below 17 CON, there's a chance of permanent hit loss on death. |
| **Charisma** | CHA | Controls training costs, Hit Doctor costs, and many other economic factors in Nork. Higher CHA significantly reduces costs. |
| **Luck** | LCK | Affects various random outcomes. Very hard to fix after creation. |
| **Willpower** | WIL | Mental fortitude, resistance to mental attacks. Important for all classes. Fixable with willpower pots but they're expensive. |
| **Agility** | AGI | Agility, accuracy, dodge chance. Important for Thieves and Martial Artists. |

### Rolling Tips

When rolling a character, the key advice from experienced players:

- **Prioritize Charisma and Luck** above all else. Aim for both to be 16 or higher. These are the hardest stats to fix later.
- Stats like CON, AGI, STR, WIL, WIS, and INT are all fixable with potions.
- Charisma can technically be fixed via the Alerian n/n quest, and Luck via Mjorin Luck pots, but these are nearly impossible for new players to obtain.
- Each class has a key stat (INT for Mentalists, WIS for Healers, etc.) - roll it as high as possible for a head start.

## Gender

Gender affects base character stats. The game had separate character worksheets for male and female characters, reflecting different stat distributions.

## Races

The game features multiple playable races, each with different stat modifiers:

- **Human** - Balanced stats, no extreme bonuses or penalties
- **Elf** - Higher Intelligence/Dexterity, lower Strength/Constitution
- **Dwarf** - Higher Strength/Constitution, lower Intelligence/Charisma
- **Halfling** - Higher Dexterity, lower Strength
- **Half-Elf** - Mix of Human and Elf traits
- **Half-Orc** - Higher Strength, lower Charisma/Intelligence

From the developer Q&A, Brad noted that he planned to add "more racial diversity" rather than adding items to make races more similar, preserving meaningful racial differences.

## Classes

Kingdom of Drakkar has 7 playable classes:

| Class | Max HP (Hit Doc) | Key Stats | Description |
|-------|-----------------|-----------|-------------|
| **Fighter** | 480 | STR, CON | Primary melee combat class. Can use all weapon and armor types. Straightforward combat. |
| **Barbarian** | 580 | STR, CON | Highest HP in the game. Has Berserk ability (extra attacks but lose control). Can't wear heavy armor effectively while zerked. |
| **Paladin** | 480 | STR, WIS | Holy warrior combining combat and healing. Has MaxStrike and Haste abilities. |
| **Martial Artist** | 480 | DEX, STR | Unarmed combat specialist. Special Chi abilities at higher levels (Chi Sweep at skill 17). Hardest class to grow. |
| **Thief** | 380 | DEX, AGI | Stealth class with Backstab, Mug, and Hide abilities. Can pick locks. Skill gains were "100% due for an overhaul" as of 2002. |
| **Healer** | 380 | WIS, CHA | Primary healing/support class. Gets first attack spell (Assault) at skill 3. Essential for parties. Gains extra weapon attacks every 8th experience level. |
| **Mentalist** | 350 | INT, WIL | Psionic/mental powers class. Lowest HP but most powerful spells. Gets only 1 physical attack per round EVER. Best coining class (Transmute at skill 10). |

### Multi-classing

Fighter/Mentalist (F/M) was the only confirmed multi-class combination. Players asked about more multi-classing options, but Brad expressed caution about balance implications.

### Merchants

"Merchants" were mentioned as a potential class. Brad's response: "I doubt i will ever have time to do that... BUT... 'MERCHANTING' may soon become very profitable."

## Skills System

Characters have multiple skill tracks:

- **Primary Skill** - Main class skill (e.g., Mentalist, Healer, Fighter)
- **Secondary Skill** - A secondary weapon/combat skill
- **Tertiary Skill** - Additional skills
- **Other Skills** - Up to 3 additional skill slots

Skill is separate from Experience Level. A character might be "18/15" meaning experience level 18, skill level 15. Experienced players emphasized that **skill matters far more than level**.

Training costs increase with skill level. At skill 23+, training costs were around 450 million gold; at skill 29+, over 900 million gold. The "chit trainer" in the Guild Hall provided an alternate training method that didn't have a hard cap.

### Skill Gain Goals (from Developer)

- **1% per hour minimum** at higher skill levels with efficient hunting
- **3% per hour** at skill level 15
- Solo vs. party: Party play provides significantly better skill gain
- The game was designed so that "people working together can accomplish more than an individual"
