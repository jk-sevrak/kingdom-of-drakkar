# Fighter Classes & Thief

## Fighter

The Fighter is the primary melee combat class and the most straightforward to play.

- **Max HP**: 480 (via Hit Doctor)
- **Key Stats**: Strength, Constitution
- **Can use**: All weapon and armor types
- **Combat**: Direct melee damage dealer. Forms the backbone of any party as a "stick" (slang for melee fighter).

Fighters gain extra attacks per round as they level up and serve as the front-line tank in parties. They are easier to build and finance than psionic classes.

## Barbarian

The Barbarian has the highest HP cap of any class and unique Berserk mechanics.

- **Max HP**: 580 (via Hit Doctor) - highest in the game
- **Key Stats**: Strength, Constitution (STR gives Barbarians HP bonuses)
- **Special Ability**: **Berserk** - grants extra attacks ("fury rounds") but character loses control and may attack party members, pick up items from ground, or run off screen
- **Armor**: Limited armor options. In Cobrahn, double Yeti was the best available (players requested better Cob armor for Barbs, and Brad indicated plans to add new lair armor)

### Barbarian Issues (from 2002 Q&A)

- **Party unfriendly**: Zerking causes Barbarians to grab items from ground and run off screen, disrupting party hunting
- **Haste removed from Zerked state**: Unlike Paladins (MaxStrike + Haste) and MAs (RP + Haste), Barbarians couldn't be Hasted while Zerked. Brad was "seriously considering" restoring this
- **Mid-level gap**: High-level Barbs were fine, but mid-level Barbs got "overly pounded" and had nowhere to hunt effectively
- **Pooling**: MAs could pool off Cob quest HPs, but Barbs couldn't. Brad said he'd "see what all it would affect/ruin"

## Paladin

The Paladin is a holy warrior combining combat and healing capabilities.

- **Max HP**: 480 (via Hit Doctor)
- **Key Stats**: Strength, Wisdom
- **Special Abilities**: MaxStrike (powerful attack), Haste capability, some healing
- **Role**: Hybrid fighter/healer. Less healing than a pure Healer but more durable

Paladins were considered strong in the 2002 era, with players noting that their MaxStrike + Haste (2-3 attacks per round) gave them significant advantages over Barbarians.

## Martial Artist (MA)

The Martial Artist is an unarmed combat specialist and the hardest class to grow.

- **Max HP**: 480 (via Hit Doctor)
- **Key Stats**: Dexterity, Strength
- **Special Abilities**: Chi abilities starting at skill 17 (Chi Sweep), Sweep, RP (Rapid Punch - 2-3 attacks per round with Haste)
- **Armor Impact**: MA skill effectiveness is hampered by heavy armor. A skill 10 MA wearing Red Dragon Scales doesn't function as well as an MA wearing a lighter Griffy Cloak.

### MA Issues (from 2002 Q&A)

- **"Hardest class to grow"** - Brad's own words
- **Big gap in abilities**: Between Sweep and Chi Sweep (skill 17), MAs had limited options. A new ability was planned to replace the non-functional DT (Death Touch)
- **Hit rate**: Players reported MAs missing 3 out of 4 attacks near the 85% skill cap before next level - Brad said if confirmed, it's a bug
- **Chi Sweep underperforming**: Players reported that regular Sweep was better than Chi Sweep, which Brad said needed investigation

## Thief

The Thief is a stealth and utility class with unique mechanics. Thieves can form psionic disciplines but **at the cost of health points** (not EP like Mentalists/Healers).

- **Max HP**: 380 (via Hit Doctor)
- **Key Stats**: Luck, Charisma, Agility (helps hide/steal/defense), Strength
- **Special Abilities**: Steal (skill 1+), Hide (skill 4-5), Backstab (skill 8), Mug (skill 10), Door, Pick locks
- **Skilling**: Via Backstab (BS) and Mug. Backstab gives BOTH weapon AND thief skill. Thieves can skill extremely fast early (skill 4 in first 10 minutes possible by stealing from dungeon crits before killing them).

### Thief Progression

| Skill | Ability |
|-------|---------|
| 1-3 | Steal from creatures (steal 10x then kill) |
| 4-5 | Hide in shadows |
| 8 | Backstab (attack from hiding with increased damage) |
| 10 | Mug (combines backstab movement with stealing). Class train at VT trainer for Door and others. |
| 12+ | Focus on weapon skilling |

### Thief Weapon Choices
- **Longsword/Greatsword**: Hit hard but harder to hide afterward
- **Dagger**: Easy to hide but less damage. +2 Silver Dagger from thief trainer; +3 Bullywog Dagger (returning, throwable)
- **Bow/Thrown**: Easiest to hide with. Poison Bow hits hard but poison kills steal XP.
- **Stiletto**: Power weapon from Breshard lair. Silver/glowing with poison.
- **Silver Griffin Sword > Slicer > Saber** (longsword progression)

### Thief Gear
- **HP Helm** (later Power Helm)
- **RB Sash** (Ratburrow)
- **Agility Rings**
- **Full Plate from Levi** (level 15 trade)
- **Black Ninja Robes** (+2 AGI, from Maeling -3)
- **Camo Robes** (+2 AGI, +1 hiding, from Aleria Seldair -1/-2)

### Thief Tips
- Steal from dungeon crits before killing them for extra thief skill
- Train thief skill to 5 on ALL classes for theft defense and to see hidden players
- Mug replaces steal at skill 10 - combines backstab movement with stealing

### Thief Mechanics

- **Stealing from players**: An intentional hostile act. The victim can legally defend themselves (kill the thief).
- **PK by spell-casters**: If a spell-caster accidentally kills a hidden thief with area spells, it's treated as an accident (not grounds for retaliation), unlike deliberate theft.
- **Cob trainer**: Only trained up to skill 19, which players felt was too low for the difficulty of Cob content. Brad noted the chit trainer in the Guild Hall was meant to supplement this.

### Thief Issues (from 2002 Q&A)

- **Skill gains "DEFINETLY not working the way i wanted"** - Brad acknowledged thief skilling was "100% due for an overhaul"
- **Backstab vs. weapon skill**: Gains for backstab were 5:1 compared to regular weapon attacks, making BS skill-building frustrating
- **Mug skilling**: "Boring and not my idea of fun" according to players

## Weapon Skills

All classes can develop weapon skills independent of their class skill:

- **Staff** - Recommended secondary for Healers/Mentalists (best caster weapons are staffs)
- **Longsword** - Common secondary for all classes (versatile, good fallback)
- **Halberd** (Hally) - Used by some fighter types. Players asked for power-weapons (P-weapons) for hally users
- **Martial Arts** - Unarmed combat skill. Best blocking but hampered by armor weight.
- **Dagger** - Used by Thieves
- **Short Sword** - Another option

### Universal Skill Recommendations

From the ICE guild's getting started guide:
- **Train Martial Arts to skill 10 on ALL classes** - provides dodge bonus and extra attacks for fighters
- **Train Thief to skill 5 on ALL classes** - provides theft defense and ability to see hidden players
- **Recommended primary weapons by class**: Barbarians = halberd, Psi classes = staff, Thieves = thrown weapons, Fighters/Paladins = great sword and long sword

### Known Power Weapons (P-weapons)

Weapons capable of destroying the Snowbeast (a quest-oriented boss):
- Power Longsword (P-LS)
- Saber
- Vamps (gauntlets)
- Chipper Staff
- Stiletto

Brad considered the Snowbeast a "quest-oriented kill" and preferred putting effort into new content rather than adding more P-weapons for old content.
