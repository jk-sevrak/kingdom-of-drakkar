# SDC Quests

## Level Progression Quests

### Level 76 Progression

**Reward**: Ability to progress past level 75 to level 76

**Steps**:
1. Travel to the surface area of SDC Forest, north of **Jarvoh**
2. Kill the roaming named NPC enemies until a **River Nugget** drops (listed as a rock -- "A small river borne golden nugget")
3. Take the River Nugget to **Jarvoh** near the entrance of SDC
4. You can now progress to level 76

---

### Level 78 Progression

**Reward**: Ability to progress past level 76 to level 78

**Requirements**: Must be level 76 to turn in

**Steps**:
1. Obtain a **Climbing Pick** first (see Progression 1 quest below)
2. Use the Climbing Pick to access the Wyvern area from the northwest hex of SDC Forest
3. Kill Wyverns around the climb-up spot leading to Wyverns +2 until a **Wyvern Talon** drops
4. Bring the talon to **Arpigo** on the east side of Wyverns +2
5. You can now progress to level 78

---

### Level 85 Progression

**Reward**: Ability to progress past level 78 to level 85 and skill 40

**Requirements**: Must be level 78 to turn in

**Steps**:
1. Collect **64 Small Golden Nuggets** by killing ElderGnomes in the Gnome Area or picking up nuggets from the cave floor
2. Head to the smelting pot in the Gnome Area
3. Open the brew window and smelt **8 Small Nuggets** at a time to create a **Medium (Fist-Sized) Nugget** (no brewing skill needed)
4. Smelt **8 Medium Nuggets** to create a **Large Nugget**
5. Take the Large Nugget to **Armon** in the Gnome Area

**Important Note**: If you did not complete the BDC Hermit quest before this to advance to skill 39, you will be capped at skill 39. To fix this, you must turn in another Large Nugget.

---

## Area Access Quests

### Progression 1 -- Climbing Pick (Wyvern Area Access)

**Reward**: Climbing Pick that grants access to the Wyvern area

**Steps**:
1. Head to the Thumper/Rockman Caves in SDC
2. Kill Thumpers and Rockmen until you collect **2 Thumper Rocks** (fragments/gems)
3. Take them to **Mneer**, located on the east side of SDC Forest (behind a wall)
4. Mneer will take the rocks from your sack and give you a **Climbing Pick**
5. **Critical**: Drop the Climbing Pick on the ground, then pick it back up -- this ties it to you
6. Take the Climbing Pick to the **most northwest hex of SDC Forest** and climb up to access the Wyvern area

---

### Progression 2 -- Miner49er Area Access (Optional)

**Reward**: Key to access the Miner49er area

**Steps**:
1. Gather **64 Small Golden Nuggets** and smelt them into **1 Large Nugget** (same process as Level 85 quest)
2. Take the Large Nugget to **Modax** in the Gnome Area
3. Modax will trade it for a **Key**
4. Hold the key in your right hand and walk onto the **SE hex in the Gnome caves** to enter the Miner49er area
5. **Beware**: There is a lair inside

**Note**: This is optional -- you can bypass this area by getting a Mass Teleport past it.

---

### Sick Caves Access

**Reward**: Access to Sick Caves -1

**Steps**:
1. Travel to the north area of SDC Forest
2. Kill the four types of animals in the area: **Direwolf**, **Mistpanther**, **Rabiddeer**, and **Gorebear**
3. Take each killed animal to **TrapperJohn** in the northeast area of SDC Forest to tan them into furs:
   - Direwolf -> Wolf Fur
   - Mistpanther -> Panther Fur
   - Rabiddeer -> Rabid Deer Fur
   - Gorebear -> Gore Bear Fur
4. Take all 4 furs west to **Mac** (the NPC who asks for them)
5. Trade in all 4 furs
6. You can now use `down` on the nearby hole to enter Sick Caves -1

---

### Sick Caves 2 Access

**Reward**: Access to climb down to Sick Caves -2

**Steps**:
1. Enter Sick Caves -1 (requires Sick Caves Access quest completion)
2. Kill **Wraiths wearing sashes** until you collect **5 Wraith Cloth**
3. Give the 5 Wraith Cloth to **Mac'** (Macsfriend) in the southeast corner of Sick Caves -1
4. **Critical**: When turning in your 5th Wraith Cloth, you MUST select the dialog option asking about the climbdown. If you miss this dialog, you will need another Wraith Cloth to get the flag that allows you to climb down.
5. You can now climb down to Sick Caves -2

---

### OzonousMax Access

**Reward**: Access to OzonousMax's lair

**Steps**:
1. Kill **Skar** in Lava City and collect the **Skar Key**
2. Kill **Spur** in Lava City and collect the **Spur Key**
3. Use the Skar Key on the **Skar Door** in Lava City
4. Use the Spur Key on the **Spur Door** in Lava City
5. Both doors must be opened to reach OzonousMax's lair

---

## Quirk Quests

### Cavern Creeper I

**Reward**: First point in Cavern Creeper quirk

**Requirements**: Level 76

**Steps**:
1. Enter the Thumper/Rockman Caves in SDC
2. Find and kill the **GiantBat** lair boss (stuns, damages gear)
3. Pick up the **Cavebat Skin Scroll** (bat page) it drops
4. Bring the scroll to **Cargon** in the Rockman Cave area of SDC
5. You gain the first Cavern Creeper quirk point

---

### Cavern Creeper II

**Reward**: Second point in Cavern Creeper quirk

**Steps**:
1. Kill **ElderGnomes** in the Gnome Area until a **Gnome Head** drops (found under the gem category)
2. Take the Gnome Head to **Jaxon**, located in the safe area west of the gnome caves
3. You gain the second Cavern Creeper quirk point

---

### Tree Hugger I

**Reward**: First point in Tree Hugger quirk

**Steps**:
1. Collect **8 Crystalline Plate Armors** by killing Gnomes, ElderGnomes, and Crushers in the Gnome Area
2. Take the 8 platemails to the **smelting pot** in the Gnome Area
3. Smelt them into a **Silver Extract**
4. Bring the Silver Extract to **Juan** in Gnome Town
5. You gain the first Tree Hugger quirk point

---

### Tree Hugger II

**Reward**: Second point in Tree Hugger quirk

**Steps**:
1. Find a **Broadleaf Arrowhead** flower in the grass area around Monrah in SDC2, which is north from the second level of the gnome cave
2. Alternatively, find the same flower anywhere in Invader's Refuge
3. Bring the flower to **Monrah** near Betrayer
4. You gain the second Tree Hugger quirk point

---

### Crystal Resistance I

**Reward**: First point in Crystal Resistance quirk

**Steps**:
1. Complete the **Sick Caves 2 Access** quest first (5 Wraith Cloth to Mac')
2. Enter Sick Caves -2
3. Kill **Spectres wearing sashes** until you collect **5 Spectre Silk**
4. Bring the 5 Spectre Silk to **Mac'** (Macsfriend) in the southeast corner of Sick Caves -1
5. You gain the first Crystal Resistance quirk point

---

### Crystal Resistance II

**Reward**: Second point in Crystal Resistance quirk

**Steps**:
1. Enter Sick Caves -2
2. Kill creatures until a **Dragon Scale** drops
3. Bring the Dragon Scale to **Marburn**, located in the west area of Sick Caves -2
4. You gain the second Crystal Resistance quirk point

---

### Dark Knowledge

**Reward**: Dark Knowledge quirk (also allows you to hit the pylons before Corosonous as a melee user)

**Steps**:
1. Collect **40 Dark Knowledge Pages** from across the SDC areas. Pages are found in increments of 5 in specific locations:
   - Pages 1-5: SDC Forest
   - Pages 6-10: Thumper Caves
   - Pages 11-15: Sick Caves
   - Pages 16-20: SDC3 Solo Area
   - Pages 21-25: SDC3 Meadows
   - Pages 26-30: Lava City
   - Pages 31-35: SDC3 Water Area
   - Pages 36-40: SDC2 IceTitan Area
2. Additionally, **Corosonous** and **OzonousMax** each drop **10 random pages** (from the 1-35 range)
3. Obtain the **Dark Knowledge Book** from **Skar**, **Spur**, or **OzonousMax**
4. Completing the book with all 40 pages unlocks the Dark Knowledge quirk

---

## Stat & Upgrade Quests

### AGI 40

**Reward**: Base Agility increased to 40

**Requirements**: Level 80

**Steps**:
1. Enter the SDC3 Solo Caves (stairs down from Lava City)
2. Find and drink **AGI Pots** (Agility Bottles)
3. Each pot increases your AGI by 1, up to a base of 40

---

### Stat 35 (STR/INT/WIS/CON)

**Reward**: Increase a base stat to 35

**Requirements**: 50 million gold in bank

**Steps**:
1. Find stat flowers throughout SDC3 areas:
   - **California Poppy** = STR
   - **Canary Island Cress** = INT
   - **Card Thistle** = WIS
   - **Cardoon** = CON
2. Hold the desired flower in your **right hand**
3. Take it to **Rogue** in Lava Town
4. With 50m in your bank, Rogue will grant a stat increase for the flower you are holding

---

### +12 Titan Weapon Upgrade

**Reward**: Improved Titan weapon (increased damage and blocking)

**Steps**:
1. Collect **8 Crystalline Leathers** from gnomes in the Gnome Area
2. Collect **8 Crystalline Plate Armors** from gnomes in the Gnome Area
3. Smelt the **8 leathers** into a **Crystalline Extract** at the smelting pot
4. **Enchant** the Crystalline Extract to **+6**
5. Smelt the **8 plates** into a **Silver Extract** at the smelting pot
6. Smelt the following together:
   - +6 Crystalline Extract
   - Silver Extract
   - Brew Pot (Swamp Water -- "A bottle containing a putrid acidic fluid")
   - Small Gold Nugget
7. This produces a **Refined Fluidic Crystal**
8. **Enchant** the Refined Fluidic Crystal to **+6**
9. Take the +6 Refined Fluidic Crystal and your **Titan weapon** to **Alex** in the Gnome Area
10. Alex will improve the weapon's damage and blocking ability

---

### Root Armor Upgrade

**Reward**: +1 to base armor (slot 1) Root Protective Quality per Gumbo turned in

**Scaling**: 1 Gumbo for +1, 3 for +2, 5 for +3, etc.

**Steps**:
1. Find **1 Omnilla Skull** by killing Omnilla in the SDC3 Water Area
2. Find **1 Chum** by killing Carnifish in the SDC3 Water Area
3. Buy **1 Brew Pot** from a shop
4. Brew the Omnilla Skull, Chum, and Brew Pot together in the **SDC Town fountain** (no brewing skill needed, but they must be brewed specifically in SDC Town fountain)
5. This creates **Gumbo**
6. Trade the Gumbo to **Worster** (NPC in SDC Town) for the Root Protective Quality upgrade

---

### Root Weapon Upgrade

**Reward**: Increased defense on a weapon

**Scaling**: 1 Lava Twig for +1, 3 for +2, 5 for +3, etc.

**Steps**:
1. Collect **Lava Twigs** (dropped by OzonousMax, Corosonous, or Firegiants in Lava City)
2. Bring the Lava Twigs to **Hermit** on the east side of Lava City
3. Hermit will upgrade the defense of your weapon
