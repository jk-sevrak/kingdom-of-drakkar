# Healer Class Guide

*"Life to allies, death to enemies."*

The Healer is a challenging class to build. With a Healer, it's brain power over muscles. Trying to hack and slash your way through Drakkar with a Healer is one of the best ways to end up dead.

## Building a Healer

### Key Stats
- **Wisdom** is the key stat - determines EP gains and healing power
- **Charisma** 16+ for affordable training and Hit Doc
- **Luck** 16+ (hardest to fix later)
- **Willpower** important for all classes, expensive to fix

### Gearing Up

**Armor Progression:**
1. **Scavenged gear** - Pick up anything from dungeon floors at the start
2. **Red Dragon Scales** - First real armor set. Adequate for a long time.
3. **Big Yeti / Kitty / Rak Cloak** - Considered "newbie gear," most players willing to help you get it
4. **Blue Shield** - Good starter shield
5. **Mama Scales** - Upgrade from Red Dragon when armor isn't blocking attacks often enough. Requires a party of sticks and healers to obtain.

**Cloak Choices (situational):**
- **Rak Cloak** - For areas with heavy assault damage (-5, -6, -7)
- **Enmiss Robe** - For hunting M-1
- **Psi-cutting gear** - For hunting liches
- **Fire protection** - For Mama hunts

**Key Equipment:**
- **HP Helm** - Hit Point helms are essential. They appraise for 8K, found as early as Nork-2. Glowing description = HP or EP helm.
- **Ratburrow Sash** - Preferred over Combat Sash. Same combat adds plus CritCure and Ratburrow teleport access.
- **Muzi (Mini Uzi) Amulet** - +2 EP regen per round. Obtained via Door Pearl (from ghost on -3.5) + 2 fishery bows + key from M-2. Must visit Peewee on km-2.
- **Uzi Amulet** - +5 EP regen per round. The best amulet. Requires killing Papa Lich (fight through 5 liches first). Demands a party of sticks, a healer, and a ment with infravision.
- **Red Dragon Gauntlets** - Standard gauntlets for Healers.
- **Rings** - Think "defense." A 0/4 ring (0 offense, 4 defense) is better than a 3/3 for Healers since they aren't fighters.

**Weapons:**
- Recommend skilling in **Staff** and **Longsword** as secondary skills
- **Slith Staff** (Nork) - +4 Wisdom bonus. Hard to get.
- **Lori Staff** (Cob) - +6 skill bonus and +6 EP regen. Combined with Uzi = 11 EP/round regen.
- Bring your staff and longsword skills to 10 before moving on. Do this around ment/healer skill 10.

### Skilling Strategy

- Healers don't get their first attack spell until **skill 3** (Assault). Until then, use Mend on yourself/others and fight with weapons.
- Primary attack disc: **Assault** - can target any visible creature
- In parties: Let sticks fight melee. Heal/cure party members. Use Assault on ranged attackers.
- **Healing and curing party-mates gains you skill** - don't just focus on kills.

### Maxxing (Hit Points & Energy Points)

- **HP Max**: 380 (via Hit Doctor, available at level 13, south of Volcano Town)
- **EP Max**: 250 in Nork (via EP pots at 500K each from the funhouse, ~1-5 EP per pot, avg ~3)
- **DO NOT rest past level 13** until you've maxxed HP to 380. Then rest to gain HP beyond the Hit Doc cap.
- Hit Doc costs: 15-30+ million gold depending on Charisma. Borrow +3 CHA rings to save millions.
- Resting tip: Wear Wisdom rings and drink from the Rat Burrow strength fountain (requires RB sash) before resting, to maximize both EP and HP gains.
- EP maxxing without resting costs ~40 million gold (80+ EP pots). Hard but worth it.
- Healers gain extra weapon attacks every **8th experience level** (2 attacks at level 8, 3 at level 16, etc.)

### Going to Cobrahn

- Minimum level 18 required. Recommend skill 15+ before going.
- First priority: Complete **Replacement** and **Juntes Ring** hit point quests (+100 HP each in Cob only)
- Start in **Tree Town** (safe), then move to **Timmy Town**
- Timmy Caves are the "newbie" area of Cob, but crits can still kill you easily
- Pick up floor twigs: CritCure (level 20), Fireball (25), Icestorm (25), Icestorm (20) - USE THEM!
- Strategy vs Timmy crits: Use Assault until half health, then Stun to prevent healing. Mace-carrying crits are immune to most spells but can be poisoned.
- Basic Cob gear: Padded GD Scales, 2 yellows, Graagh rings, Padded IV helm
- Get a **Curvy Longsword** (+5 sword, found on Timmy -1, ties) as fallback weapon

### Party Role

- **CritCure tip**: When reviving a dead party member in combat, grab the corpse and **move to a safe area first**. CritCure drains half your HP - casting it in the middle of a fight often gets you killed immediately after.
- Keep party members healed and cured
- Use Assault on ranged attackers that sticks can't reach
- A smart, generous Healer is welcome almost everywhere in Drakkar
