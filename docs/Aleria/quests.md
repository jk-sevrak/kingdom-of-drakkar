## Good Aleria Quest (requires G/G alignment)

**Requirements**: G/G alignment + Bandit Lord Shortsword + Winter Wolf Fur (tanned) + Seldari Queen Goblet + Mujaba's Key + GoblinKing Axe

**Reward**: Choose one of the following:
- `"tal, fortune"` -- 1 million gold
- `"tal, fame"` -- Charisma Potion
- Good Alerian Helm (Feather Fall lv15, +1 Willpower, dances when stunned)
- Also drops Koss's Morningstar from Lord Koss

**Full NPC Dialog Chain**:

1. Find Loranon, say: `"lor, sever"`
2. Find Caspian, drop the Bandit Lord Shortsword on his hex, say: `"cas, kassel"` then `"cas, evidence"`
3. Find Duke Talinar, say: `"tal, quest"`
4. Find Eldoral, say: `"eld, allegiance"`
5. Find Caspian, say: `"cas, promotion"`
6. Find Duke Talinar, say: `"tal, fur"`
7. Tan a Winter Wolf and take the Winter Wolf Fur
8. Find Arogar, drop the Winter Wolf Fur on his hex, say: `"aro, fur"` -- take the coins he drops
9. Find Duke Talinar, drop the coins from Arogar, say: `"tal, fur"` then `"tal, solution"` then `"tal, coins"`
10. Find Caspian, say: `"cas, promotion"`
11. Find Lady Ela, say: `"lad, hello"`
12. Find King Borav, drop GoblinKing Axe on his hex, say in sequence: `"kin, alliance"` -> `"kin, goblin"` -> `"kin, treaty"` -> `"kin, deal"` -> `"kin, axe"` -- take the ring he drops
13. Find Lady Ela, hold the Seldari Queen Goblet in hand, say in sequence: `"lad, liadril"` -> `"lad, secret"` -> `"lad, seldari"` -> `"lad, allies"` -> `"lad, goblet"`
14. Find Caspian, say: `"cas, dub"` -- this makes you **Champion of Carrena**
15. Find Duke Talinar, say: `"tal, quest"`
16. (Have Mujaba's Key at this point)
17. Find Loranon, say: `"lor, sever"`
18. Use Mujaba's Key to get past the first skull in the dungeon you find yourself in
19. Find Hessian, say: `"hes, ela"` then `"hes, borav"` then `"hes, talinar"`
20. Kill Lord Koss and take Koss's Morningstar to Duke Talinar, choose your reward

## Evil Aleria Quest (requires E/E alignment)

**Requirements**: E/E alignment + Bandit Lord Shortsword + Seldari Queen Goblet + Winter Wolf Fur (tanned)

**Reward**: Choose one of the following:
- `"lor, fortune"` -- 1 million gold
- Evil Helm
- You can also keep Duke Talinar's Tower Shield

**Full NPC Dialog Chain**:

1. Find Loranon, say: `"lor, sever"`
2. Find Culcula, say: `"cul, hello"` then `"cul, allegiance"`
3. Find Hessian, say in sequence: `"hes, pass skull"` then `"hes, talinar"` then `"hes, pass skull"` then `"hes, ela"` then `"hes, pass skull"` then `"hes, borav"`
4. Find Lord Koss, say: `"lor, hello"` then `"lor, kneel"`
5. Find Koron, drop the Bandit Lord Shortsword on his hex, say: `"kor, hello"` then `"kor, mission"` then `"kor, banditlord"` -- take the shortsword from the ground
6. Find Lord Koss, say: `"lor, ela"`
7. Find Culcula, say: `"cul, ela"`
8. Find Lady Ela, hold the Seldari Queen Goblet in hand, say: `"ela, hello"` then `"ela, goblet"`
9. Find Koron, say: `"kor, hello"`
10. Tan a Winter Wolf and take the Winter Wolf Fur
11. Find Arogar, drop the Winter Wolf Fur on his hex, say: `"aro, fur"` -- take the coins he drops
12. Find Koron, say: `"kor, hello"`
13. Find Lord Koss, say: `"lor, hello"` then `"lor, quest"`
14. Hold a Pink Leaf in your hand, go upstairs to meet Duke Talinar
15. Kill Duke Talinar and take his Tower Shield
16. Take the Tower Shield to Lord Koss and choose your reward

## Neutral Aleria Quest (requires N/N alignment)

**Requirements**: N/N alignment + Bandit Lord Shortsword + Winter Wolf Fur (tanned) + GoblinKing Axe + Seldari Queen Goblet + Pink Leaves (2)

**Reward**: Choose one of the following:
- `"lor, fame"` -- Charisma Potion
- `"lor, fortune"` -- Loranon's Staff (+5 combat, +1 EP regen, +1 WIS)

**Full NPC Dialog Chain**:

1. Find Loranon, say: `"lor, sever"` then `"lor, peace"` then `"lor, neutrality"` then `"lor, task"` then `"lor, neutrality"`
2. Find Caspian, drop the Bandit Lord Shortsword on his hex, say: `"cas, kassel"` then `"cas, evidence"`
3. Tan a Winter Wolf and take the Winter Wolf Fur
4. Find Arogar, drop the Winter Wolf Fur on his hex, say: `"aro, hello"` then `"aro, fur"` -- take the coins he drops
5. Find Duke Talinar, say: `"tal, fur"` then `"tal, solution"` then `"tal, coins"` then `"tal, redemption"` (if you want the atone)
6. Find King Borav, drop the GoblinKing Axe on his hex, say: `"kin, hello"` then `"kin, axe"` then `"kin, alliance"` -- take the ring he drops
7. Find Lady Ela, hold the Seldari Queen Goblet in hand, say: `"lad, hello"` then `"lad, goblet"`
8. Find Loranon, click him, say: `"lor, task"`
9. Find Eldoral, drop a Pink Leaf on his hex, say: `"eld, hello"` then `"eld, leaf"`
10. Find Culcula, drop a Pink Leaf on his hex, say: `"cul, hello"` then `"cul, leaf"`
11. Find Loranon, say: `"lor, armistice"` then choose your reward

## Bandit Lord Access

1. Find the NPC in NW Caves, say: `"npc, password"`
2. Take the password to Tiny, say: `"tin, <password>"`
3. **Note**: If you leave, you must re-do the quest

## Light Ring

1. Take a GoblinKing Axe to King Borav
2. Drop it on the ground, say: `"kin, axe"` then `"kin, alliance"`
3. **Reward**: Light Ring

## Neutral Helm

1. Trade Loranon's Staff to Tamorwil
2. **Reward**: Neutral Helm

## Poison Amulet

1. Locate the statues in Seldari Forest and read all of them
2. Drop the Light Ring on Elshea's hex
3. Repeat the words from the statues to Elshea: `"els, sol kalier kalehn"`
4. **Reward**: Poison Amulet

---

## Seldari Forest Access

Wear a Light Ring to enter the Seldari Forest (through the waterfall NW of the 3-bird seller).

---

## Seldari Dungeon Access

Wear a Poison Amulet to enter the Seldari Dungeon.

---

## Seldari Queen Access

Hold a Seldari Eye while stepping onto the Pylon in -2 to be teleported to the Seldari Queen.

## Tarak Castle Access

1. Find Tarak in the prison cell in Carrena -- he tells you about a Slimebeast
2. Kill the Slimebeast and take the Slimebeast Hammer back to Tarak
3. Drop the hammer on his hex, say: `"tar, hammer"`
4. Tarak kills you and escapes -- you wake at the healer
5. Go to the jail guard outside the jail, say: `"xxx, tarak"`
6. Find Kiara in town, say: `"kia, blessing"` then `"kia, <whatever she said to you>"`
7. Take Mujaba's Dagger to Karazov on the island south of Mujaba's lair
8. Drop the dagger on his hex, say: `"kar, kukri"` -- he gives you a Floating Potion
9. Go to the caves SW of town and look for a clearing with a hex that says "you are beneath a castle"
10. Drink the Floating Potion to float up to Tarak's Castle
11. Kill Tarak
12. **Reward**: Blast Hammer (+4, Level 5 Blast) + Evil Tarak Robe

---

## Tarak Robe Alignment Change

Take Tarak's Robe to Kiara:
- If you are N/N, say: `"kia, bounty"` -- receive the **Neutral Robe**
- If you are G/G, say: `"kia, bounty"` -- receive the **Good Robe**

## Charisma Quest (n/n Quest)

One of the very few ways to fix the Charisma stat after character creation. Located in Aleria. The Neutral quest rewards a Charisma Potion (choose `"lor, fame"`), and the Good quest also offers a Charisma Potion (choose `"tal, fame"`). Nearly impossible for new players to access due to the high-level requirements and Aleria access gating.
