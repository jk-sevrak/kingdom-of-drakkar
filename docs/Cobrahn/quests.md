## Hit Point Quests

Together the Replacement, Ring, Shackle, and Graagh quests grant +400 HP in Cob. The first three (+300 HP) are essential for survival.

### +100 HP: Guard Replacement

The first HP quest new Cobrahn characters should complete. Requires help from other players but is fairly easy.

1. Climb to Tree Town, find Drizzle
2. Say: `"dri, replacement"` -- Drizzle gives you a name
3. Climb down, go to the Guard House (north of the tree climb up)
4. Say to the NPC: `"xxx, replacement is <name>"` (using the name Drizzle gave you)
5. **Reward**: +100 HP in Cobrahn

### +100 HP: Juntes' Brother (Ring Quest)

Second HP quest to complete. Also requires help but fairly easy.

1. Find Ionas in Desert Town and talk to him -- he gives you a ring
2. Travel SW into the swamp, find Juntes
3. Click Juntes while holding the ring in your right hand -- he gives you a ring to take back
4. Return to Ionas with the exchanged ring
5. **Reward**: +100 HP in Cobrahn

### +100 HP: Kaldor Shackle

1. Kill Kaldor and take his Key
2. Open Kaldor's Jail Cell (east of the lair)
3. Click the prisoner inside -- he gives you Kaldor Shackles
4. **Tip**: Stockpile extra shackles as the HP Doc asks for them
5. Take shackles to Drizzle in Tree Town, click him
6. **Reward**: +100 HP in Cobrahn

### +100 HP: Graagh's Halberd

1. Kill Graagh on the castle top floor for a halberd
2. Climb down the well, go east to Duke Moreal
3. Take Graagh's Halberd to Duke Moreal in the well in Graagh's Castle
4. **Reward**: +100 HP in Cobrahn

## Minor HP & Recovery Quests

### Boone Quest

Talk to Boone upon arriving in Cobrahn. **Reward**: +6 HP.

### Steve Quest (Timmy Town)

When you die, Steve sends you to kill a monster and bring it back. **Reward**: +2 HP recovery.

## Equipment Quests

### Infravision Helm (Maggio Quest)

1. Find Maggio in Timmy Town and click him to get a name to kill
2. Search for that name in Desert Town and make sure he dies (drag a zoo mob to kill the named crit -- you cannot kill it directly)
3. Search the corpse for Maggio's Ring
4. Take the ring to Maggio and trade it to him
5. **Reward**: Infravision Helm

### Anti-Steve Amulet

1. Find a Cthon Egg in the Psi Tower
2. Trade the egg to Murg in Desert Town
3. **Reward**: Anti-Steve Amulet (wearing it prevents the 2 HP death loss from Steve)

### Earthcrush Prot Amulet

1. Take a Gold Brick to an NPC in Desert Town
2. Trade for the amulet
3. **Reward**: Earthcrush Protection Amulet

### Po Earthcrush Prot Bracers

1. Kill Pokick and Pomend and take both of their bracers
2. Trade the bracers to Prisona for a Po Ring
3. Trade the Po Ring to someone for the Po Earthcrush Prot Bracers
4. **Reward**: Po Earthcrush Protection Bracers

### Green Dragon Helm

1. Kill the Green Dragon and tan the corpse for scales
2. Take Green Dragon Scales to the trader behind the Green Dragon lair
3. **Reward**: Green Dragon Helm

### Beetle Fullplate

1. Obtain Beetle Scales (from the Beetle lair)
2. Take Beetle Scales to an NPC in Timmy Town, click to trade
3. **Reward**: Beetle Fullplate

### Lucien Gauntlets

1. Obtain a Roc Egg
2. Trade the Roc Egg to Lucien
3. **Reward**: Lucien Gauntlets

### Abner Boots

1. Obtain Silver Dragon Scales
2. Trade Silver Dragon Scales in Tree Town
3. **Reward**: Abner Boots

### Jasper Boots

1. Tan an alligator
2. Trade the tanned alligator to Jasper
3. **Reward**: Jasper Boots

### Titanium Shield

1. Find a Wooden Bolt (drops in the Giant Keep)
2. Take it to the Spectre Dungeon, find Igli
3. Trade the Wooden Bolt for a Spectre Sash
4. Take the Spectre Sash to Sasha in the same dungeon, trade for the Spectre Ring
5. Trade the Spectre Ring for the Moon Shield in the Spectre lair
6. Trade the Moon Shield for the Titanium Shield in UD-2
7. **Reward**: Titanium Shield

### Kite Shield

1. Obtain a tanned DeathSasquatch fur and a Titanium Shield
2. Trade both to the trader above the Titanium Shield Trader in UD-2
3. **Reward**: Kite Shield

### King's Robe

1. Obtain a Graagh Halberd and a Loriapi Robe
2. Take both to the NPC in Conqueror's Club, trade them
3. **Reward**: King's Robe

### Padded Armor

1. Obtain Large Yeti Fur and the armor piece you want to pad
2. Take them to the padding NPC in Ningizidda's lair (locked behind a door)
3. Trade for padded armor (improved version of the original armor)
4. Most armor types can be padded

### Padded Helms

1. Obtain Large Yeti Fur and the helm you want to pad
2. Take them to Merton in Timmy Town
3. Trade for a padded helm (improved version of the original helm)
4. Most helm types can be padded

## Weapon Quests

### Forged Weapons

1. Get Beetle Armor and a Kaldor Dagger, plus a store-bought weapon of your choice
2. Take them to Artifex
3. Hold the Beetle Armor and the Kaldor Dagger, click on the trader
4. Then hold the store-bought weapon and click again
5. **Reward**: Forged Weapon (upgraded version of your store-bought weapon)

### Improva Weapons

1. Gather the 4 elemental scrolls
2. Obtain a Forged Longsword, Shortsword, or Hammer
3. Take them to Improva in UD-1
4. **Reward**: Improva Weapon (further upgraded version)

## Skill Quests

### Energylance

**Requirements**: Mentalist at skill 24 or greater

1. Collect the 4 amulets from the Psi Tower
2. Hold each amulet one at a time as you step around the portal on the top floor of the Tower until you "hear a click"
3. Step on the teleporter -- you will find the Energylance Scaler
4. Trade the robe to an NPC in Timmy Town to be taught Energylance
5. **Reward**: Energylance discipline

## Potions & Consumables

### 3oz Constitution Major (Bob Quest, Repeatable)

1. Talk to Bob in Tree Town -- he gives you a note
2. Carry the note to the person in Timmy Town -- they redirect you to Desert Town
3. The scroll is taken in Desert Town
4. Return to Bob
5. **Reward**: 3 Major Heal Potions
6. Say `"bob, reset"` to repeat the quest

### Shalala Mini-Fate Potion

1. Talk to Huddleston in Timmy Town -- he gives you a shop number
2. Go to Desert Town, find Shalala
3. Say: `"sha, ###"` (replacing ### with the shop number Huddleston gave you)
4. **Reward**: Mini-Fate Potion

### Dion Mini-Fate Potion

1. Find Dion on the world map
2. Give her either a gem with value >30k or a ring with value >6k (all UD-5 gems work)
3. After a few trades, Dion will run to a new location
4. **Reward**: Mini-Fate Potion

### Timmy Atonement

Do a quest involving Timmy in Timmy Town. **Reward**: Atonement.

## Access Quests

### Conqueror's Club Access

Bring the following items to Conqueros above Vanidor's lair:
- Chipuda Staff
- King's Key
- Berovar Greatsword
- GoblinKing Axe
- Mujaba Dagger
- BanditLord Shortsword
- Graagh Halberd
- Loriapi Staff
- Kaldor Mace

**Password**: Zebra!

### Doom Town Access

1. In the Giants Keep, find the four pillars
2. Perform the "Pillar Dance": step on the north hex, west hex, south hex, east hex, then the middle one
3. You should now be able to get to Doom Town

### Lanapi Access

Get a killshot on Lazloth to gain access to Lanapi's lair.

### Lazloth Access

1. Go upstairs in Conqueror's Club
2. Kill Ooga, use the key to unlock the door
3. Place 4 Espear Bows (from killing archers) on the SW tiled hex
4. In the air puzzle, type the steps: `E S E SW SE`
5. You are now at Lazloth's lair

## Utility Quests

### Lair Regen

1. Take a Regen Rock to an NPC in the Conqueror's Club
2. He will regen either Beetle, Graagh, or Loriapi lair

### Unprone Gear

1. Take 2 of the same item to an NPC in the Conqueror's Club
2. He will give you one that does not prone
