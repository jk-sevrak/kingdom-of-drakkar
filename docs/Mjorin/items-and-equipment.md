# Mjorin Items & Equipment

All items in Mjorin must be found or crafted in-world, since players are stripped of equipment upon entry.

## Armor

| Item | Source | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| **Leather Vest with Plates** | 100gp in South Town, or Orc Leaders | Standard armor. |
| **Scale Mail** | Trade a Red Gem | Best armor available in Mjorin (~250gp value). |
| **BD (Black Dragon) Scales** | Kill and tan a Black Dragon | Found on Orc King Castle level 2. Trade for Halberd. |

## Weapons

| Item | Source | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| **Long Sword** | 10gp purchase or shield trade | Basic weapon. |
| **Spear** | Found in Prisons | Uses short sword skill. Sells for 2gp. |
| **Halberd** | Trade BD Scales | Best weapon in Mjorin. Prones enemies. |

## Rings & Accessories

| Item | Source | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| **Orc Leader Ring** | Orc King Castle level 2 | Random drop from Orc Leaders. |
| **Denaba Ring** | Denaba Caves | Random drop from Denabas. |

## Gems & Trade Items

| Item | Source | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| **Purple Gem** | Ground in Denaba Caves, dropped by Giants | Trade for Red Gem. |
| **Red Gem** | Trade Purple Gem | Trade for Scale Mail. |
| **White Pearl** | Sharks | Trade for Major Con. |
| **Shield** | Prisons (dropped by guards/prisoners) | Trade 10 for Long Swords. |

## Potions & Consumables

| Item | Cost | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| **5gp Healing Pot** | 5gp (South Town) | Heals 25 HP. |
| **15gp Healing Pot** | 15gp (South Town) | Heals 40-60 HP. |
| **25gp Healing Pot** | 25gp (South Town) | Heals 70-90 HP. |
| **50gp Poison Pot** | 50gp (South Town) | Cures poison. |
| **Major Con** | White Pearl trade | Raises Constitution, heals to max HP. |
| **Mjorin Luck Pots** | Rare | One of the very few ways to boost the Luck stat. Nearly impossible for new players to access due to the equipment-stripping entry mechanic. |
