# The World of Mjorin

Mjorin (sometimes spelled "Majorin") is a challenge world in Drakkar with unique access requirements that strip all equipment on entry.

## How to Access Mjorin

1. Go up the **Funhouse** in Nork Town
2. At the top, take the steps to a portal room
3. Take the **lower portal** (the upper portal returns to Nork)
4. **WARNING: When stepping into the lower portal, you will be STRIPPED of all equipment!**

This stripping mechanic makes Mjorin a unique challenge — you enter with nothing and must survive with whatever you find inside.

## Major Locations

### South Town
The main trading hub upon arrival. Sells healing potions and armor (leather vest with plates for 100gp). Primary resupply point.

### North Town
Source of gear and gold. Set up healer recall here for a safe respawn point. Items sell for 1gp (most items) or 2gp (spears).

### Prisons
Early-game resource farming area. Key strategy:
- Prisoners fight guards — let them weaken each other
- Pick up shields dropped in combat
- Trade 10 shields for Long Swords (10gp each) for starting gold
- Spears found here use short sword skill

### Denaba Caves
Contains purple gems on the ground. Creatures (Denabas) fight each other, so wait for weakened targets. Denabas deal 30-50 damage plus poison (18k XP each).

### Orc King Castle
Multi-level dungeon:
- **Level 1**: Orcs, gnolls, orc leaders
- **Level 2**: Black Dragon scales found here (trade for a Halberd). Orc leader rings drop here.
- **Orc King**: Boss creature (40k XP)

## Item Trading Chains

Mjorin has a gem/item trading economy since you arrive with nothing:

1. **Purple Gems** → found on ground in Denaba Caves, dropped by giants
2. **Purple Gem** → trade for **Red Gem**
3. **Red Gem** → trade for **Scale Mail** (250gp value, best armor available)
4. **White Pearls** → from sharks → trade for **Major Con** (raises Constitution, heals to max)
5. **BD (Black Dragon) Scales** → kill and tan a Black Dragon on Orc King Castle level 2 → trade for **Halberd** (prones)

## The Princess Anne Quest (Luck Pot)

**Requirements**: Level 13+, MA skill recommended

**Key Details**:
- Players are stripped of all psionic disciplines and teleport locations on entry (no lockers available)
- Must progress through a key system: RED gem key, GREEN gem key, platinum key (some single-use, some reusable)

**Bosses**:
- **Orc King**: 5-minute respawn, drops plate armor/spear/coins
- **Gnoll King**: 4-5 minute respawn, drops platinum key, respirate kabuto, leather boots
- **Black Dragon**: Drops scales and black skeleton key

**NPC Trading Chains**: Swampbats → Denabas → Makers/Crestrion (rings for gems, gems for keys)

**Orc Archers Gauntlet**: 15 minutes each solo, arranged in pairs, respawn hourly, positioned behind counters (ranged/psi only)

**Reward**: Luck Potion (+2 permanent Luck), 5M coins paid training, alignment atonement

**Sub-areas**: South Town, North Town, Prison, Bubbler Caves, Orc Castle, Pumphouse, Orc Archers gauntlet

**Unsolved Mystery**: Splunky Arrow NPC — nobody discovered what it trades for

**Notes**: Water damage during traversal; some doors lock/unlock on weekly cycles

## Key Tips

- Set up healer recall in North Town immediately
- Farm Prisons first: collect shields, trade for Long Swords, sell for gold
- Buy healing pots from South Town before venturing out
- Denabas fight each other — wait for weakened targets
- The Black Dragon is extremely dangerous (80-333 claw damage) — only attempt with a group
- **Luck Pots** grant +2 permanent Luck, making Mjorin essential despite the stripping mechanic
