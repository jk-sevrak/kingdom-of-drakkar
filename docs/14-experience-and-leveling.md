## Key Stat Thresholds

- **Constitution 15+**: Must maintain at all times to avoid HP/stat loss on death
- **Constitution 18**: Needed when resting for maximum HP gain per level
- **Strength 18**: Required for Fighters to maximize damage
- **Intelligence 19**: Needed for Mentalists to maximize EP gain per level
- **Wisdom 19**: Needed for Healers to maximize EP gain per level
- **Charisma**: Higher = cheaper training and Hit Doctor costs (each point above 18 saves 5% on Hit Doc)

## EP Potions

- Cost: **500,000 gold** each at the Funhouse
- Give **1-6 EP** per bottle (average slightly below 3)
- EP max in Nork: **250**

## Yellow Potions (HP)

- Yellow pots give HP
- Usable up to **150 HP** (after which you need the Hit Doctor)

## Level 13: The Critical Milestone

Level 13 is a crucial milestone for all characters:
1. **Hit Doctor access** unlocks - can now purchase HP up to class maximum
2. **Stop resting** at level 13 to accumulate un-rested levels
3. **Max HP first** via Hit Doctor before resting
4. This strategy allows gaining HP beyond the Hit Doctor cap through normal leveling when you finally rest

## Training

Two types of training exist:
1. **Skill training** - Practice combat + pay trainer to improve weapon/class skill
2. **Class training** - Occurs at advancement intervals (when you meet level/skill thresholds for new abilities)

Higher Charisma reduces training costs. The **chit trainer** in the Guild Hall offers an alternative training method with no hard skill cap.

## Cobrahn Stat Traders

In Cobrahn, stats can be raised to **21** (beyond Nork's 18-19 cap) by trading specific items:

| Stat | Trade Item | Trader | Max |
|------|-----------|--------|-----|
| **Strength** | Silver nugget | Arminius | 21 |
| **Agility** | Brass ring | Conrado | 21 |
| **Constitution** | Thin emerald bottles | (common drops) | 18 |
| **Wisdom** | Sapphire | Trader west of Desert Town | 21 |
| **Intelligence** | Turnip | NW caves east side | 21 |
| **Willpower** | Packet of mandrake | Trader east of big river | 21 |
| **EP** | Grub | Trader | +1 EP per grub |

Grubs come from Cthons and Centipedes. Silver nuggets are in upper Timmy Caves. Brass rings are very rare (from Ninjas). Mandrake found under Juntes/forest area.

## Dion Mini-Fate Pots

**Dion** is a wandering NPC in Cobrahn who trades rings (6050+ GP value) or gems (40K+ value) for mini-fate potions. These have a ~75-90% chance of positive effects including random stat changes, temporary overmax HP, random training, and various buffs.

## Macros

The game supported macro commands:
- **ARG command**: `ARG <repeat>` - for repeating actions
- Macros could be set up for common actions like attacking, focusing, and berserking
