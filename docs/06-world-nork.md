# The World of Nork

Nork is the starting world of Kingdom of Drakkar, where all new characters begin their adventure. It's the most thoroughly documented world across fan sites.

## Major Locations

### The City of Nork
The main starting settlement. New players begin here. Contains:
- Shops and vendors for buying/selling gear
- Trainers for all classes (new characters arrive as Merchants and must find a trainer to dedicate to a profession)
- The **Steel Flower Inn/Pub** - social gathering area with personal lockers for item storage (first right inside)
- Scroll vendors selling Succor scrolls, Respirate scrolls, and discipline-imbued scrolls
- Dungeons beneath the city, at least **10 levels deep** (the 9th and 10th levels contain "formidable foes")

### The Town of Maeling
A town in the Nork world area. Maeling -3 (M-3) is noted as a good area for Mentalist Illusion spell use.

### Volcano Town (VT)
Located south of the main area. Notable for:
- **Hit Doctor** - Located just south of Volcano Town. Available at level 13. Allows characters to purchase additional HP up to their class maximum. Costs vary by Charisma (15-30+ million gold).
- VT Griffin - a dangerous creature. Being eaten by VT Griffin could cause loss of ALL purchased hit points.

### Frore
An icy/frozen area within the Nork world. Contains:
- Multiple lairs (see below)
- The **Deplete trainer** for Mentalists (skill 17) - accessed via a hole by Frore's north gate, through water paths. Bring a teleport twig - there's no way out without one!

### Rat Burrow
Contains a **Strength Fountain** that provides a strength boost when drunk from. Requires a Rat Burrow sash to enter. Used by players before resting to maximize HP gains per level. (There are rumors the fountain causes faster aging.)

### Evil Nork (EN)
A dangerous version of the Nork area. Contains lair-type creatures that roam freely:
- Big crossbow guys
- Auto strippers
- These lair-type creatures can take permanent hit points on death

### Funhouse
Sells **EP pots** for 500,000 gold each (give 1-5 EP, average ~3).

### Fishery
A location where:
- Fishery Bows are obtained (needed for Muzi quest)
- Fishery Staff available as starting weapon for casters

### Guild Hall
Contains:
- Chit trainer (alternative training method with no hard skill cap)
- Account lockers for safely transferring gear between characters on the same account

## Nork Dungeon Levels

The Nork dungeon has multiple levels of increasing difficulty:
- **Nork -1 (N-1)** through **Nork -7 (N-7)** - Standard dungeon levels
- **Nork -3.5** - Contains the ghost that drops the Door Pearl (needed for Muzi)
- **M-1, M-2** - Maeling levels (M-2 has the key for Muzi quest, traded for a flawless black pearl)
- **KM-2** - Where Peewee is located (Muzi quest destination)
- **-5, -6, -7** - Heavy assault areas (need Rak cloak)

## Nork Lairs

Lairs are instanced boss dungeons with unique challenges. Lair creatures can permanently take hit points on death. Known Nork/Frore lairs:

| Lair | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **Rat Lair** | Beginner dungeon |
| **Bat Lair** | Early-game lair |
| **Spider Lair** | Early-game lair |
| **Skeleton Lair** | Contains undead |
| **Beetle Lair** | Giant beetles |
| **Ogre Lair** | Mid-level |
| **Boris Lair** | Major dungeon, mid-level |
| **Tarak Lair** | Contains boss Tarak |
| **Breshard Lair** | Named boss |
| **Vanidor Lair** | Requires a party for safe completion |
| **Mamard Lair** | Higher difficulty, fire protection needed for Mama hunt |
| **Phantom Lair** | Undead/phantoms, may need special items |
| **Liches** | End-game Nork content. 5 liches guard Papa Lich who carries the Uzi amulet. Requires psi-cutting gear. |
| **Snowbeast (SoB)** | Quest-oriented boss. Requires specific P-weapons (Saber, Vamps, Chipper, Stiletto, P-LS) to kill. |
| **Warden Lair** | Very dangerous. Players avoided it because skill loss on death couldn't be regained. Brad planned to add "nifty new items" to Wardens. |
| **Lori** | "Insanely tough" boss who drops the Lori Staff (+6 skill, +6 EP regen). Dies "all the time" with the right group. Hard hunt requiring many players. |

## Nork Economy

- **Gold** is the primary currency
- Training costs escalate dramatically at higher skill levels
- Hit Doctor costs 15-30+ million depending on Charisma
- EP pots: 500K each from the funhouse
- Transmute twigs found on KM -4 & -5 (for non-ment coiners)
- Players could max out bank accounts relatively quickly with Mentalist popping
- Transfer of gold between Nork and Cob was deliberately restricted by Brad to prevent "polluting" Nork's economy

## Nork Gear Progression

### Starting Gear (obtainable with help)
- Red Dragon Scales (armor)
- Big Yeti, Kitty (fur armor pieces)
- Rak Cloak
- Blue Shield
- Longsword or Fishery Staff

### Mid-Level Nork
- HP Helm (appraise 8K, found on N-2+)
- Ratburrow Sash
- Combat rings (defensive adds preferred for casters)
- Red Dragon Gauntlets
- Muzi amulet (+2 EP regen)

### End-Game Nork
- Mama Scales
- Uzi amulet (+5 EP regen)
- Chipper Staff (Mentalist) or Slith Staff (Healer)
- Vamps (best gauntlets)
- Attack Sash (high-level content)

## Nork Tips

- Items found on the floor can be tried on in safe areas - if a helm's description says it's "glowing," it's an HP or EP helm
- Appraise items at vendors to learn their value before selling
- Ask (don't beg!) experienced players for help getting basic gear
- Party hunting gives significantly better skill gains than solo
- Don't level past 13 until HP is maxed at the Hit Doctor
- The Gambler (casino) existed but Brad didn't want to put more effort into it
