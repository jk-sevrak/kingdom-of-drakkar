# Kingdom of Drakkar: Overview

Kingdom of Drakkar (KoD) is a graphical MMORPG that has been running since **1989**, making it one of the longest-running online multiplayer RPGs in history. It bills itself as "the #1 online multiplayer role-playing game since 1989."

## What Is It?

Drakkar is a **top-down view graphical role-playing game** -- a persistent "living world" where players build characters by gaining experience, improving skills through training, and venturing further in search of greater adventures. It emerged during the transition era from text-based MUDs to graphical MMORPGs, and was originally hosted on **MPG-Net (Multi-Player Games Network)**.

## The Drakkar (Lore)

The game's namesake, "the Drakkar," is the **Dark Queen** -- a legendary evil entity whose power looms over the entire game world. The evil emanating from Cobrahn "even rivaled the power of the legendary Dark Queen, the Drakkar." She corrupted the Four Champions of the Norkken Guard, turning former protectors of the city of Frore into its tormentors.

## Platforms

- **Windows** (primary client - "DrakkarZone" launcher/lobby application)
- **Android** (APK client released later - described as having "a lot of rough edges, but its great for 'killin crits sitting in a coffee shop!'")
- **Pocket Drakkar** - A dedicated hardware project (pocketdrakkar.com) with prototype "Pocket Founder" units shipped as of July 2019

## Pricing Model

- **Nork World One**: Completely free
- **Drakkar Silver / Drakkar Gold**: Paid plans unlocking additional scenarios
- **Guild Hall**: $4.50/month (Basic: trainer to skill 20, 3 lockers, portals, minor CON pot/day, item tier, cheaper stores) or $7.50/month (Deluxe: trainer to skill 35, 20% discount, additional features)
- The game originally charged **$4/hour** to play; monthly bills of $100-$300 were common in the early days
- Special events occasionally opened all content for free (e.g., Christmas 2016: "TONIGHT/TOMORROW ONLY... *EVERYONE IS A PAYER*")

## History & Context

- **1989**: Game launched, originally on MPG-Net
- **~2001**: Migrated to DrakkarZone (DZ), an independent hosting platform
- **2002**: Developer Q&A; website created; plans for "Grimoire" scenario
- **2014**: Featured on Massively.com; Sysop Journal started
- **2015**: "Challenge Nameless" mode introduced
- **2016**: Android client released; tutorial video contest; Christmas free-play event
- **2019**: Pocket Drakkar units shipped; new data center migration; "Uber Aleria" events; early game improvements

Drakkar was contemporary with the "golden age" of MMORPGs (EverQuest, Dark Age of Camelot, etc.) and had players who moved between these games. The Lords of Entropy guild, for example, played both Drakkar and DAoC simultaneously.

## Game Worlds

The game features multiple distinct worlds ("scenarios") with increasing difficulty:

1. **Nork** - The starting world where all players begin. Contains the City of Nork, the Frore ice area, the town of Maeling, Homlet, Carrena, Volcano Town, and dungeons at least 10 levels deep.
2. **Cobrahn** (Cob) - An advanced world accessed through a portal deep in Nork's dungeons. Contains Tree Town, Timmy Town, Doom Town, Desert Town, Seldar, and more challenging content.
3. **Aleria** - Another advanced world reached via Urmak the Boatman. Features a war between Duke Talinar's Forces of Light and Lord Koss's Minions of Darkness.
4. **Mjorin** (Land of Mjorin) - An additional exploration area, less commonly documented. Source of rare Luck Pots.

There were also "alt" worlds (alternate versions of the main game segments) and "Uber" variants (e.g., "Uber Aleria").

## Core Gameplay Loop

1. Create a character (choose gender, race, class, allocate stats)
2. New characters arrive as **Merchants** and must find a trainer to dedicate to a class
3. Explore dungeons and wilderness areas
4. Fight creatures for experience, skill, and loot
5. Buy/sell/appraise items at NPC vendors
6. Train at trainers to improve skills
7. Max out health and energy points via Hit Doctor and EP pots
8. Form parties for group content
9. Complete quests for rewards and access to new areas
10. Progress to harder worlds (Nork -> Cobrahn/Aleria)

## Key Game Philosophy

From the developer Q&A:

- **Skill over level**: "Do not fall for the trap of concentrating on your level! Skill is what matters in Drakkar. Level is secondary."
- **Group-oriented**: "This is a group oriented gaming, and the sum of a group is more than the sum of its parts."
- **1% per hour minimum**: Brad targeted a minimum of 1% skill gain per hour at higher levels with efficient hunting.
- **No 'winning'**: "There is no 'winning' the game in the traditional sense." Players set their own goals.
- **PKing forbidden**: "Killing player characters is strictly forbidden. PKing is not allowed."

## Fan Community

The Drakkar community was remarkably active in creating fan sites across every major web hosting platform of the late 1990s/early 2000s:
- GeoCities, Angelfire, Tripod, 8m.com, GuildHappy, EarthLink
- Dedicated domains (drakkarmoy.com, lords-of-entropy.de)
- Fan sites served as the unofficial wikis before game wikis became standard
- Player-created databases documented items, creatures, maps, and strategies
- Official Facebook page at facebook.com/KingdomOfDrakkar
- Forums at drakkarzone.com/forums

The game had a truly international player base, with a significant German-speaking European community (the Lords of Entropy guild had its own .de domain and German-language CMS).
