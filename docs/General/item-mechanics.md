# Item Mechanics

## Equipment Slots

Characters can equip items in the following slots:
- **Body Armor** (Scales/Plate)
- **Cloak** (Back slot)
- **Helm** (Head)
- **Gauntlets** (Hands)
- **Boots** (Feet)
- **Bracers** (Wrists)
- **Sash** (Waist)
- **Amulet** (Neck)
- **Rings** (Ring slots)
- **Shield** (Off-hand)
- **Weapon** (Main hand)

## Tying

Some items "tie" to the character who equips them, meaning they can only be used by that character. Examples: Curvy Longsword, boots, sashes, helms, rings.

## Enchanting

Mentalists can enchant items to increase their combat bonus:
- Up to +3 at skill 12
- Up to +5 at skill 17 (costs 8 permanent HP per plus above +4)
- Up to +6 at skill 19 (costs 15 permanent HP per plus above +4)
- Up to +9 at skill 24 (costs 15 permanent HP per plus above +4)

**Full enchanting cost (+0 to +9)**: ~134 permanent HP, ~150M experience, ~28 Constitution, plus aging effects.

## Imbuing

Mentalists can recharge magical items or imbue new disciplines:
- At skill 17: imbue wooden twigs (bought from Healer Trainer shop)
- At skill 18: imbue gold bracers
- Costs 5 permanent HP per imbue (6 HP at skill 20+)
- Every 50 imbues: lose 3-5 Constitution
- Items may explode if you attempt imbuing before skill 18!
- Do not wear HP adds when casting Imbue

## Sensing

Mentalists can sense item properties:
- At skill 3: identify disciplines and energy in items
- At skill 14: sense amulets, bracers, and succor twig destinations
- At skill 15: sense leather rings (stat rings show "Physical Properties")

## Padding

Armor can be padded with animal furs for additional combat adds:
- **Bear fur**: +3 padding adds
- **Yeti fur**: +4 padding adds
- Some items can be padded multiple times (e.g., Junte's Robe can be padded twice)

## Appraising

Items can be appraised at vendors to determine their value. HP helms appraise for 8K.
