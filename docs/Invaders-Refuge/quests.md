# Invaders Refuge Quests

## Iron Bread Armor Upgrade

Upgrade your armor's AC by using Iron Breads at anvils found throughout the IR dungeon. Each anvil adds +2% to the item's base AC value.

### Requirements
- Iron Bread (dropped by Chefs and Bakers in the IR dungeon)
- An upgradeable item (armor, robe, helm, boots, bracers, or Santa Gauntlets)
- Access to anvils in the IR dungeon (anvils range from level 1 to level 26)

### Steps

1. Kill **Chefs** and **Bakers** in the IR dungeon to collect **Iron Bread**
2. Locate an **anvil** in the dungeon (they are found in many locations at various levels from 1 to 26)
3. Hold the **item you want to upgrade** in your **right hand**
4. Hold the **Iron Bread** in your **left hand**
5. **Click the anvil** (walk onto the hex) to apply the upgrade
6. The anvil will increase the item's base AC value by **+2%**
7. Repeat at different anvils -- each anvil can only be used **once per item**

### Eligible Item Types
- Armors
- Robes
- Helms
- Boots
- Bracers
- Santa Gauntlets

### Important Notes
- Each anvil level (1 through 26) can only be applied once per item
- This upgrade stacks with other upgrade systems (silks and Nork goblets)
- **Recommended upgrade order**: To maximize your armor's AC, apply upgrades in this order:
  1. First: Nork goblet upgrades
  2. Second: Silk upgrades
  3. Third: Anvil (Iron Bread) upgrades

## Undead Silk Armor Upgrade

Upgrade your armor's AC by collecting Undead Silks (Spectre Silks) from enemies in the IR dungeon. Each silk adds +5 AC to your armor. There are 15 total silks to collect.

### Requirements
- Access to the IR dungeon
- Ability to kill and tan enemies wearing green silks
- Access to the NPC in the southwest IR caves

### Steps

1. Explore the IR dungeon and look for enemies that appear to be **wearing a green silk**
2. **Slay** the silk-wearing enemy and **tan** the corpse in town to obtain an **Undead Silk**
3. Each silk has a **random number** assigned to it (1 through 15), based on the level of the area where it was found
4. Bring the silk to the **NPC in the southwest IR caves** to:
   - Check the number of the silk
   - Apply it to your armor
5. Each silk number can only be applied to your armor **once**
6. Each silk adds **+5 AC** to your armor
7. Collect and apply all 15 silks for a total of **+75 AC**

## Major's Sash Quest

Obtain the Major's Sash by completing a deer meat gathering and cooking quest for the Mayor of Invader's Refuge.

### Reward
- **Major's Sash** -- 5/5, Advanced psionic Damage Deflate I (Level 90 req). A beautifully woven sash depicting a tree in spring-time.

### Requirements
- Ability to kill Deer outside IR town
- Access to a brewing station
- Stans (sold nearby in IR town)

### Steps

1. Kill and tan **Deer** outside of the IR town until you have collected **40 Raw Deer Meats**
2. **Brew** 2 Raw Deer Meats with 1 Stans (sold nearby) to produce **1 Cooked Deer Meat**
   - Alternatively, you can purchase Cooked Deer Meat in town for **50 platinum each**
3. Repeat until you have **20 Cooked Deer Meats**
4. Turn in all 20 Cooked Deer Meats to the **Hunter** NPC to receive a **Deer Meat Feast** (gift)
   - The Deer Meat Feast has Advanced psionic Feast of Health
5. Bring the **Deer Meat Feast** to the **Mayor** of Invader's Refuge
6. **Receive the Major's Sash**

### Summary

| Step | Action | Result |
|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Kill + tan 40 Deer | 40 Raw Deer Meats |
| 2 | Brew 2 Raw Meats + 1 Stans (x20) | 20 Cooked Deer Meats |
| 3 | Give 20 Cooked Meats to Hunter | 1 Deer Meat Feast |
| 4 | Give Feast to Mayor | Major's Sash |

## Santa Holiday Event

A seasonal event that occurs during the winter holidays. Santa appears as a roaming boss in the IR dungeon.

### How It Works

1. **Santa roams** around in the IR dungeon during the holiday event period
2. Enemies in the IR dungeon will randomly drop **Santa's lost gifts** when slain
3. To weaken Santa, **hold gifts in your hand** -- Santa will randomly take them from you
   - Alternatively, hand gifts to **SantasHelper** in the IR town (less effective)
4. As Santa receives more gifts, he becomes **easier to hit** and deals less damage
5. Initially, Santa hits extremely hard (**20,000+ damage**) and is very difficult to hit
6. Once weakened sufficiently, fight and kill Santa for powerful seasonal drops

### Santa's Abilities
- Attacks with his weapon (bisento or halberd)
- Can **stun** you
- Has a ranged **%HP attack** similar to banshees
- **Warning**: If Santa kills you, he will **strip you** (remove your equipment)
