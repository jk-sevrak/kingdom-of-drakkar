# The World of Invaders Refuge

Invader's Refuge (commonly abbreviated as **IR**) is one of the end-game scenarios in Kingdom of Drakkar. It is a large, dangerous dungeon-based world featuring powerful dragons, undead creatures, and unique upgrade systems for armor. The scenario is built around a sprawling dungeon with enemy levels ranging from 0 to 29, making it a deep and challenging environment for high-level characters.

![Invaders Refuge Map](../images/maps/invaders-refuge/invaders-refuge.png)

## Entry Requirements

- Characters must be high level to survive (enemies scale through level 0-29 zones)
- Contains both a surface town and a large multi-floor dungeon
- Accessible from the main game world (linked alongside Nork, Aleria, Cobrahn, Nameless Lands, GDH/BDC/SDC, and Sullen Keep)

## Major Locations

### IR Town

The main settlement and safe zone within Invader's Refuge. Contains:
- **Shops and vendors** for buying and selling gear, including cooked deer meat (50 platinum each)
- **Tanner** for processing hides and silks
- **The Mayor** -- quest NPC who rewards the Major's Sash upon completing the deer meat feast quest
- **The Hunter** -- quest NPC who accepts cooked deer meats in exchange for a gift (Deer Meat Feast)
- **SantasHelper** -- seasonal NPC who accepts Santa's lost gifts (less effective than handing directly to Santa)
- Brewing stations (stans sold nearby for combining ingredients)
- Deer roam outside the town and can be killed and tanned for Raw Deer Meat (quest material)

### IR Dungeon

The primary adventuring area. A massive multi-level dungeon containing:
- Enemy zones scaling from **level 0 to level 29**
- **Anvils** located throughout the dungeon at various levels (1 through 26), used for the Iron Bread armor upgrade quest
- Various creature types including mummies, banshees, spectres, chefs, bakers, and numerous dragon types
- Enemies that occasionally wear visible **green silks** (Undead Silks / Spectre Silks) used for the armor upgrade quest
- **Brewing herbs and flowers** dropped randomly throughout the dungeon
- **Gems** that sell for 10 platinum coins
- Random drops of protection bottles (fire, ice, acid)

**Tip**: To find roughly the level of the area you are in, subtract 1 from the amount of platinum gained when searching your kills.

### Southwest IR Caves

Contains an NPC used for the Undead Silk armor upgrade quest. Bring silks here to:
- Check the number assigned to each silk
- Apply the silk upgrade to your armor

## Lairs and Dragons

Invader's Refuge is home to several powerful dragon lairs and boss encounters. All IR lair dragons can be killed and tanned for **Dragon Plate** armor.

### Smoke Dragon
- Casts a strong **acid breath**
- Bites for approximately **8,000 average damage** per bite
- Prones frequently

### Dragon (Fire)
- Casts a strong **fire** attack
- Bites for approximately **5,000 average damage** per bite
- Located at coordinates **(275, 107)**

### Sand Dragon
- Casts a strong **lightning** attack
- Bites for approximately **9,000 to 11,000 average damage** per bite
- Located at coordinates **(275, 107)**

### Ice Dragon
- Casts a strong **ice breath**
- Appears in two different areas with varying difficulty:
  - **Level 15 area**: ~8,000 average damage per bite, coordinates **(160, 223)**
  - **Level 23 area**: ~6,000-9,000 average damage per bite, coordinates **(386, 303)**

### SpiderDragon
- Casts a strong **fire breath**
- Attacks with claws

### Santa (Seasonal Holiday Event)

A special seasonal boss encounter that appears during winter holiday events.

- Roams around in the IR dungeon
- Attacks with his weapon (bisento or halberd)
- Can **stun** you
- Has a ranged **%HP attack** similar to banshees
- Hits extremely hard (**20,000+ damage**) and is difficult to hit until you bring him enough of his lost gifts
- Lost gifts are found randomly in the IR dungeon when slaying enemies
- **To weaken Santa**: Hold gifts in hand and Santa will randomly take them. Alternatively, hand gifts to SantasHelper in the IR town (less effective)
- **Warning**: If Santa kills you, he will **strip you** (remove your equipment)
- Drops powerful seasonal equipment (Santa Boots, Santa Gauntlets, Santa MA Gauntlets, Santa Hat, Santa Robe, Santa Bisento, Santa Halberd, Santa Cane Totem, Santa Cookie Totem)

## Tips for Adventuring in IR

- **Armor upgrade order matters**: To maximize AC on your armor, it is recommended to first add the Nork goblet upgrades, then the silk upgrades, and lastly the anvil (Iron Bread) upgrades
- **Bring protection**: Enemies cast fire, ice, acid, and lightning -- protection bottles drop in the dungeon
- **Brewing ingredients** are plentiful throughout the dungeon; collect herbs for crafting potions
- **Platinum is the currency**: Gems sell for 10 platinum, and cooked deer meat costs 50 platinum in shops
- **Dragon lairs** require a well-geared party; dragon bites deal thousands of damage per hit
