# Quests

## The Mausoleum Quest (Unsolved)

One of the oldest and most mysterious quests in Kingdom of Drakkar, **hard-coded into the original game code**. This quest predates Nork-2, N-3.5, Maeling, Volcano Town, Frore, Cobrahn, the Fun House, Mjorin, and all Nork levels from 6 down -- meaning it was part of the very first version of the game.

### The Three Known Mausoleums

Each mausoleum is associated with a classical element:

#### 1. Nork Town Mausoleum (Element: Fire)
- Located in Nork Town
- Flaming braziers indicate the Fire theme
- Pillars cannot be destroyed or illusioned
- Altar cannot be destroyed or illusioned
- **Mind is probed** when entering the center square (only triggered by keyboard movement; mouse movement does NOT trigger it)
- Quitting on center square restores to Nork Town
- Level 1 merchants get teleported away and cannot stand on the center square

#### 2. Beneath Nork Paladin Trainer (Element: Earth)
- Located underground beneath the Paladin trainer
- Pillars turn to water when blown up. Pillars may be illusioned. Passwall works on pillars.
- Altar may be illusioned but not blasted. Passwall does NOT work on the altar.
- Mind is probed on center square (keyboard only, not mouse)
- **Any level character** (even merchant level 1) can stand on the center square
- Different pillar graphics from the other mausoleums
- Center square is "open ground." Outer pillars stand on "gravel road."
- Jumping into holes leads to a small cave, part of which is directly under the center square

#### 3. Cloud Giant Castle Mausoleum (Element: Air)
- Located in a castle in the clouds
- Pillars cannot be destroyed or illusioned
- Altar cannot be destroyed or illusioned
- **Cannot step on center square** -- teleports you 2 squares south

### The 4th Mausoleum (Undiscovered)
Location unknown. Believed to have **Water** as its element, likely located "somewhere watery."

### Common Observations
- All three mausoleums have an **Altar** 1 square north of center. Altars are "counters" (items can be placed on them).
- Each altar has a runner with tan background/red outline and a **cup**. The Nork altar has an upright cup; the other two have a tipped cup with spilling liquid.
- Cups appear encrusted with red and green gems
- Altars have **two snakes facing each other**; one appears to stick its tongue out
- The mind probing on 2 of 3 center squares suggests something specific is being checked for (perhaps a particular item, stat, or alignment?)
- The quest remains one of Drakkar's enduring mysteries

## Hit Point Quests (Cobrahn)

These quests grant bonus HP that only apply while in Cobrahn:

### Replacement Quest
- +100 HP in Cobrahn
- One of the first things new Cob characters should complete
- Requires help from other players but is fairly easy

**Steps**:
1. Climb to Tree Town, find Drizzle
2. Say: `"Dri, replacement"` -- Drizzle gives you a name
3. Climb down, find the named crit in the shack by the tree stump
4. Say: `"Name, replacement"` (using the name Drizzle gave you)
5. **Reward**: +100 HP in Cobrahn

### Juntes Ring Quest
- +100 HP in Cobrahn
- Second HP quest to complete
- Also requires help but fairly easy

**Steps**:
1. Talk to Ionas in Desert Town to receive a Ring
2. Travel SW to Juntes, exchange rings with the NPC there
3. Return to Ionas with the exchanged ring
4. **Reward**: +100 HP in Cobrahn

### Shackle Quest (3rd Cobrahn HP Quest)
1. Kill Kaldor and take his Key
2. Open the closet east of Kaldor's Lair
3. Get shackles from the prisoner inside
4. Take shackles to Drizzle in Tree Town
5. **Reward**: +100 HP in Cobrahn

Together the Replacement, Ring, and Shackle quests grant +300 HP in Cob, which is essential for survival.

## Muzi (Mini Uzi) Quest

**Reward**: Muzi amulet (+2 EP regen per round)

**Requirements**:
1. **Door Pearl** - Obtained from the ghost on N-3.5
2. **Two Fishery Bows** - From the Fishery area
3. **Key from M-2** - Obtained by trading a Flawless Black Pearl for it on Maeling level 2

**Steps**:
1. Gather all three items
2. Travel to **Peewee** on KM-2
3. This is the hard part - requires someone to dex you to Peewee, or a Mass Teleport Mentalist

## Uzi Quest

**Reward**: Uzi amulet (+5 EP regen per round) - the best caster amulet in the game

**Requirements**:
- A party of several "sticks" (fighters)
- A Healer
- A Mentalist with Infravision

**Steps**:
1. Go SE of Frore, jump to the NE side of the hole
2. Find the door in the NW corner, go 6 hexes east, then SW
3. Kill **6 Liches sequentially** -- each Lich drops an amulet that is needed to access the next
4. Kill **Papa Lich** to obtain the Uzi amulet
5. Requires psi-cutting gear for the lich fights

## Sash Quest (Ratburrow)

Involves items from the N-3.5 area:
- Spider Queen egg (from Spider Queen Lair)
- Sanquin egg
- Rewards the **Ratburrow Sash** (combat adds + CritCure ability + Ratburrow teleport access)

## Snowbeast Quest

The **Snowbeast (SoB)** is a quest-oriented boss that can only be killed with specific Power Weapons:
- Saber
- Vamps (gauntlets)
- Chipper Staff
- Stiletto
- Power Longsword (P-LS)

Brad considered this a "quest-oriented kill" and preferred focusing on new content rather than adding more weapons for this 10-year-old encounter.

## Alerian Charisma Quest (n/n Quest)

One of the very few ways to fix the Charisma stat after character creation. Located in Aleria. Nearly impossible for new players to access.

## Nork Quests

### Goblin Lord Quest
1. Find the silver key on N-1, open the locked door in SE corner of N-1
2. Kill the Goblin Lord, take the key and Purple Gem
3. Open the door with the key
4. Say to Pelagus: `"Pelagus,proof"` -- he gives you a ring
5. Go to Genetta, say: `"Genetta,pelagus"` -- she gives you an amulet
6. Go to Hector, drop the ring and amulet on him, say: `"Hector,heirloom"`
7. **Reward**: 1/1 ring with Level 2 Fire/Ice Protect

### Lizard King Quest
1. Find Sir Halden, say: `"sir,oath"`
2. Find Lady Annalie, say: `"ann,wards"`
3. Go to the SW corner of N-2, use the teleport into the Lizard King's lair
4. Kill the Lizard King, take the Doll (also drops +1 mace)
5. Return to Annalie, say: `"ann,doll"`
6. **Reward**: +1 CON up to 18 (one-time only)

### Ratburrow Sash Quest
1. Talk to Mungho, say: `"Mun,rat"` -- he asks you to kill a rat
2. Kill a rat, give it to Mungho
3. Talk to Dagano the Mayor, say: `"dag,mission"` -- he gives you a collection mission
4. Collect from N-3.5 creatures:
   - Spider Queen egg
   - Sanquin egg
   - Dark Guard axe
   - Minotaur axe
5. Drop all items on Dagano, then tell him about each:
   - `"dag,heathen"` (Dark Guard axe)
   - `"dag,spider"` (Spider Queen egg)
   - `"dag,sanquin"` (Sanquin egg)
   - `"dag,bull"` (Minotaur axe)
6. **Reward**: 2/2 Ratburrow Sash + CritCure + Ratburrow shortcut + STR fountain access

### Chipper Key (Billy & Moose Quest)
1. Talk to Billy in Frore, get fetching stick
2. Lead Moose back to Billy
3. **Reward**: Key to Chipuda lair (ties)

### Power Gear Quest
1. Trade SnowBeast skull to King in Frore for Power House Key

### Poison Bow Quest

### Mummy King Quest (N-3)

### Griffon Quest

### Betty Quest
1. Get the Doll from Betty's father near Frore
2. Go to the Frore Minotaur/Vampire building, blow the west wall open
3. Hold the Doll in your hand, lead Betty home

   - Clear the entire path of crits before leading her
   - A Mentalist can wall off crits to protect her along the route
4. **Reward**: Atone + random treasure

## SDC & End-Game Progression

### Talon/Nugget Progression
End-game content requires collecting progression items:
- Kill thumpers for **fragments**
- Collect **talons** to unlock level 78 access
- Collect **64 gold nuggets** (= 1 large nugget) to reach level 85
- This gates access to the **Silver Dragon Consort (SDC)** area

### +12 Killer Weapon Quest
Craft an enhanced killer weapon through the SMC progression chain. Produces the strongest craftable weapons in the game.

### Mormar Entry Quest
Required for floor-by-floor progression through the 25-level Mormar dungeon in the Nameless Lands. Culminates in reaching the Rift Guardian.

## Aleria Quests

### Good Aleria Quest (requires Good/Good alignment)

**Requirements**: G/G alignment + tanned Werewolf fur + BL Sword + Seldari Queen Cup + Mujaba's Key + Goblin King Axe

**Full NPC Dialog Chain**:
1. Go to Caspian, say: `"Cas,Kassel"` -- drop BL Sword on him -- say: `"Cas,evidence"`
2. Go to Lord Talinar, say: `"Talinar,quest"` -- then back to Caspian: `"Cas,promotion"`
3. Go to Lord Talinar, say: `"Tal,fur"` -- drop WW Fur at Arogar -- say: `"Aro,fur"`
4. Return to Lord Talinar, say: `"Tal,fur"` -- then: `"Tal,solution"` -- then: `"Tal,coins"`
5. Go to Caspian, say: `"Cas,promotion"` -- then go to Lady Ela: `"Lad,hello"`
6. Go to King Borav, say in sequence: `"Kin,alliance"` -> `"Kin,goblin"` -> `"Kin,treaty"` -> `"Kin,deal"` -> `"Kin,axe"`
7. Go to Lady Ela, say in sequence: `"Lad,liadaril"` -> `"Lad,secret"` -> `"Lad,seldari"` -> `"Lad,allies"`
8. Kill the Seldari Queen, hold the Goblet
9. Return to Caspian, say: `"Cas,dub"` -- this makes you **Champion of Carrena**
10. Go to Lord Talinar, say: `"Tal,quest"` -- use Mujaba's Key, kill Lord Koss
11. **Reward**: Good Alerian Helm (Feather Fall lv15, +1 Willpower, dances when stunned) + Morningstar + enchantment for Tarak access

### Evil Aleria Quest (Evil Duke Quest, requires Evil/Evil alignment)

**Requirements**: E/E alignment + BL Sword + SQ Goblet + WW Fur

**NPC Dialog Chain**:
1. Visit Culcula and begin the evil quest chain
2. Proceed through Evil Tower NPCs: Hester -> Koron -> Lord Koss -> Lady Ela -> Arogar
3. Kill Talinar
4. **Reward**: Duke Shield (+30 HP, glowing, good aligned) -- can be traded for Evil Infra Helm (limited infravision, 1x1 area, -1 luck)

### Neutral Aleria Quest (requires Neutral/Neutral alignment)

**Requirements**: N/N alignment, dedication to Loranon

**Steps**:
1. Become N/N alignment, dedicate yourself to Loranon
2. Kill the Bandit Lord, give sword to Caspian
3. Kill a Werewolf, give fur to Arogar
4. Talk to Talinar, trade the GK Axe for the Light Ring
5. Give the Light Ring to Lady Ela
6. Kill the Seldari Queen, give the cup to Lady Ela
7. Give the Pink Leaf to Culcula and Eldoral
8. Return to Loranon and choose your reward:
   - **Fame**: CHA Potion
   - **Fortune**: Staff (+5 combat, +1 EP regen, +1 WIS)

### Tarak's Hammer Quest
1. Kill the Slimebeast, take the hammer to Tarak in Carrena jail
2. Tarak escapes and kills you -- you wake at the healer
3. Kill Mujaba, take the dagger
4. Trade the dagger to Karzov for a Float Bottle on Monkey Island
5. Drink the Float Bottle in the whirlpool shade, float up to Tarak's Castle
6. Kill Tarak
7. **Reward**: Blast Hammer (+4, Level 5 Blast) + Evil Tarak Robe (trade robe to Kiara)

## Maeling Quests

### Lightning Hammer Quest
1. Obtain a BNR (Black Ninja Robe) -- hold it in your hand
2. Go to the middle of Maeling Town, trade the BNR
3. **Reward**: Lightning Hammer (+4 Off, Level 5 Lightning)

### Basilisk Quest
1. Go to lower Volcano Town, find Kuron
2. Say: `"Kur,secret"` -- Kuron gives you an enchantment and a clue to kill a specific monster
   - Clue examples: "Forces to ground" = **Beshita**, "Frogfaced one" = **Bullywog**
3. Find the clue monster in Maeling Forest or Badlands, kill it, bring it back to Kuron
4. Kill the Basilisk, tan the corpse for scales
5. Trade the scales to Bavic
6. **Reward**: Basilisk Shield

## Mjorin Quests

### Mjorin Princess Quest
1. Buy the North Town key
2. Kill the Orc King, obtain the Gnoll key
3. Kill the Gnoll King, trade armors for further keys
4. Rescue Princess Anne from the Gnoll King dungeon -2
5. Open the Portal to the King's Throne Room
6. **Reward**: Atone + +1 Luck

## Additional Cobrahn Quests

### Guard Duty Replacement (1st HP Quest - Easy)
1. Climb to Tree Town, find Drizzle
2. Say: `"Dri, replacement"` -- Drizzle gives you a name
3. Climb down, find the named crit in the shack by the tree stump
4. Say: `"Name, replacement"` (using the name Drizzle gave you)
5. **Reward**: +100 HP in Cob

### Graagh Halberd (3rd HP Quest)
1. Kill Graagh on the castle top floor for a halberd
2. Climb down the well, go east to Duke Moreal
3. Trade the halberd to Duke Moreal
4. **Reward**: +100 HP in Cob

### Bob Quest (Repeatable)
1. Talk to Bob in Tree Town, receive a note
2. Carry the note to the person in Timmy Town -- they redirect you to Desert Town
3. The scroll is taken in Desert Town
4. Return to Bob
5. **Reward**: 3 Major Heal Potions
6. Say `"bob,reset"` to repeat the quest

### Boone Quest
Talk to Boone upon arriving in Cobrahn. **Reward**: +6 HP.

### Steve Quest (Timmy Town)
When you die, Steve sends you to kill a monster and bring it back. **Reward**: +2 HP recovery.

### Maggio HP Helm Quest
1. Click on Maggio's heads, read the crit name that appears
2. Go to Desert Town, find the named crit
3. Drag a zoo mob to kill the named crit (you cannot kill it directly)
4. Search the corpse for a ring
5. Return to Maggio, click his heads while holding the ring
6. Trade the Black Pearl for an Infravision Helm in Timmy Cave
7. **Reward**: Infravision Helm

### Anti-Steve Amulet Quest
1. Talk to Murg in Desert Town
2. Kill Cthon in the Psi Tower, take the egg
3. Trade the egg to Murg
4. **Reward**: Anti-Steve Amulet (prevents the 2 HP death loss from Steve)

### Shalala Mini-Fate Quest
1. Talk to Huddleston in Timmy Town -- he gives you a shop number
2. Go to Desert Town, find Shalala
3. Say: `"sha,#"` (replacing # with the shop number Huddleston gave you)

## Seasonal Events

### Valentine's Day Event
- Receive a potion from Valentina, trade with another player to change its properties
- +1 CHR possible from the potion
- Click on Valentina's heads for a gem -- trade the gem for Vampire Gauntlets

### Halloween Event
1. Visit the Costumist, say: `"Cos,basket"` (need 1 GP in inventory, right hand must be empty)
2. Receive a Pumpkin Basket
3. Buy costumes: Creature costumes 50 GP, Lair costumes 100 GP
4. Trick-or-treat the following NPCs: Talbot, Annalie, Sir Halden, Guard at N-3.5, Welby, Moonshadow, Sal, Wartberg, Hector, the Crier
5. **Candy effects**: +1 Stat (up to 19), +50 HP overmax, +50 EP overmax, +14K-19K XP
