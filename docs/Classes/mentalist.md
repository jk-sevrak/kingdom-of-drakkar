# Mentalist Class Guide

*"So you think you've got what it takes to be a Mentalist in Drakkar?"*

The Mentalist is one of the most challenging classes to develop. Ments have the lowest hit points of all classes, offset by some of the most powerful and formidable spells in the game. If you don't have the patience to think your way through a fight, make a fighter class.

## Building a Mentalist

### Key Stats
- **Intelligence** is the primary stat - determines EP and spell power
- **Charisma** and **Luck** 16+ (hardest to fix)
- All other stats fixable with pots

### Skilling Strategy

**The Skill vs. Level Trap:**
Mentalists have powerful area-effect spells that kill many creatures at once, rapidly gaining experience but minimal skill. This leads to unbalanced characters (e.g., level 18 / skill 13). The key: balance area-effect spells with single-target spells.

- **Good skill spells** (single-target): Enmiss, Espear - use these for kills
- **Bad skill spells** (area-effect): Fireball, Icestorm, Firebreath - use to soften creatures, then finish with single-target
- Technique: Cast area spell to lower creature HP, pick off individuals with Enmiss/Espear, repeat
- Advanced technique: Let creatures onto your hex before area spells to pile loot for easy Transmute

**Primary skilling spells**: Enmiss (Nork), Espear (Cob)

### Coining (Making Gold)

Mentalists are the **best coining class** thanks to Transmute (skill 10):
- Transmute converts floor items to gold
- Value scales with skill: 10K at skill 10, 50K at skill 14, 80K at skill 18
- Build large piles (up to 255 items) then pop for hundreds of thousands of gold
- Warning: Too-large piles may cause node-outs (disconnects)
- Ments are coveted in non-lair parties for their ability to pop junk piles

### Gearing Up

Identical base progression to Healers with key differences:
- Switch from Red Dragon Scales to **Mama Scales** when your own fire/ice spells start damaging you
- **Do NOT wear Full Plate (FP)** - it halves psi power!
- **Chipper Staff** (Nork) - Spells act 2 skill levels higher. Rare and hard to get.
- Ments get **only 1 physical attack per round EVER** - so offensive ring adds are less valuable. Prioritize defensive rings.
- Click on the mana bar to see numeric EP display with regen rate

**Key Mentalist Gear:**
- **Tarak Robe** - +1 EP regen (requires good alignment)
- **BNR** - Helps avoid attacks
- **SNR** - Fire/ice protection
- **Slith Cloak** - +15 EP bonus
- **Chipper Staff** - Spells act 2 skill levels higher
- **Dancers Boots** - Save if stunned
- **Lizard Boots** - 50 fire protection
- **Snow Boots** - 50 ice protection
- **Muzi** - +2 EP regen/turn
- **Uzi** - +5 EP regen/turn

### Maxxing

- **HP Max**: 350 (Hit Doc, level 13+)
- Same strategy as Healer: Don't rest past 13 until HP maxed
- Rest with **Intelligence rings** equipped (not Wisdom - that's for Healers)
- EP pots from funhouse: 500K gold each, 1-5 EP per pot

### Party Role

- Cast protection spells (ProtFire, ProtIce, EnergyShield, Absorption) on party members
- Cast Infravision on party (25 EP per person - need Uzi for large parties)
- Kill ranged attackers (bowmen, bullywogs) with targeted spells
- Use Illusions to block creature paths
- Use FindFriendly for an extra combatant
- Transmute loot piles for the party's gold
- Enchant and Imbue items for party members (at significant personal cost at high levels)
