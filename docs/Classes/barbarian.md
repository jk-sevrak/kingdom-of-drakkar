# Barbarian Class Guide

The Barbarian is the easiest to play, fastest to develop, and has the highest HP cap.

- **Max HP**: 580 (via Hit Doctor) - highest in the game. 600 with Mino Blood, 623 in Cobrahn.
- **Key Stats**: Strength, Constitution (STR gives Barbarians HP bonuses)
- **Special Abilities**:
  - **Zerk (Berserk)** - 4 berserk levels from "Agitated" to "Frothing at the Mouth." Grants extra attacks ("fury rounds") and more strength, but character loses control and may attack party members, pick up items from ground, or run off screen. Hold gauntlets in right hand to prevent accidental friendly attacks.
  - **Autobalm** - auto-heal when HP drops below 30% while berserked
  - **Level 16**: +4 STR while berserked
  - **Level 18**: +50% HP while berserked
  - **Level 20**: +4 extra attacks at full berserk
  - **Level 21**: Stun immunity at full berserk, +1 extra attack
  - **Level 25**: Fury Rounds (4x swings)
  - **Level 51**: Cleave ability
- **HP**: Max 580 HP (Hit Doctor), 600 with Mino Blood, 623 in Cobrahn
- **Zerking tip**: Continuously zerking can leave "holes" in attack. Each rage increase = not hitting that round.
- Can heal with both hands full via **Autobalm**
- Can break psionically imbued items for XP
- **Best with halberds** (polearms)

### Zerk Stage Progression

Zerk stages: No Zerk -> Fidgety -> Anxious -> Wild -> Crazed -> Out of Control -> Frothing

| CT Level | Talent | Per-Stage Breakdown |
|----------|--------|-------------------|
| 16 | +4 STR | +1 at Fidgety, +1 at Wild, +1 at Crazed, +1 at Frothing |
| 18 | +50% HP | +10% at Fidgety, Anxious, Wild, Crazed, Out of Control |
| 19 | Immune to Scare | At Frothing only |
| 20 | +4 Attacks | +1 at Anxious, +1 at Wild, +1 at Crazed, +1 at Out of Control |
| 21 | Immune to Stun Discipline | All zerk levels |
| 22 | +4 AGI | +1 at Wild, +1 at Crazed, +1 at Out of Control, +1 at Frothing |
| 23 | Immune to Mindwipe. Immune to Scare at any zerk level | -- |
| 24 | +2 Attacks | +1 at Out of Control, +1 at Frothing |
| 25 | 4 swings possible on Out of Control round. Heal on successful hit while Frothing | -- |
| 26 | Immune to ALL combat stuns while Frothing | -- |
- **Armor**: Limited options. Distrust psionics, resistant to psionic attacks, have difficulty with psionically imbued items (Succor scrolls). In Cobrahn, **DeathSasquatch Fur** (+200 HP, 0/7 combat adds, Barb only) is the prize gear. **Grraaagh Halberd** (+5 STR, on-contact Push, proning, Barb only) is the top weapon.

## Barbarian Issues (from 2002 Q&A)

- **Party unfriendly**: Zerking causes Barbarians to grab items from ground and run off screen, disrupting party hunting
- **Haste removed from Zerked state**: Unlike Paladins (MaxStrike + Haste) and MAs (RP + Haste), Barbarians couldn't be Hasted while Zerked. Brad was "seriously considering" restoring this
- **Mid-level gap**: High-level Barbs were fine, but mid-level Barbs got "overly pounded" and had nowhere to hunt effectively
- **Pooling**: MAs could pool off Cob quest HPs, but Barbs couldn't. Brad said he'd "see what all it would affect/ruin"
