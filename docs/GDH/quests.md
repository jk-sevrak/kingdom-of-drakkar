# GDH Quests

## Progression Quests

These quests must be completed in order to advance through the areas of GDH. They are sequential -- each one unlocks access to the next area.

### Progression 1 (GDH1 Area 1 to Area 2)

**Objective**: Deliver a Hatchling Scale to the Botanist.

1. Travel to the southwest portion of the first GDH area
2. Kill a Hatchling and tan its corpse to obtain a **Hatchling Scale**
3. Bring the Hatchling Scale to the **Botanist** NPC in the first area of GDH
4. **Result**: You can now progress to the second area of GDH1

### Progression 2 (GDH1 Area 2 to Area 3)

**Objective**: Feed the Lunches by trading Mercenary Twigs to them.

1. Head to the Escapee Caves, located in the middle-west area of GDH
2. Find **LucidEscapee** and speak to him. Say: `Lucid, Help`
3. Kill Mercenaries in the area until you have collected **2 Mercenary Twigs** (you will need 10 total twigs traded)
4. Type `trade enable` in command mode
5. Trade the twigs to the **Lunches** that wander the Escapee area (drag the twig to the Lunch's character box; the twig must be in your hand)
6. After trading 10 twigs total, return to **LucidEscapee** and click him to complete the process
7. **Result**: You can now progress to the third area of GDH1

### Progression 3 (GDH1 Area 3 to Area 4)

**Objective**: Defeat Medusa and deliver her gem to the Servant.

1. Kill **Stone Golems** in the area until you obtain a **Golem Essence** (a glowing brown stone)
2. Kill the **Beholder** lair boss to obtain a **Beholder Staff**
3. Hold both the Beholder Staff and the Golem Essence in your hands
4. Right-click the Golem Essence to activate the Medusa fight
5. Gather a party and kill **Medusa** to obtain her **Medusa Gem** (a tiny pulsating green gem)
   - Alternatively, you can find the gem rarely from Remains enemies in the area
   - Tip: You can wall Medusa in using Illusion to help manage the surrounding enemies
6. Take the Medusa Gem to the **Servant** NPC in the SwampWitch/Golem area
7. **Result**: You can now progress to the fourth area of GDH1

### Progression 4 (GDH1 to GDH2)

**Objective**: Obtain the Climbing Twig from FoodGuardian and use it to climb to GDH2.

1. Gather a party and kill the **FoodGuardian** lair boss (located in the far southwest corner of GDH1)
   - Warning: FoodGuardian can hit up to 8 times per round (4 strikes max per target), sucks EXP and Skill on hit, and reflects melee damage
2. Collect the **Climbing Twig** from FoodGuardian's drops (an extremely strong wooden twig entwined with white silk)
3. Hold the Climbing Twig in your hand
4. Go to the south-west-most hex of the swampwitch side of the water and climb up
5. Once you have climbed up, you can safely discard the twig (or save it for another class -- each class needs their own twig)
6. **Result**: You are now in GDH2

## Equipment Quests

### Combat Earring

**Reward**: Combat Earring (+1/+1)

1. Kill a **Hatchling** in GDH1
2. Tan the corpse and pick up the **Hatchling Scales**
3. Find **Barnlot** in the third area of GDH (near Medusa)
4. Trade the Hatchling Scales to Barnlot
5. **Reward**: Combat Earring -- a small gold earring with a crossed sword and shield near the tip

### Gatherer Robe

**Reward**: Gatherer Robe (+6/+6, Damage Reflect)

1. Kill **Gatherers** in GDH1 until one drops a **Gatherer Feather**
2. Take the Gatherer Feather to the **Farmer** NPC at the entrance of GDH
3. Click the Farmer to trade the feather for the robe
4. **Reward**: Gatherer Robe -- a large cape made of smoldering thorns

### Needler Ring

**Reward**: Needler Ring (+7/+7, +4 All Stats)

1. Kill **Needlers** in the second area of GDH until you obtain a **Needler Heart**
2. Bring the Needler Heart to **Sturderon** in the second area of GDH
3. **Reward**: Needler Ring

### Vine Sash

**Reward**: Vine Sash (+4/+4)

1. Kill red **Militia** enemies in GDH until you loot **Canteens**
2. Collect **20 Canteens** total
3. Go to **Captain** and say: `capt, help` to turn in the Canteens
4. **Reward**: Vine Sash -- a very narrow vine stretching almost three feet in length

### Vine Shield

**Reward**: Vine Shield (a strange shield made from interwoven living vines)

1. Visit the **Official** NPC in GDH1
2. He will ask you to save 10 LostFarmers
3. Find **LostFarmers** wandering the area
4. Say `los, follow` to get them to follow you
5. Lead each LostFarmer from the Official to the boat near Captain
6. Repeat until you have saved all **10 LostFarmers**
7. Return to **Official** to receive your shield
8. **Reward**: Vine Shield

## GDH2 Quests

### Castle Access

**Objective**: Obtain keys to enter the Castle in GDH2.

1. Kill NPCs near the castle to find a **Castle Key** (opens the first door)
2. Kill **Merc Captains** (lair bosses in the castle area) to find **2 more keys**
3. Use the keys to unlock successive doors and gain full access to the castle interior
4. **Result**: Full access to the Castle, including Wakka and the kitchen

### Barbarian Fields Access

**Objective**: Gain access to the Barbarian Fields in GDH2.

1. Speak to **Chef** located in GDH2. He will ask you to protect him while he cooks
2. Protect **Chef** for **80 rounds** (he will thank you when done)
3. Kill Mercs in the Castle and collect **10 Infected Apples**
4. Give the 10 Infected Apples to the **Cook** in the Castle kitchen
5. Head over to **Danfur** near the locked door to the Barbarian Fields
6. Talk to Danfur after completing the above tasks
7. **Reward**: Danfur gives you a **Barbarian Fields Key** to open the doors

### Improved Thermature Ring

**Reward**: Improved Thermature Ring (+9/+9, +5 All Stats)

1. Kill the **Thermature** lair boss in GDH2 (Dragon River) to obtain a **Thermature Ring**
2. Collect **10 Bear Meat** from killing bears/Cave Bear in the Barbarian Fields
3. Take the 10 Bear Meat to **Smokie** in the northeast of the Barbarian Fields
4. Trade the 10 Bear Meat to Smokie to receive **Smoked Bear Meat**
5. Take both the **Thermature Ring** and the **Smoked Bear Meat** to **Krog** in GDH2
6. Click Krog with both items in hand
7. **Reward**: Improved Thermature Ring

### Marton Robe

**Reward**: Marton Robe (+5/+0, +1 All Stats, Lightning Prot, Acid Prot, Psi-Cutter)

1. Obtain **1 Milky Gem** through brewing
2. Obtain **1 Red Robe** through brewing
3. Trade both items to **Marton** in GDH2
4. **Reward**: Marton Robe -- a large cape made of a highly cohesive form of blue flame
5. See the [Brewing Guide](../General/brewing-system.md) for details on brewing the Milky Gem and Red Robe

### Basic Bow

**Reward**: Basic Bow (vine bow)

**Note**: Requires a brewing skill of 11 to obtain the Pure Water prerequisite.

1. Brew a **Pure Water** (requires brewing skill 11)
2. Bring the Pure Water to **Gipler** in GDH2
3. Trade the Pure Water to Gipler
4. **Reward**: Basic Bow (vine bow)

### Advanced Bow (Improved Vine Bow)

**Reward**: Advanced Bow (improved vine bow)

**Note**: Requires brewing skill of 11.

1. First obtain the **Basic Bow** (see above)
2. Kill **Mature Green Dragons** in GDH2 until you collect **3 Green Dragon Tongues**
3. Brew the 3 Green Dragon Tongues together with **1 Acid Pot** to obtain an **Infected Tongue** (blue/grey tongue)
4. Brew the Infected Tongue together with the Basic Bow
5. **Reward**: Advanced Bow (improved vine bow)

### Basic Glaive

**Reward**: Basic Glaive

**Note**: Requires a brewing skill of 11 to obtain the Pure Water prerequisite.

1. Brew a **Pure Water** (requires brewing skill 11)
2. Bring the Pure Water to **Armon** in GDH2
3. Trade the Pure Water to Armon
4. **Reward**: Basic Glaive

### Advanced Glaive (Improved Glaive)

**Reward**: Advanced Glaive (+9/+9, Psi Cutter, Returning, Fire/Ice Prot)

**Note**: Requires brewing skill of 11.

1. First obtain the **Basic Glaive** (see above)
2. Kill **Youth Green Dragons** in the GDH2 area until you obtain a vial of **Green Dragon Blood**
3. Brew the Green Dragon Blood with **3 Acid Pots** to make a **Mixed Blood Vial** (red vial)
4. Brew the Mixed Blood Vial together with the Basic Glaive
5. **Reward**: Advanced Glaive (improved glaive)

## Quirk Quests

### Wackaflexes

**Reward**: Wackaflexes quirk (up to 5 points)

1. Head to the **Castle** in GDH2
2. Find a key nearby to unlock the door leading to **Wakka** (a steel key with an imperfection near the end)
3. Open the locked door and find Wakka
4. Say: `wakka, begin` to start the encounter
5. Fight and kill all of the **moles** that spawn
6. When you succeed, click Wakka again
7. **Reward**: You gain 1 point in the Wackaflexes quirk
8. Repeat up to **5 times** to max out Wackaflexes (there is a small delay between attempts)

### Bear None

**Reward**: Bear None quirk (up to 5 points)

This is a multi-step quest involving brewing, the Cave Bear lair, and the Trapper NPC.

1. Collect **10 Bear Meat** from killing bears in the Barbarian Fields
2. Bring the 10 Bear Meat to **Smokie** in the Barbarian Fields (northeast). He gives you **Smoked Bear Meat**
3. Brew the Smoked Bear Meat with other reagents to make **Bear Beer** (requires brewing skill 13-15)
4. Activate the **Cave Bear** lair in GDH2 and kill the Cave Bear to get **Climbing Hooks**
5. Use the Climbing Hooks to climb up to the **Trapper** from the south side of the Barbarian Fields
   - The climb-up point is at the southern area of the fields
   - You can also climb back down from the Trapper's location
6. Give the Trapper a **Bear Beer** to start the encounter (similar to the Wakka mole encounter)
7. Make sure you kill **all of the bears** that spawn
8. **Reward**: You gain 1 point in the Bear None quirk
9. Repeat up to **5 times** to max out Bear None
