# Skaars Kingdom Quests

Skaars Kingdom (Sullen Keep) features a linear progression system that unlocks higher level caps and access to deeper areas of the keep. There is also a repeatable armor crafting quest for each tier.

## Progression Quests

SK progression is gated through a series of quests that must be completed in order. Each progression unlocks new areas and raises your level and/or skill cap.

---

### Progression 1 -- SK Cave Access

**Prerequisite**: Level 85, access to SK (via SDC quest + stairs near Fire Town)

**Reward**: Access to SK Cave, level cap raised to 87, skill cap raised to 42

**Steps**:
1. Enter SK through the stairs near Fire Town in SDC (requires level 85)
2. You will arrive in **SK Forest** (Area 1)
3. Kill **Hunters** in the forest area -- they are in temporal flux
4. Hunters will drop an **AGI Pot** (Progression Pot)
5. The pot requires **level 85** to drink
6. Drink the pot -- you should receive the message: **"Your lungs feel stronger"**
7. You now have access to SK Cave, your level cap is raised to 87, and your skill cap is raised to 42

**Notes**:
- The Hunters are the primary enemy in the forest area
- The AGI pot drop is random, so you may need to kill many Hunters before one drops
- Make sure you are level 85 before attempting to drink the pot

---

### Progression 2 -- Climb to the Keep

**Prerequisite**: Progression 1 complete, level 86

**Reward**: Access to SK Silver (the keep's Silver floor)

**Steps**:
1. Enter **SK Cave** (unlocked from Progression 1)
2. Kill creatures throughout the cave
3. Collect a **Climbing Book** (dropped by cave creatures)
4. Collect **10 Climbing Pages** (also dropped by cave creatures)
5. **Fill the Climbing Book** with all 10 pages
6. Reach **level 86** (you must be at least level 86 or "you're not powerful enough")
7. Find the **puddle** in the cave (located in the southern portion of the cave map)
8. **Climb up** on the puddle with your completed Climbing Book
9. You will emerge in the keep's Silver area

**Notes**:
- Both the book and pages are random drops from cave creatures, so expect to spend some time farming
- You cannot climb up until you have both the completed book AND are level 86
- The puddle is the only way up from the cave to the keep

---

### Progression 3 -- Level Cap to 88

**Prerequisite**: Progression 2 complete (climbed up from cave)

**Reward**: Level cap raised to 88

**Steps**:
1. After climbing up from the cave (Progression 2), you will be in SK Silver
2. Find and **use the stairs** that connect the cave to SK Silver
3. Travel between the cave and SK Silver using these stairs
4. Simply using the stairs flags your progression to level 88

**Notes**:
- This can be done at any time after completing Progression 2
- There is no specific kill requirement -- just use the stairs
- The stairs to the Barracks area are located on the Keep Base (Keep +0) map

---

### Progression 4 -- Level Cap to 90

**Prerequisite**: Progression 3 complete, level 88

**Reward**: Level cap raised to 90

**Steps**:
1. Reach **level 88**
2. Find and **take the stairs to the Black area** of SK
3. The stairs to the Black level are located on the Keep +1 (Gold Level) map
4. Using the stairs at level 88 flags your progression to level 90

**Notes**:
- You must be level 88 before the stairs will advance your progression
- The Black area (Keep +0) is significantly harder than Silver and Gold

---

### Progression 5 -- Level Cap to 95

**Prerequisite**: Progression 4 complete, level 90

**Reward**: Level cap raised to 95

**Steps**:
1. Reach **level 90**
2. Find and **take the stairs to the Green area** of SK
3. The stairs to the Green level are located on the Keep +1 (Gold Level) map
4. Using the stairs at level 90 flags your progression to level 95

**Notes**:
- You must be level 90 before the stairs will advance your progression
- The Green area (Keep +2) contains the Green Captain, the toughest lair boss in SK
- Level 95 is the maximum level cap in SK

## SK Armor Quest

**Prerequisite**: Access to the relevant floor tier (Silver, Gold, Black, or Green)

**Reward**: A tier-appropriate Fieldplate (Barbarian/Martialist) or Boneplate (all other classes)

This is a repeatable crafting quest that produces the signature armor of Skaars Kingdom. Each tier of armor requires pages collected from enemies and a mold purchased from an NPC.

### Steps

1. **Collect 10 armor pages** of the desired type and tier:
   - Choose **Field pages** if you are a Barbarian or Martialist (for Fieldplates)
   - Choose **Bone pages** if you are any other class (for Boneplates)
   - Pages come in four tiers: Silver, Gold, Black, and Green

2. **Page drop locations**:
   - **Silver Pages** -- Drop infrequently from Silver enemies, somewhat frequently from Gold enemies
   - **Gold Pages** -- Drop infrequently from Gold enemies, somewhat frequently from Black enemies
   - **Black Pages** -- Drop infrequently from Black enemies, somewhat frequently from Green enemies
   - **Green Pages** -- Drop infrequently from Green enemies, somewhat frequently from Parapet enemies

3. Once you have 10 pages, travel to **Keep +0 (Base floor)**

4. Find **Armornon** on the base floor and **buy a mold** corresponding to your desired armor type

5. Take the filled pages and the mold to the **Forger** NPC (located nearby on the same floor)

6. **Turn in** the pages and mold to the Forger to receive your completed armor

### Armor Tiers

| Tier | Fieldplate (Barb/MA) | Boneplate (Others) | Level Req |
|------|---------------------|-------------------|-----------|
| Silver | 15/15, +10 stats, 500 Absorb | 20/20, +10 stats, 1000 Absorb | 87 |
| Gold | 20/20, +12 stats, 700 Absorb | 25/25, +12 stats, 1200 Absorb | 89 |
| Black | 25/25, +14 stats, 900 Absorb | 30/30, +14 stats, 1400 Absorb | 92 |
| Green | 30/30, +16 stats, 1100 Absorb | 35/35, +16 stats, 1600 Absorb | 95 |

All armor is Glowing and provides bonuses to AGI, INT, STR, and WIS.

### Tips

- Farm pages on the floor **above** your target tier for the best drop rates (e.g., farm Gold enemies for Silver pages)
- If you need Green pages, Parapet enemies have the best drop rate for them
- You can craft multiple sets of armor as you progress through the tiers
- Boneplates have higher Off/Def stats and more Damage Absorption than Fieldplates of the same tier
