# Skaars Kingdom Items & Equipment

All items in Skaars Kingdom (Sullen Keep) organized by equipment slot. SK gear represents some of the highest-tier equipment in Kingdom of Drakkar, with level requirements ranging from 85 to 95.

## Armor

SK armor is crafted through the SK Armor quest (see Quests page). There are two types: **Fieldplates** for Barbarian/Martialist, and **Boneplates** for all other classes (Fighter/Mentalist, Healer, Mentalist, Paladin, Thief). Each tier requires collecting 10 armor pages and purchasing a mold from Armornon.
