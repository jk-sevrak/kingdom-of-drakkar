# The World of Skaars Kingdom

Skaars Kingdom (SK), also known as **Sullen Keep**, is a high-level scenario in Kingdom of Drakkar. It is considered an extension to the Nameless Lands (NL) and shares the same "alts" (alternate characters) with it. SK is a multi-tiered keep with progressively harder floors, designed for characters level 85 and above. The scenario features color-coded difficulty tiers, a unique armor crafting system, and some of the most powerful gear in the game.

## Entry Requirements

- **Level 85** minimum to enter
- You must first complete a quest in **Silver Dragon Consort (SDC)** to gain access
- Enter by going up stairs near **Fire Town** in SDC at level 85
- SK shares the same alt restrictions as Nameless Lands

## Major Locations

### SK Forest

The initial outdoor area upon entering Skaars Kingdom. This area contains **Hunters** -- enemies in temporal flux that drop the AGI Progression Pot required for the first progression quest. The forest serves as the gateway to the SK Cave.

### SK Cave

An underground cave system accessed from the forest. Key features:
- **Creatures** drop the Climbing Book and Climbing Pages needed for Progression 2
- **Basilisk Lair** -- Located in the southeast corner of the cave. The Basilisk stuns and breaks bottles. Drops Stat Pots and Basilisk Gripclaws.
- **Shady NPC** -- Located in the cave, sells **anti-prone potions** for 3 million gold
- **Climb-Up Point** -- A puddle in the cave that allows you to climb up to the keep once you have a completed Climbing Book and are level 86+

### Sullen Keep Base (Keep +0 / Black Level)

The ground floor of the keep itself. Contains:
- **Armornon** -- An NPC vendor who sells armor molds for the SK Armor quest
- **Forger** -- An NPC nearby who forges your completed armor pages + mold into finished armor
- **Black Captain Lair** -- The lair boss of the Black floor. Drops Stat Pots and Black Captain Gripclaws.
- **Stairs to Barracks** -- Used for Progression 3, connecting the cave to SK Silver

### Sullen Keep +1 (Gold Level)

The first upper floor of the keep. Contains:
- **Gold Captain Lair** -- The lair boss of the Gold floor. Drops Stat Pots and Gold Captain Boots.
- **Stairs to Black Level** -- Used for Progression 4 (requires level 88)
- **Stairs to Green Level** -- Used for Progression 5 (requires level 90)

### Sullen Keep +2 (Green Level)

The second upper floor and one of the most dangerous areas in SK. Contains:
- **Green Captain Lair** -- The toughest lair boss in SK. Drops any 2 rares and a Stat Pot. The Green Captain is described as a "very tough fight" that "hurts." Drops include: Attack Amulet, Stolen Skill Amulet, Battle Cleric Amulet, Nightwalker Amulet, Sullen Bracers (all variants), Sullen Insight I and II Earrings, Sullen Sash, and Spartan Helm.
- **Stairs to Gold Level**
- **Stairs to Parapet**

### Sullen Keep +3 (Parapet)

The uppermost level of Sullen Keep. Contains:
- The highest-tier enemies in SK
- **Invader Scrolls** drop here, providing access to the Invader's Refuge scenario
- **Stairs back to Green Level**
- Green and Parapet armor pages drop from Parapet enemies

## Difficulty Tiers

Skaars Kingdom uses a color-coded tier system for its floors, with each tier representing progressively harder enemies and better loot:

| Tier | Area | Level Range | Notes |
|------|------|-------------|-------|
| **Forest** | SK Forest | 85 | Entry area, Hunters drop Progression Pot |
| **Cave** | SK Cave | 85-86 | Basilisk lair, Climbing Book/Pages |
| **Silver** | SK Silver/Barracks | 86-87 | First keep tier after climbing up |
| **Gold** | Keep +1 | 87-89 | Gold Captain lair |
| **Black** | Keep +0 | 88-92 | Black Captain lair |
| **Green** | Keep +2 | 90-95 | Green Captain lair, best rare drops |
| **Parapet** | Keep +3 | 90-95 | Highest tier enemies, Invader Scrolls |

## Lairs

### Basilisk
- **Location**: SK Cave (southeast corner)
- **Abilities**: Stuns. Breaks bottles.
- **Drops**: Stat Pot, Basilisk Gripclaws
- **Notes**: Bring stun protection if possible. Be cautious about carrying potions in bottles since the Basilisk can destroy them.

### Gold Captain
- **Location**: Gold Floor (Keep +1)
- **Drops**: Stat Pot, Gold Captain Boots
- **Notes**: Standard captain lair fight

### Black Captain
- **Location**: Black Floor (Keep +0)
- **Drops**: Stat Pot, Black Captain Gripclaws
- **Notes**: Standard captain lair fight

### Green Captain
- **Location**: Green Floor (Keep +2)
- **Drops**: Stat Pot, plus any 2 of the following rares:
  - Attack Amulet
  - Stolen Skill Amulet
  - Battle Cleric Amulet
  - Nightwalker Amulet
  - Sullen Bracers (Armor)
  - Sullen Bracers (Regeneration)
  - Sullen Bracers (Strike)
  - Sullen Insight I Earring
  - Sullen Insight II Earring
  - Sullen Sash
  - Spartan Helm
- **Notes**: Very tough fight. Hurts. This is the premier lair in SK and the source of the best rare equipment in the scenario.

## Level and Skill Progression

SK features a unique progression system where characters must complete quests to unlock higher level caps:

| Progression | Requirement | Reward |
|-------------|-------------|--------|
| Progression 1 | Drink AGI Pot from Forest Hunters at level 85 | Access to SK Cave, level cap raised to 87, skill cap raised to 42 |
| Progression 2 | Fill Climbing Book with 10 pages, climb up at level 86 | Access to SK Silver |
| Progression 3 | Use stairs between Cave and SK Silver | Level cap raised to 88 |
| Progression 4 | Take stairs to Black area at level 88 | Level cap raised to 90 |
| Progression 5 | Take stairs to Green area at level 90 | Level cap raised to 95 |

## Connection to Other Scenarios

- **Silver Dragon Consort (SDC)**: SK is accessed from SDC via stairs near Fire Town
- **Nameless Lands (NL)**: SK shares the same alt restrictions as NL and is considered an extension of it
- **Invader's Refuge (IR)**: Invader Scrolls drop from Parapet enemies in SK, granting access to the IR scenario

## Free Account Guide (by Skaar)

Skaar, a well-known player, authored a comprehensive guide for free account players. While the full guide covers the entire free-to-play progression from Nork through Cobrahn and beyond, the key points relevant to SK-level play include:

- **Multiboxing**: Running at least 2 accounts is almost a requirement due to the small player base
- **Barbarians** are the easiest and most effective free class to grow -- best damage, self-healing, highest HP
- **Class Tier List for Free Accounts**:
  - **A Tier**: Barbarian -- best damage, easiest to gear, self-healing, highest HP
  - **B Tier**: Mentalist (MT taxi, ES/ABS disciplines, glass cannon damage), Thief (stealth killing, lock picking, gear recovery)
  - **C Tier**: Healer (support/CC), Martial Artist (rivals Barb damage at skill 30 with good gear, pool chi abilities)
  - **D Tier**: Paladin (outclassed by Barb/MA), Fighter/Mentalist (weakest free class)
- **Race**: Outcast is recommended for almost all free characters (19 STR / 18 AGI natural -- best to-hit and to-miss bonuses)
- **HP Maxing**: The Bero sword grind in Cobrahn Hold is the most time-consuming but important step for free characters (100-150 swords needed, 2-hour respawn timer, expect 1-2 months)
- **Stat Maxing**: Cobrahn item trades raise stat caps to 21; Dion raises them to 23
