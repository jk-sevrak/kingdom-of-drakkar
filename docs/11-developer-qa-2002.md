# Developer Q&A Session - April 10, 2002

This is a summary of the complete developer/player Q&A session held on April 10, 2002, transcribed by Morgar and Alyx of the Merchants of Yore (MoY) guild. Brad (the developer) answered player questions about game mechanics, upcoming content, and balance issues.

## Brad's Opening Statement

> "My view of drakkar now is a product i want to expand out into the future, so i'm currently looking for ways to 'blow the top' off the game, giving me open level and skill expandability. This includes larger maps, new scenarios, more experience and skill levels, and more items which can affect more of your character. (and of course nasty creatures guarding them)"
>
> "I'm here to answer your questions, and get feedback from you, as much as you are here to ask me questions."

## Key Announcements & Confirmations

### Grimoire (New Scenario)
- Expected to release in 2002
- Geared for characters experience level 23-25+
- 35% chance the skill cap would be lifted in Grimoire
- "There is a lot of coding to be done for skills above 30"

### Skill System
- Target: **1% per hour minimum skill gain** at higher levels with efficient hunting
- Solo players at skill 20 were getting ~3% per hour; partied players much higher
- "Some say they cant get 1%, others say they are getting 3" - significant variance
- Paying skill "works great unless you're fighting near the gain cap, then it kind of flattens out"
- PSI skill in Cobrahn needed to be brought "up to par with weapons skill"

### New Content Plans
- New lairs planned for both Nork and Cobrahn
- New items planned
- New Barbarian armor piece in Cobrahn expected "this month"
- New Mentalist direct damage ability planned "between ESPEAR and ELANCE"
- New disciplines planned for both psionic classes (Healer and Mentalist)
- Character art changes: new character pictures at level 20, 30, and 50, with plans for more individualized art
- A discipline might allow players to change their character model appearance

### Weekly Updates
- Brad agreed to post formal development updates (rather than relying on informal conference room chats)

## Class-Specific Discussions

### Mentalists
- Brad envisioned moving Mentalists from "moderate forever damage dealers to high damage short term damage dealers (as an option)"
- HP would remain primarily a fighter class domain - giving Mentalists more HP would require compensating fighter classes
- Each class has a stat that helps with HP (players need to figure out which setup works best)
- A new spell was planned between Enmiss and Espear for skill 10 Mentalists who were "not having much fun enmissing things to death"

### Healers
- Players requested built-in protection against their own Firestorm, which Brad hadn't considered
- Similarly, Mentalists requested protection from their own Shockwave - Brad: "sounds kind of obvious though. I'll write that one down"
- New discs planned for both psionic classes after talking to high-level players

### Barbarians
- Haste while zerked was being "seriously considered" for restoration
- Mid-level gap identified: high-level Barbs fine, but middle-level Barbs "get overly pounded" with nowhere to hunt
- Party unfriendliness acknowledged: zerking grabs items and runs off screen
- SoB (Snowbeast) fur as good Barbarian armor was discussed
- New Cob armor piece planned for Barbs

### Martial Artists
- "Hardest class to grow" - Brad
- Chi Sweep possibly underperforming (Sweep reportedly better)
- Non-functional DT (Death Touch) would be replaced with a new ability
- Big gap between Sweep and Chi abilities was acknowledged

### Thieves
- Skill system "100% due for an overhaul"
- Backstab gains were 5:1 compared to weapon skill - not intended
- Chit trainer in Guild Hall trains above skill 19

### Paladins
- MaxStrike + Haste gave 2-3 attacks per round
- Considered in good shape relative to other classes

### Fighters
- Power-halberd (P-hally) was requested
- Brad preferred adding new content over new P-weapons for 10-year-old quests

## System & Technical Topics

### Economy
- Gold transfer between Cob and Nork deliberately restricted
- Training costs at skill 29+: 900+ million gold
- Brad acknowledged training costs were "tricky" due to the chit trainer
- Item drop rates were varied intentionally "to create an economy"

### Online Features
- Alphabetized online user list: "so would i... I think thats actually doable"
- Text colors for different chat types (shouts, talk, sysop messages): planned
- Ignore user: planned as a slash command
- Friends list categories: wanted but dependent on the author's schedule
- Single consolidated forum: Brad planned to buy an EZBoard forum

### Character Services
- Character name changes: capability existed but not easy enough for a game feature. Would return when "draktools gets ported"
- Character transfers between accounts: "may offer a service in the future (for a fee)"
- Free account transfers from IEN to DZ: looked "bleak" due to man-hour costs

### Miscellaneous
- **Merchant class**: "I doubt i will ever have time to do that... BUT... MERCHANTING may soon become very profitable"
- **New races**: "probably not in the near future, since its just me doing the programming"
- **Promotion classes** (e.g., Pally -> Crusader, Ment -> Wizard): Brad had pitched the idea before and "got roasted"
- **Guild unique items** (special cloaks): "Sounds cool... with new artwork, who knows"
- **Loot rotting with NPC/environment kills**: Would not be reversed, but player-caused poison/etc. deaths would be fixed to drop corpses
- **The `ARG` command**: Syntax is `ARG <repeat>`
- **Target dummies**: Brad tried to implement but "it kept crashing the game"

## Notable Quotes

Brad on solo vs. party play:
> "Party gain is just 'not penalizing' people for partying, by giving them the skill of every kill. The alternative is to penalize them, and that is just not the point of a MULTIPLAYER game."

Brad on Drakkar's future:
> "I guess because I don't have the time and want to see my original creation be able to continue for another 10 years, and keep higher level people interested, instead of having them quit when there is nothing left to do."

Brad on lair creatures taking HP:
> "Lair creatures do take hit points on drakkarzone... Will this change? I dont think so, not for any mean reason, but it does seem to make them available to people who actually want to hunt the items."

Brad on Lori:
> "I don't think we will be changing lori, she dies all the time, and seems appropriate for her items. She is supposed to be insanely tough."
