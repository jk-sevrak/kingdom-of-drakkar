## Priority 1 — Core Sites

### mpgn.com (MPG-Net, 1991–1998)

MPG-Net was the dial-in gaming network founded by Jim Hettinger (Tantalus Inc., Key West FL) that hosted Kingdom of Drakkar as its flagship RPG. The 1997–1998 Wayback captures are historically critical.
