Tricks and Tactics
The Bear eXPress. To gain XP rapidly. Kill all Bears you see.
Memorize this layout in your head. Kaldor Castle woods to Timmy ladder going north and west  between Kaldor Castle and Graagh. Just on the Northwest side of Kaldor Castle is a good spot for bears. Sweep the forest in a farming pattern. Unlimited bear because of fast regen.
Cobrahn Brown Bear. Fair amount of HP.

Hally 2 Step. A way to carry less IH more loot.
Collect a zoo and lead them into a small room. In a 2 x 2 box pattern, start this method. Hit, move, hit, move. Barb way to solo 30 crits and Barney in Thumperville even if small.
Max Grubbin'
Develop a route in the areas you feel comfortable with. Run around and only kill the easy crits and scoop all the treasure you can find on the ground. Fill your sack even with the little gems and twig back and forth. This will raise cash in a hurry.

The Nork -4 Lure aka Dragging the whole level. Start in the upper west side room and slowly drag the crits down the hallway to the East, then go South to the other East/West hallway and continue West to the end. You will be rammed and attacked the whole way. Wear good gear. Once at the end of the road, you should have a 30+ crit zoo. For a 60+ zoo, go around again through a hole in the wall to the South connecting the trip in a loop. Use a multi-attack when the zoo is really big for skill bonus. (Rumor has it that a 10+ kill gains a skill bonus) Sweep is good for MA skilling. Other goodies will be dropped by the crits. Barbs like breakables like hp helms, rings, mountain boots.
