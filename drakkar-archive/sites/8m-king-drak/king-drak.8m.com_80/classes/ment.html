WELCOME TO THE MENTALIST BROTHERHOOD

You have chosen wisely brother Mentalist.

As a Mentalist, you have the power to defend you and your allies with powerfull spells, as well as to destroy your enemies with some of the most devesating offensive spells available.

But beware, for while you can easily kill any opponent for a distance, if they can reach you then you may be in trouble.

As a Mentalist, you have the least hit points of all the classes.  Your energy points (eps) are your lifeblood, if these run out then you must rely upon weapons, and with your limited amount of health, you will not last long in any serious battle.

If you think you are going to be soloing lairs or teleporting your friends all around nork, then Ment probably isn't for you. Ments arn't much use in lairs until at least skill 12 (when they get energy shield) and are extremely slow in gaining skill. Their area effect spells usually means that they can gain in experience fairly easily, but not skill.

ROLLING A NEW MENT

As with all classes, a high Charisma and Luck is essential.  These are the only two stats that are not able to be permanently raised during the game.  Willpower is also recommended, as these pots are very rare, and therefore very expensive.

The most important stat when rolling a ment is Intellegence.  The higher your int, the more eps you gain when leveling.  And as mentioned before, eps are your lifeblood, without them you can't cast spells, and without spells, ments are near useless.  Try to get this to at least 17, preferably higher.

Agility can help with avoiding attacks.  While this is not essential, it can help a lot, espeically at early levels where you don't have a good shield or decent MA ability.  Do not reroll a otherwise good roll just because agility is low.  Agility pots are fairly common and can be purchases from other players for relativly cheap - anywhere from free to 75k, usually around 50k.

Recommended races are either Woodlands Dweller or City Dweller.  Woodlands Dweller have a chance of getting 19 Intillegence from the starting roll, which can help a lot!  But they the same troubles as most other races have when trying to get high Charisma or Luck.  City Dweller's are boring - almost every player is a City Dweller, but they have the best chance of getting 18 Charisma and Luck.  My advice, try Woodlands Dweller until you get bored trying to get high Charisma/Luck, then go try City Dweller.

starting A NEW MENT

After dedicating at the Mentalist Trainer, you will receive your first and most important spell, ENMISS (energy missile).  Head down to n-1 and test it out.  For a measly 5 eps, it is your best offensive and skilling weapon.

By clicking on the mana bar (blue bar in the top right corner of the screen), you will see a number representation of the amount of mana you currently have compared to the maximum that you can have. I find this much easier to read, as i know exactely if I can cast a certain spell. It also tells you how much you regen each turn, allowing you to plan better.

Important skills for a Mentalist are, in order of importance:

    Mentalist    -     the most important
    MA            -     can improve your blocking ability quite considerably, even while carrying a shield.
    Staff           -     the best blocking weapon
    Bow           -    not essential, but helps when you have run out of eps

Obviously, your Mentalist skill is the most important as the one you should work on first and foremost.  I wouldn't go to far from n-1 until I'm around skill lvl 6 and have fireball.

The other skills can be left to for a while, as there is no need for them at the earlier levels.

Once you start kicking some serious butt on n-1, try going a little deeper.  There is nothing to dangerous till n-3.  Just watch out for anybody carrying a big sword or ranged weapon, and target them first.

At experience lvl 10, you might want to try your luck at prisons.  It is here that you will see the first weakness of ENMISS.  It can't hurt all creatures.  The stunners here are immune to it, but luckily are not immune to your area affect spells such as FIREBALL or ICESTORM.

By this stage you should be starting to wear some more advanced armor and cloaks. The breast plate armor found in n-2 and prison is much better than anything found on n-1 and will do until you can get anything better.  Yeti armor isn't too bad either, unfortunatley they are immune to ENMISS as well as FIREBALL and ICESTORM, which is where bow skill comes in (either that or ask for some help from another player). Basically you should be wearing the best armor you can. The only armor i've found that you shouldn't wear is full plate (fp), which halves your psi power when worn.  A rak cloak makes you practically immune to the Rakshasa Psi attacks, and will serve you well for a very long time.  It may also be time to start looking for a shield.  A simple wooden shield can be bought from a shop in Volcano Town, but these aren't that good.  Steel shields are often left on the counter in the Steel Flower for people to use, just ask before taking.  Or if you are feeling very lucky, you may try to find one in the area just east of Maeling.  Go out the east gate and go straight down.  There are usually 3 NPC's here, sometimes they carry shields.  This area is very hard, so beware.  You may try to lure one of them into town and let the townies help you kill it, but they are still very dangerous.  Your best bet is probably to ask a higher level player to get you a shield.

Sometime around this level you should try to get your hands upon a MUZI (mini-uzi).  This is one of the most important pieces of equipment and Mentalist can have.  It increases your eps regeneration by +2 per turn....this is a huge increase, allowing you to almost shoot ENMISS all day.  To do this you will need help.  First of all you need a ghost pearl from the ghost on n-3.5.  It may take a couple of goes before finding a pearl that ties to you, but ghost is pretty good skilling so its a win-win situation.  Next you need two silver bows.  The best place to find these are Fisheries.  You will definetely need help here, the archers carrying them hit VERY hard.  While you are there you might also want to pick up a staff, much better than a plain wooden one.  Now you will need a DEX to PEEWEE, so start asking around alt 1 SF, many larger players keep a twig set there for just such an occasion.  After giving the pearl and the bows to PEEWEE, you are the proud owner of a brand new MUZI -- prepare to kick some major butt!!

ADVANCED MENTALIST STRATEGIES

Start looking for Int rings.  By wearing these when leveling up, you receive more eps then you normally would.  There is no real need to wear these as your normal hunting gear though.

Once you reach skill lvl 10, you gain the ability to cast TRANSMUTE.  You will learn to love this spell.  It transforms anything on the ground into gold.  Not much gold mind you but a lot better than just leaving all the junk on the ground.  A MA friend of mine was pulling about 70k per trip from n-5, i was pulling about 150k from the same area...primarily due to TRANSMUTE.  To get maximum effectiness of this spell, try to kill enemies on the one spot, this builds the pile up and means that you only have to search and pop the one pile instead of several.  I usually build up 3 or 4 piles, this allows me to jump between the different piles, which means that I can still avoid most attacks, but still build up the piles.

MA ability improves your blocking ability remarkably.  Try to get your hands upon a pair of RD Gaunts, this improves your combat ability, and means that your punches do more damage, which helps while building up MA skill.  This is when you start to wish you had rolled higher Strength.

At higher skill levels you will experience a huge slow down, as it takes longer and longer to advance in skill.  There isn't too much that can be done about this, but some hints that have come through on the SAS forums lately:
 

Party with a thief of higher or equal level to you.  When a thief backstabs, he donates his thieving skill to the party, which means that the skill that you last used will gain the skill.  This should give you a large boost in skill gain.

Use ENMISS instead of the area affect spells.

Concentrate solely on skilling.  Don't worry too much about coining.

And of course, Train.
