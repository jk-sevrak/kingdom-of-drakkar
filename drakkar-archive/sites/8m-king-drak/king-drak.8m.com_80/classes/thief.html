THIEF
So here is the deal my main crit is actually a mentalist but I also play a thief, since I am the only thief in the guild I am the thief leader and have been asked to write this info page the information I will present will be from my limited knowledge and from what I have picked up talking to people in the Steel Flower.

ROLLING A THIEF

Well as always your should always try to get the best luck and charisma stats possible because pots can not be found or purchased for these, also you should get a high agility stat because agility helps you hide, steal and also helps u defend from attacks. You can find or buy agil pots rather easily but I find it easier just to get a good roll in it. Also strength is a must, thieves must also use weapons they can not just relay on thievery, yes u can get a pot for this but they are rather expensive 500k is the going price in the SF.

RIGHT, LETS START STEALING!!

Right now that you have good a good roll (I hope) and are ready to start using your thief go to your macros and set a macro for steal and hide, then head for N-1.

Right now go up to a few crits and start trying to steal from them you might have trouble getting anything from them but dont worry you are still getting good skill once u have tried to steal off 1 crit 10 times kill it with either a bow of maybe a dagger you can purchase by the thief trainer though the wall at the Steel Flower for 500 gold coins, continue to steal off a crit 10 times (even if you get the message “you cant seem to find anything to take” keep stealing from him till you have tried to steal off him 10 times and then kill with a weapon of your choice.

You should find that thieves skill extremely fast, when I first started my thief I reached skill 4 in the first 10 minutes I went to see if I had reached skill 2 yet but to my amassment I was already skill 4.

Oh and one very important thing Train as much as you can when ever you can!! You will find it makes skilling even faster. At skill 4 or 5 you should start to be able to hide this you will find is very handy, but remember it depends on your skill lvl as to what crit’s you can hide from so dont go thinking nothing can see you when you are hiding.

At skill 8 set a marco for backstab this is very handy to and you will grow to love this, When you are hidden you can put a dagger or other weapon (apart from bow which is pretty useless for backstab) in you hand and backstab a crit form a few hexes back, you will automatically attack and move to the hex of your foe with Increased damage then attacking normally.

OK now you have reached skill 10 set a marco for mug and head to the VT trainer this guy is the guy you go to for your disciplines go to class train and you should get Door and a few others.

By the way thieves do not have EP’s (energy points) when they form a spell they use Hp’s instead so watch ya self when using them.

Right now you might as well change your steal marco to the mug marco because you wont need to use steal anymore, as you will find mug is much better than steal, mug is kinda like a backstab using steal you automatically move to the same hex as your target and steal from it in one go and you will also find it inflicts a tiny bit of damage 1-2 hp.

Well by now N-1 should be getting a bit boring you can go and have a look around N-2,N-3,N-3.5,yeti cave (ground floor) and maybe the prison (if you have a bit of help) until skill 12 you get the same amount of skill stealing from N-1 crits then any other place so if you want a safe place to skill just stick with good old N-1 yes I know its boring but you can soon get out of there.

Right now at skill 12 its time to get some weapon skill and if u have reached lvl 13 time to start maxing hp’s as well. But before you start skilling ya weapon you have to choose which wep u prefer.

If you want to hit really hard then choose either a longsword or a great sword, you will b amazed how hard you can backstab with either of these, but the down side of these hard hitting weapons you will find that you need more skill to hide from crits than if you used smaller weapons.

If you want to b able to hit things without been seen then dagger is the way to go it nearly doesn’t prohibit you from hiding at all the downside of course is that it doesn’t have the hitting force of the bigger weapons.

Then there are weapons that can hit from a far taking even less skill to use them without being seen such as bow. The Poison Bow is a favourite with new thieves but it can take the xp from your skills.

HOOD'S NOTE ON PBOW: I love the Poison Bow. Its small enough to allow you to hide easily, and it hits heaps harder then any of the other bows i've found (at least the ones that are easy to get). It can be used as a backstabbing weapon, sure it doesn't hurt as much as Zed's Slicer does, but it does enough. The downside, as Zed has mentioned is that if the poison kills the target, you lose the exp from the kill. Personally i think the ability of being able to kill from the shadows makes up for this, but make up your own mind...

Now that you have chosen a weapon go skill it on a mino if it is silver or maybe a orge chief (till skill 10) well just see the pally section about skilling weapons :-) Now once you have a weapon skill of maybe 10 or 11, you can start playing with backstab.

When you backstab you get both weapon and thief skill, you hit MUCH!!! harder than normal and its just plain FUN!!!! At skill 13 thief and 12LS I hardly ever hit normally at all im always using backstab because of the GREAT skill gain and awesome hitting power.

Well thats all there is to say once you get to about skill 12 thief and 12 weapon its all just backstab, Backstab, BACKSTAB from there.

THIEF GEAR

Helm: Hp helm till you can get hold of a power helm (only large crits own P helms)

Sash: Rat Borrow Quest sash

Gaunts: What ever you can get hold of :)

Bracs: depends on what protection you need

Boots: Alerian hippogriffen boots are great for backstabing

Rings: AGIL RINGS!! what ever you can get hold of

Armour: fullplate from levi (can only trade for it at lvl 15)

Cloaks/Robes: Black Ninja Robes (+2 agil found at maeling -3) or even better camo robes!! (+2 agil and +1 hiding skill, found randomly at aleria best luck to find them is at seldair -1 or -2)

Weapons: Longsword: sliver griffen sword is a good one to start with when you get bigger try to get hold of a slicer and if you get VERY big get a saber (drools)

Greatsword: well get a normal GS and get it enchanted by a good ment to +4 or if you get big enough get a GS slicer

Bow: Poison bow (get by killing n-5 roc) or KM-4 or KM-5 or trade KM-5 for a thief only crossbow (great for hiding with)

Dagger: +2 sliver dagger purchased at thief trainer at SF, better tho is the +3 bullywog dagger from a bullywog of course (also can be thrown, it is returning) and if you can get the mother of all daggers the silleto this dagger is a power weapon (sliver and glowing) and has some mean poison, you have to kill a breash for this weapon.
