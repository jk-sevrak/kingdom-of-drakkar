SAS ver. 5.12 - Build 216

 

New features:


Dynamic game categorizing.
Games can now be grouped by either genre type (Fantasy, Strategy, etc.) or subscription level (Free, Premium, etc).
In addition, genre type doors (or subscription level doors) can be moved around in the room or hidden from view.
Click on OPTIONS/GameCustomControl on the panel to see more options.

Game Parental Controls added.
Games, Ratings groups or Rating attributes can be locked. A game becomes "Blocked" if it is either explicitly locked, or its Rating group is locked, or one of its Rating attributes it locked.  Making changes to Parental Control settings requires providing your account's security word.
Click on OPTIONS/GameCustomControl on the panel to see more options.

Game Session Lists.
In certain games, a session list is provided, for users to be able to join one of many sessions provided by that game. Some games only have one default session available, in which case the session list is hidden from view.

Private Messages between users (non-table messages) now use a new format.
They now display and keep track of messages sent from both parties so you can look back and follow the flow of conversation more easily. You can also save the conversation in a log.

 

Bug fixes:


Lots of small bug fixes, over the course of several months.
