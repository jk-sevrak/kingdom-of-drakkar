SAS 24bit versions 5.00 - 5.02  (Builds 200 - 202)

 

Where do I start from?


The SAS undergoes a facial. New interface, new look, 24 bit graphics, more functionality (and more to be added soon)

 

New features (Among other things) :


The button toolbar is replaced by the panel (or dashboard if you prefer).

From it you can access pretty much everything.

Your active persona shows on the center of it, and clicking on your face will show your active shield.

Drop a persona card from your wallet to the panel to make it your active persona.

Using the panel COM button you can access general communication features/options such as AFK and DND as well as your speed-dial.

Using the panel PDA button you can access your profile, wallet, cardfile, briefcase, editor, and folder browser.

Use the HOME (lobby), EXIT and Navigation arrows on the panel (as well as the JUMP TO arrow) to navigate thru the system.

New "User Map" feature will show number of players in each room.

To the right on the panel, watch the notification icons flash upon new events, like new email, new forum messages, new/unread newspaper articles etc..

Access the current room announcement and game invitations right from your panel.

The panel and main view (root window) can be aligned to either left, center or right on your screen.


The newsreader is now integrated with the panel. The Forums icon will flash when new messages are detected in any of your subscribed newsgroups. Clicking on the icon will pop-up a menu with some basic forum functions as well as a list of all newsgroups that have messages in them so you can change to them from right there.

New option will bring the SAS to the foreground when calls, messages or pages arrive.

New keyboard shortcuts. Right click anywhere on the background for a complete list of commands with their shortcuts.

 

Bug fixes:


The SAS can now recover a download even if it crashes while the download is in progress.

Bugs fixed relating to the ad system.

Several other smaller bugs fixed - it's been a while since I last counted.
