NEW FEATURES:

Ad Cache options. Options for specifying how long before cached ads expire and how big you allow the cache folder to get. Right click on the ad window and choose settings to view the ad settings window.

Cache optimizations. Ads are now saved as Compressed Raw Gifs instead of encoded Gifs. Can be read up to 8 times faster than regular encoded Gifs, and are usually a bit smaller in size)

DirectSound support.
If DirectSound is available on your system and enabled from the SAS Sound Control window then it will be used.
With DirectSound you get better quality sounds playing simultaneously, as well as directional sounds. Else sounds play as before (one at a time, no direction)
Option added in the Sound Control window (under tools toolbar) to turn DirectSound support on or off on the fly (by default it is turned on).
Option also added to reverse speaker order if in Direct Sound mode.

NOTE: When DirectSound is enabled only applications that use DirectSound can play sounds.

"Non priority sound" support. If not using Direct Sound, sounds such as the context menu sounds and the clock tick sound will no longer interrupt other sounds.

Now if you click on doors they will open right away (bypassing the default delay).

Screen toolbar buttons now also show the 'hand' cursor.

Other smaller changes/improvents...too small to mention.

RELATING NOTES:

In order for the SAS to use DirectSound you need to have DirectSound 5
installed on your computer.

If you don't have it you can get it from here:

http://www.microsoft.com/msdownload/directx/dxf/enduser5.0

If you are having problems using it in the SAS one of the following may be
happening:

1. If the DirectSound option in the SAS Sound Control window does not turn on,
then you either don't have DirectSound 5 or you have a previous version of it.
The SAS needs DirectSound 5 in order to utilize DirectSound.

2. If the DirectSound option in the SAS Sound Control window DOES turn on, but
sounds don't play as they should, then you have DirectSound 5 but some of your
Sound Card drivers may not be installed or up-to-date. Install DirectX 5 again
and reinstall those drivers.

In Any case, you can always turn the option off in the SAS Sound Control window
so that the SAS doesn't even try to use DirectSound.
