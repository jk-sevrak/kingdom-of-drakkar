NEW FEATURES:

In the newsreader, an "auto-filler' feature was added.
When you click to POST, the program now calculates and adds as many blank lines
at the end of your post as necessary (and if needed), so that to please the
News Server's need to have at least as many new lines of text as included lines
of text.
(i.e. now you shouldn't get a message saying "more included text than new text")
