I no longer hear sounds in the SAS.
 When I try to exit the SAS, a window pops up saying "Shutting down" but it never goes away and the SAS never exits

You're probably having problems with Direct Sound.
It seems that you have DirectX installed on your computer, but it is missing some drivers.
You can do one of the following:

  • Disable DirectSound play from the SAS Sound Control window (under the
    tools toolbar - sounds will still play but without using DirectSound)
  • Reinstall DirectX and update its drivers, then try DirectSound from the SAS again.
    You can get the latest DirectX from here: http://www.microsoft.com/directx/homeuser/downloads/default.asp

When entering the SAS, the system freezes before or while playing the welcome sound

It looks like your system is having problems playing sounds using DirectSound.
The SAS will detect that the next time you run it and it will turn DirectSound Off for you.
If you're still having problems playing sounds with DirectSound turned off, the SAS will detect that too and turn Sounds Off alltogether next time you run it.

While the SAS is running I don't get sound to play from my other applications.

When DirectSound is enabled in the SAS, only applications that use DirectSound can play sounds.
If you want to be able to hear sounds from other applications, turn DirectSound off from the SAS Sound Control window.

I can no longer get the SAS to resize.

When using DirectSound in the SAS this may cause the above behavior (under some Win95 systems).
Until we fix this or until you upgrade to Win98, you can turn DirectSound off from the SAS Sound Control window.

I sometimes see the wrong faces on players.

Hold down the Ctrl key and right click on the player's face either from a crystal ball (if on a table), or from the player list window (active list).
Choose the "Repair Face" option. Give it a few seconds, the new face will appear (if it needs repairing).

When I switch from a group in the Newsreader, all the headers in that group dissapear, they don't show up again.

One of 2 things may be happening here :

  • When you leave a group the newsreader will remove any expired articles if so specified in the Newsreader Options Window (under the Files tab).
    The option looks something like this: "Remove articles older than..."
    You can turn this option off or set its value to a larger number of days so that the articles don't expire as soon.
    The expiration period is based on the day the article was posted and NOT on the day you downloaded it, so if you just downloaded an article that was posted 10 days ago (if you haven't visited that group for 10 days or more), and you have the above setting set to 6 days for example, when you leave the group, that article will be removed since it is already expired.
    When using "Get Next x Headers", that will retrieve the previous block of headers (older ones), typically expired ones, and if they are expired, they'll be removed when you leave that group (if above option is set).
  • You may be in "View Unread Messages Only" mode, so when you go back to
    that group you don't see any messages since you've already read them.
    You can change from "View Unread Messages Only" to "View All" from the Article commands
    menu (or use Alt-M to toggle between them)

I set myself to DND, so why can people still page me in the game?

The DND status only affects you while in the SAS.
If you want to block pages in the game then do so from the game.
In Drakkar type "pager enable" or "pager disable". "pager status" will show you the current pager on/off status
Other games have different ways of doing that.
