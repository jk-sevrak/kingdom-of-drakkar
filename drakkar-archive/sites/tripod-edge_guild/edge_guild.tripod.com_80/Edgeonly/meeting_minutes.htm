The biggest this to note here is it looks better for Edge if we can do basic things without  the aid of other guilds, so if you can wait , please do so , just remember what its like when the shoe is on the other foot.

This goes for Cob and for Nork stuff please try to be patient and other members if asked please try your best to help out as soon as you can
