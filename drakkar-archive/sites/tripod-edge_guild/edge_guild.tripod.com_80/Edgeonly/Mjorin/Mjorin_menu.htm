The path to Mjorin

To get to Mjorin you need to go un the Funhouse in Nork town. When you get up the funhouse you take the steps at the top. you will then be in a portal room , take the lower portal ( the top portal will take you back to nork )

NOTE : when you step in the lower portal you will be stripped
