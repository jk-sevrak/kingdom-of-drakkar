Just some notes on things in cob :

- If you are in timmy town and want to get to tree town , quickest way is to stand in the door but the IH seller and slam, then when you load back in you will be in tree town.

- Silver rocks are found in the timmy caves , I found 3 in the NE corner up buy the brass ring trader

- Brass rings  are to be found on the MA guys in the desert

- Bubble shields are found in the secret part of timmy caves down the hole then up to the NE

- Notes on padding , Bear gives +3 extra adds , Yeti gives +4

- You can find the ES rings in the NW caves , they are rare though

- You get to the spec lair by going south west of the bridge from Boone , you will see a pile of bones in the bottom on the map , climb up here,,, 

- The run to DT is hard but not impossible , you start via the roc lair

- The HP doc will give you 1 hp / crit requested, max 550 base for a pally at lvl 20 tested so far 

- There is a guy in desert town that will give mini fate pots if you get him the right info 

- There is a lady called Dion that if you are very nice to will give you fate pots , she moves all over cob 

- There are some extra lockers in the desert town all you have to do is find the password
