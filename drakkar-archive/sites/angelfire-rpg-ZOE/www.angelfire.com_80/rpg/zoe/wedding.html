The Wedding
Talon Dracule was Zoe's love and almost as soon as they met, they knew they were to be together... forever. Well, she soon found out that "together forever" meant... 2 days or so before he would try to have some other chick kill her. Sadly, this was a huge disappointment to her. In the beginning... Talon loved her just as deeply as she loved him. He gave her this ring when he asked her to marry him.
She was completely thrilled and set off at once to make plans. First stop? Ariel's shop to pick out a dress on the internet. Zoe had whipped out her laptop and soon Ariel and her were looking at dresses. When she saw this one, she knew she had to have it.
Zoe was, of course, beautiful at the wedding, wearing this slammin' red and white lace dress. Wun Shadow, her best friend and bodyguard served as best man. It all seemed so perfect. But now it is all over... her only question?
"Why the hell did I have to ruin the dress by wearing it to the wedding?"
After all... she looked so good, if she were ever to wear it again, no doubt there would be a second wedding in the future.
