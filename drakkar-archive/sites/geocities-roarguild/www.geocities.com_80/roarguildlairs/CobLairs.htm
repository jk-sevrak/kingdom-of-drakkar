Information

 

Black Dragon:

Claws and casts acid. kill and tan for his scales, search corpse for a black pearl (his eye) and a key. Located in gauntlet (north area of t-1)

 

 

Beetle:

Claws you and steals Exp, as well as uses a %hp attack, casts Sw. kill and tan for his scales. Located down a hole in the swamps.

 

 

Death Sasquatch:

Hits with a club. in his lair there are many psi crits around. kill and search for  water scroll, possible 10/0 ring. tan him for great barb armor or trade armor for a king shield. Located on ud-5.

 

 

Loriapi:

Hits with a staff that takes hp if not wearing a lori robe. casts espear, elance, shockwave, deplete. kill and search for her robe, staff, 5/5 sash, 8/8 ring, possible Yp or Statue (Always drops 1 of the 2).

 
 

Gold Dragon:

Claws and uses Earthcrush. kill and tan for scales, search corpse for a key and a possible gold bar that trades for ec ammy. Located in gauntlet (north area of t-1).

 

 

Silver Dragon:

Claws,  Casts lightning. kill and tan for scales, search corpse for diamond with a dragon inside and a key. Located in gauntlet (north area of t-1).

 

 

Red Dragon:

Claws and casts Firebreath. kill and tan for scales, search for a fire ruby and a key. Located in gauntlet (north area of t-1).

 

 

Boris:

2 located in lair. Hits with a greatsword. kill and search for possible 6/6 sash, a good gs, and possible Yp. Located down stairs in south part of Nw caves.

 

 

Mama Green Dragon:

Claws and uses corspray. kill and tan for scales search for gem. Located in gauntlet (north area on t-1)

 

 

Roc:

Claws you. kill and search for an egg that trades for ma only gauntlets. Located up some Hay in south area of nw caves.

 

 

Automan:

Attacks with a bow, Autoheals when almost dead which is infinite. kill and search for crappy bow and crappy cloak. Located in Lori's lair.

 

 

Kaldor:

Hits with a mace that damages gear, casts stun, Psi crush and Ec. kill and search for his robe, dagger, mace, key, boots and ammy. located in Kaldor castle.

 

 

Vanidor:

Hits with an axe, hits 2 times per round (1 time per axe) kill and search for vani plate, 2 axes and a sash. Located Under ud-5

 

 

Graagh:

Hits with a hally. kill and search for his hally and a 8/8 ring. Located on the top floor of Graagh castle.

 

 

Juntes:

Punches you. kill and search for juntes gauntlets and robe. Located west from graagh castle in a tent.

 

 

Mr. Alvecara:

Hits with a bow. kill and search for his bow, sash robe, bracers. Located above evil timmy's lair.

 

 

Ningizidda:

Bites you, which poisons you, claws. kill and tan for scales, search corpse for helm. located under Arun lair area. (in the big hole)

 

 

Evil timmeh:

Hits with a bow and has a pet wolf, pets name is woof. kill and search for robe and bow. located in the south area of t-2 up some stairs.

 
 

Giant King:

Hits with a hally or greatsword. kill and search for his hally and sash. located up some stairs in ancient keep area.

 

 

Son Of Graagh:

Hits with a hally or spear. kill and search for either weapon. Located above Graagh's lair. there are 5 sogs.
