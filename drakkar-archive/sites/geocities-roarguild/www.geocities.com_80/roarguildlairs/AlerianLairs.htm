Information

 

Fire Lizard:

Claws and casts Firebreath. kill and search for 0/2 -5 hp bracers. Located northwest of mujaba lair.

 

 

Hippogriff:

Claws you. kill and search for haste boots. Located in the desert, randomly regains.

 

 

Great wyrm:

Bites you, has on-contact stun and poison. kill for possible rares. Located in Thumperville.

 

 

Ghoul Lord:

Claws you. kill and search for possible rares. Found in Ghoul dungeons through a wall.

 
 

Koss:
Hits with a mace. kill and search for his mace. located east of Treehouse climb up ladder.

 

 

Bandit lord:

Hits with a sword, Casts Blind. Kill and search for his bandit lord sword. located southeast of the broken town in the alerian ice flows.

 

 

Tarak:

Hits with a hammer which has on-contact blast, casts assault. kill and search for his hammer and e/e robe, which trades for g/g or n/n robe.

 

 

Duke:

Hits with a sword, kill and search for his Shield. Located Northwest of the boat guy.

 

 

Mujaba:

Hits with a dagger. kill and search for his dagger and a key for ch, drops random rares rarely. located around the east end of alerian desert.

 

 

Slimebeast:

Bites you, which poisons you, also damages gear. kill and search for taraks hammer for taraks quest. located northwest of iceflows down a grass hex.

 

 

Goblin King:

Hits with an axe. kill and search for his axe which is used to get the light ring. Located under dwarf village through some caves.

 

 

Kragan:

Attacks with a club, which has on-contact push, similar to cloud giant. kill and search for his push club and a useless helm. Located down in Nw caves.

 

 

Gothar:

Attacks you with assault. Can drop rares. located under koss.

 

 

Seldari Queen:

Attacks and Casts lightning, kill and search for sq goblet. located on s-2.
