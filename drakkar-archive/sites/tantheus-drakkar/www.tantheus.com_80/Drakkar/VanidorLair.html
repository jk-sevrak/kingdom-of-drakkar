Cobrahn : Vanidor Lair Stairs

Thanks to Seville's Page

Vandor would welcome you to his lair by shaking hands but his hands are full with 2 Pets and 2 Axes. Axes are +5. Best tactic is to draw off and kill each Pet in turn, then take on Vanidor directly. A very fun Lair. This cave leads down the stairs to Vanidor. Useful as a staging area. Taking the stairs Northeast of the Lair will take you to Undead City -5.
