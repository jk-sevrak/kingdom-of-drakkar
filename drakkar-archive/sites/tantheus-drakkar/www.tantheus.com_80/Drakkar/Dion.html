Bring Dion green stone rings of high value (6200 GP+) or a diamond. He will reward you with a mini fate potion. Various estimates say 75% to 90% are good fates. Take a chance. Dion will not trade while in danger so you will have to kill off the surrounding zoo. Below are just some of the various locations Dion is likely to pop up.
