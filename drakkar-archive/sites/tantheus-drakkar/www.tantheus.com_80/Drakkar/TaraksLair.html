Kram and I stomping Tarak. hehe

Kill Slimey and take his hammer. Go to Carrena and go down hatch. Give Hammer to Tarak by saying hammer. He kills you with it and you re-appear at healer. No stat change. Then go to Kiara the cloak trader for a blessing and get enchanted by repeating the last three words thus allowing you to pass safely by the whirlpool. Go to Mujaba and kill him for his Punch Dagger. Get a float bottle from Karsov on Monkey Island by trading dagger for it and float up to the Tarak. Say Enter the Southwest Caves. Find the Whirlpool. Then West to a clearing. When you are under his castle, drink the float bottle. He has a zoo and Blast Hammer to destroy you. Kill him and take his Hammer and Evil Tarak Robe. Evil Robe can be traded for another in Carrena with Kiara. Once you have traded the robe, you will have to be re-enchanted to return to Tarak's Castle.graverobber.
