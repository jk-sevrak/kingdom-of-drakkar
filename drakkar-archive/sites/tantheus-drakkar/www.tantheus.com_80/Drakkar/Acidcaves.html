Acid Caves:

Thanks to Seville's Page

From the West gate of Frore, continue West to stairs and go up. Door/blow up wall to the South and get in this room. Take the stairs down if you want to go to the Acid Caves.

Acid Caves:

Good place to coin and find leather rings. Drop down to the Dark Tower or the Multilair if you dare. Go west through the water to go to Evil Nork. Protection from Acid Bracers are always on the ground.
