Aleria evil Quest Information:

Thanks to Shar's Page

Prize is a +30 hit point poor blocking good aligned shield that can also be traded for a EVIL infra helm that gives user a limited infra on contact. Able to see 1 HEX away in darkness.

Before finishing the EVIL quest you need to have 3 items.
BANDIT LORD SWORD SELDARI QUEEN GOBLET WW FUR
You may obtain these before starting the quest and will probably make it a little quicker overall.

1. Go E/E by killing alerian townies (or a volunteer) using grenades, or PSI. Make sure they are not RED before the killing blow.

2. Go to Culcula: She is on a small island to the NE of the con seller.
 Type: Cul, hello
Cul, allegiance

3. Go to Evil Tower (all NO TWIG...IN OR OUT): It is SW of the con seller...It is helpful to have the BL sword and the Queen's cup with you at this time.

A. Go NE to stairs leading up, go up and stand on Hessaan...DO NOT try to pass him the skull will try to kill you but never will.
Type: Hes, pass skull
Hes, talinar
Hes, pass skull
Hes, ela
Hes, pass skull
Hes, borav
B. Go upstairs to Lord Koss.
Type: Lor, hello
Lor,kneel
C. Go downstairs to where you started...from the stairs go SW to stairs leading down. You will be in a dungeon. You need to go W out the door, then N in the small room (it helps to have detect on, because there are secret walls that you have to pass now). Go through the walls SE til you get to Koron.
Type: Kor, hello
Kor, mission
(If you have not done so, you must now go kill the Bandit Lord and get his sword and take it back to Koron)
Drop BL sword at Koron's feet;
Type: Kor, Banditlord
Pick up sword (DO NOT say anymore to Koron at this point or else he will make U repeat BL quest over and over)

4. Go to Koss
Type: lord, ELA
He will send you back to Culcuta, go to her and
Type: Cul, ELA
she will ask for the Goblet
(If you have not done so, you must now go kill Queen Cyressa and get her Cup and take it to Lady Ela) Go to Climb up Stairs in the Forrest: NW of the Con Seller, but not directly; NW of the wall you pass before you get to the con seller.
A. Climb up and go to Lady Ela, hold cup from the Queen in your hand.
Type: Lad, cup
She will trade the cup for the Goblet, and you will hear Lord Koss, yelling.

TIP: NEVER EVER drag goblet to your face, as it will disappear and you will NOT get it replaced!

5. Go to Culcula
Type: Cul, hello
Put the goblet in your hand
Type: Cul, goblet
6. Go to Koron
Type: Kor, hello
Kor, hello
(If you have not done so, you must now go kill and tan a Winter Wolf for his fur)
7. Go to Arogar: NW in building past the ice flows...he is trader there in the s room.
DROP WW Fur
Type: Aro, fur
Pick up the coins<br> 8. Go to Koron
Type: Kor, hello
Kor, hello
9. Go to Lord Koss
Type: Lor, hello
Lor, quest
This whole process should take less than 1/2 hour if you have the 3 items before you 'go evil'. At this point, I believe you need NOT be EVIL anymore. So you can atone yourself as you see fit. Thus even Paladins can do the EVIl quest with np since they only need to be evil for a short period of time.
THE FINAL BATTLE:
( You must have found or obtained a Pink Leaf from a friend at this time.)
10. Go to Good Tower to the NW of Urmak (boat pass seller). Hold leaf and go upstairs and kill Talinar for your shield.
Further info. about how to fight Talinar can be provided upon request :-).
You may use the shield or go back to Talinar (Requires a 2nd leaf) and trade for one of the following:
1. Fortune ( 1 meg GP )
2. E/E Infravision Helm - -1 Luck )
