Tarak's Hammer Quest Information:

Thanks to Tsarns Page , Special thanks to Wuotan_Edge for additional comments.

Start by talking to Tarak while he in in jail, and he will tell you of the slimebeast and the hammer you must get from it (HAVE to be there and see/hear it die for this to work)....kill slimey, take hammer back to tarak.drop it on him and say tar,hammer..at this point, he escapes and kills you. you will appear at the healers..go to the guard outside the jail...things you can say to him- , tarak , south ,castle he tells about Kiara when asked about the castle....go to her and say kia,blessing and repeat the words she tells you to..afterward, are able to pass the whirlpool in the southrn caves. to get to tarak, kill mujaba, take his dagger to Karazov on the island south of his tent, drop dagger and say "kar,kukri". He will drop a levitation potion for each dagger....take the potion past the whirlpool and search for a grey area (the shadow of taraks castle) on one hex there is text saying you are directly below the castle....drink the pot and you float up to his castle (this is called the float spot)

once there, there is a zoo to deal with in the 1st area, and up the stairs there, is another zoo and taraks lair..kill him and take the prizes, a +4 lv5 blast hammer and a evil aligned robe u trade to kiara (drop and say kia,bounty) for a neutral or good aligned robe (depends on align of crit trading) .
