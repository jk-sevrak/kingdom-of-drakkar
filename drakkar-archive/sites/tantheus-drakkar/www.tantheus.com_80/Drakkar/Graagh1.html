Graagh Castle Surface
Beware of the Graagh! His minions surround a walled in cornfield that has Pitcher Plants lurking amongst the ears of corn. Ment crits, archers and hally bearing crit are in this square shaped area. Climb down the well in the center to see the duke and explore Graagh-1. Climb up at the corner stairs to go into individual rooms at the corners of the castle. Some rooms contain Archers that use blue Espear Bows. Climbing the center stairs in the back of this level, you can reach Graagh +1 and contiue around with crits firing missile weapons from the slit holes in the walls. Note to Self: Bring a Healer with CC. Kill the Graagh for a Barb only weapon or trade it to the duke for 100 HP. Works only once.
