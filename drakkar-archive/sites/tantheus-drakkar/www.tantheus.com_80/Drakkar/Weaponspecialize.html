Weapons Specialization: By Seville

Referenced : Seville's Page

All fighter types are allowed to be specialised in weapon classes. Each specialisation you have is like an extra skill level in that weapon class. You are allowed 3 specialisations per character. You may choose to specialise or spec all in one weapon class or up to 3 different ones at one spec level each. Even two specs on a weapon and one on another is allowed. However, if you want the weapon that hits very hard, spec'ing all on one weapon class is recommended. You do not have to spec in a weapon until you want to if eligible. You must go to your Trainer to spec a weapon. Hold weapon in right hand and Type (trainer name), specialise (weapon class) The first spec level comes at 15th Level. The next at 17th and again at 19th. However,..
If you wish to Triple Spec in a single weapon class, you must spec at 15th, 19th and 24th. Its a hard road but its worth it.
