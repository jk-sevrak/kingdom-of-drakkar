Back to Aleria.
Kill Slimey and take his hammer. Go to Carrena and go down hatch. Give Hammer to Tarak by saying hammer. He kills you with it and you appear at healer. No stat change. Then go to Kiara the cloak trader and get enchanted by repeating the last three words. Go to Mujaba and kill him for his Punch Dagger. Get a float bottle from Karsov on Monkey Island by trading dagger for i and float up to the Tarak. Say Enter the Southwest Caves. Find the Whirlpool. Then West to a clearing. When you are under his castle, drink the float bottle. He has a zoo and Blast Hammer to destroy you. Kill him and take his Hammer and Evil Tarak Robe. Evil Robe can be traded for another in Carrena with Kiara.
