Chipper Key. Go to Frore and talk to Billy. Get his pet's fetching stick and go east to big clearing. Lead the Moose back to Billy to get the key. Chipper Key ties.
Light Ring. Kill Goblin King and take his Axe. Go to the Dwarf Mine village and trade for ring with King Borav by dropping Axe and saying Kin,goblinking.
Poison Amulet. Enter the Seldari Forest and go to the statues. Read them and go east to Elshea. Drop Light Ring at her feet. Repeat the words to her and receive the "pammy".
STR Halberd. Go down in the KM dungeons and get a 6 oz Bloodroot and a Minotaur Blood. Go to KM -5 and hold both items and trade for STR Halberd.
Mummy King. Go to Nork -3 and kill monsters until you find a key. Go to the Northeast corner of Nork -3 and open locked door. Kill the MummyKing and tan it. Go behind the west bank of Nork and talk to Wartberg. Drop Mummy Cloak and 1995 GP to trade for Protstun Amulet.
