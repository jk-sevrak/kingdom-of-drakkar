Poison   Psionic Cost: 20 EP

Turns a creature's blood to poison. The toxicity and duration of the poison depends on psionicist's skill level. Low stress factor.
PowerWord Death (Area Discipline)   Psionic Cost: 100 EP

Allows psionicists to funnel their psionic energy into a powerful one-word assault that inflicts grave damage, according to the skill level of the psionicist, upon all visible hostile creatures. Allies and friendly creature are not affected. Very high stress factor.
PowerWord Heal  (Area Discipline)  Psionic Cost: 100 EP

Allows psionicists to funnel their psionic energy into a powerful one-word command that heals the psionicist and all visible allies afflicted with poison, blindness or physical damage. Very high stress factor.
MassTeleport   Psionic Cost: 250 EP

Allows a character to teleport a number of other characters to a memorized location.
PassWall   Psionic Cost: 20 EP

Allows a character to pass through walls and some types of rock. The duration of the discipline is proportional to the psionicist's skill level. Be careful that you are not inside something solid when the discipline depletes and if so, an explosion will occur. Moderate stress factor.
ProtFire   Psionic Cost: 10 EP

Creates a fire-resistant shield around a character. You can form this discipline on yourself or others. Low stress factor.
Mend   Psionic Cost: 5 EP

Cures minor damage. The amount of damage mended is proportional to the psionicist's skill level. You can form this discipline on yourself and others. Low stress factor.
InfraVision   Psionic Cost: 25 EP

Allows a character even in the darkest conditions by displaying most creatures and terrain in the infrared spectrum. You can form this discipline on yourself and others. To cast on others, you must be at least skill level 12. Low stress factor.
LimVis   Psionic Cost: 10 EP

Makes a character invisible to most creatures, even if the character takes damage. However, the character become immediately visible if the character inflicts damage. You can form this discipline on yourself and others. The duration of this discipline is proportional to the psionicist's skill level if formed on others and unlimited if formed on yourself. To cast on others, you must be at least skill level 17. Low stress factor.
Lightning   Psionic Cost: 15 EP

Creates a bolt of lightning. Low stress factor.
Light   Psionic Cost: 10 EP

Illuminates an area. Counteracts the Darkness discipline and may break the Hide discipline of nearby thieves. Low stress factor.
