Stuff to Know
How does one get Club House access?
1. Kill Chipuda and trade Staff for Power Helm.
2. Kill Breshard and trade Stilletto for Power Robe.
3. Kill Uther and trade Broadsword for Sabre.
Sweep Homlet with Stiletto. Everything will fall fast to poison and you can collect the coin.
To look at yourself, type List Self.
To look at someone else, type List (Crit's Name).
To look at what is in a player's hand, type Look at (Crit's Name)'s (Item Name).
Sometimes items that look the same aren't. Go to a shop and put item on counter. Type (Shopkeeper's Name), appraise (Item Name). If doing a lot of items like rings, make a macro and change the names when necessary.
To see your skills and specializations, type List Skills.
To see your stats, alignment and movement, type List Stats.
How to tell if an area is twiggable? Look at a twig. If glow is subdued then area is nontwiggable.
If you are a barb and in a lair fighting, keep hitting zerk and it will attack if it doesn't increase your rage.

Search the grass up to a few hexes away from the gates of Frore. The Gold Dragons kill all monsters that trespass into the city. You might be surprised at what you will find sometimes.
Disarming Traps:  All characters with some degree of thievery skill, not just thieves, can attempt to disarm traps. Thieves do not need to disarm traps, they can just simply pick them up. To attempt to disarm a trap, type disarm, direction. Valid directions are N, S, E, W, SE, SW, NE and NW. An unsuccessful attempt may activate trap.
