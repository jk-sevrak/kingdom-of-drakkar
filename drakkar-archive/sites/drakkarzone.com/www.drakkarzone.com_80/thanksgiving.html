Thanksgiving in Nork!

The citizens of Nork want to wish you a Happy Thanksgiving
and they extend to you an invitation to join them in their
celebration!
The festivities will begin on Thursday, November 29th at about
8pm Eastern time and will continue uninterrupted until
Sunday, December 2nd at about 10pm Eastern time!
When it was pointed out that these dates actually fall exactly
one week after the normal dates of the holiday as it's
celebrated in the U.S., the residents of Nork replied with
a collective "So What!"
Be sure to make plans to attend! Hunt Turkeys! Eat Cornbread!
Talk with Samoset, Becky and Pilgrims!
Watch Jord squeal in terror as the dread Norkken Turkey
attacks him with his fearful talons!
Be there! It will be fun!
