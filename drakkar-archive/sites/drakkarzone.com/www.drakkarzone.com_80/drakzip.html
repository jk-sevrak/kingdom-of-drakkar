When you click on the link to download Drakkar.zip, save the file somewhere on your computer. Most browsers will ask you if you want to open the file as soon as you've finished downloading it. Do this and extract the files to your Drakkar folder on your computer. It's probably useful for you to find where your Drakkar files are stored on your computer so that you can extract the Drakkar.zip files to the proper directory.

Then, all you have to do is highlight all the files in the Drakkar.zip archive and tell it to extract the files to the drakkar folder on your computer. It will usually work alright if you just overwrite the files in your drakkar folder. However, we have had reports of some odd problems related to some files not being overwritten properly or something of that nature, so it might be useful to delete everything in your Drakkar directory before extracting all the files from the Drakkar.zip archive.

Do not delete the CRTS folder from your Drakkar directory because this folder contains all your character macro files.
