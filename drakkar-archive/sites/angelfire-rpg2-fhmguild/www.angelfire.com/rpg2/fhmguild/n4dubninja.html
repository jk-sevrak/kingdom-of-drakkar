Hardly anyone ever bothers with this lair. Not that the spoils aren't good, but as we all know by now the lair is a pain to get to. But it looks like we're finally going here soon!
As for some general information; the lair is located on M-4. You need several items, as well as 2 grenades or 2 charges of DOOR to get to it. The items needed are a rock armor, a SER, a lightning tanto and a 0/3 sash. See the table below.

Gem needed for access to the lair Item obtained Pic
Golem lair A small purple gem with a small, pulsating light inside Rock armor
Tengu lair A small purple gem with a small piece of stone trapped inside SER
Stunner lair A small purple gem shot through with ray-like fractures Lightning tanto
Single ninja lair A small purple gem flecked with green 0/3 sash

Named ninjas on M-4, either with or without SNR, drop a random purple gem. The lair crits always drop the same gems. A lair golem always drops a gem to the Tengu lair, and vice versa. The same goes for the Stunner/Single ninja lair.

Lair stunners will always drop a lightning tanto and take over an hour to regen. Same for single ninja lair. The SER is a random regen, but the Tengu's only take 5 minutes or so to regen and come in pairs. The rock armor is probably the hardest item to obtain; it's a random regen but the lair golem takes over an hour to regen. You'll have to tan him for his rock armor. You can tell if the golem is tanable by looking at him (#look at golem). It'll mention something about bits of stone, you can't miss it.

When you've obtained all the items you need to get to the double ninja lair, you have to cast DOOR or drop a grenade in 2 spots on M-4. Then you can walk through a portal. The portal will take you to a room with some more portals. One of them will warp you right back, but the other one will give you a vision as to what item you need. I believe the order is always the same; lightning tanto first, then rock armor, then 0/3 sash, then the SER. All items poof upon portalling. After portalling with the SER you end up in the double ninja lair. Click here to see a pic of the lair.
