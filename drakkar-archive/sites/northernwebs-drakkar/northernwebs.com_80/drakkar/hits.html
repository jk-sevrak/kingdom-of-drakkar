Character maximum hits: is the max hits you can buy for your character class..Check out the table below if you're not sure.
Cost per hit: is the cost for your next hit. You can find out what it is by trying to buy hit's with no money down. He will tell you how much it will cost you for one Hit Point.
Result: Is the total of how much it will cost you to max your character.
