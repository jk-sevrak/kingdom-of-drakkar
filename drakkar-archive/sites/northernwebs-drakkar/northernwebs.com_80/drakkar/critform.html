Gear Request Form

Welcome to the MoY Guild, Gear Request form. Please fill out the form below, as completely as possible. If you do not know a particular item, leave it blank. In some cases a gear hunt may be turned down on the basis of you being unable to survive the hunt. In other cases a hunt may be conditionally approved, pending the obtaining of some needed components or gear before hand (such as the case of needing psi cutters before attempting a hunt).

It is critical that all members understand that everyone is trying to advance their characters. If you request a hunt for a piece of gear, please make every effort to be available at the scheduled time. And remember, if the guild hunts for you, its only fair that you help by hunting for your guildmates.

If you are requesting gear from the Nork Scenario, then list your crit as it would appear in Nork. If you are requesting an item from cob, list your crit as it appears in cob.

You can use the form to let us know what your secondary crits may need. However, all secondary crits will recieve a lower priority than main crits. If your main crit is already fully equipped and you're now working on a secondary crit, please say so in the comment field.
