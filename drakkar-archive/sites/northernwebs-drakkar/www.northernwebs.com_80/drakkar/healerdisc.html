Healer Boost Information

     
This page contains an updated list of Healer discs for Drakkar. It also contains Healer boost information at the bottom.

Note: Listed discs that carry the Target Optional message means if you don't specify a target, you will form the disc on yourself.

Example:
Typing "form heal" will heal yourself.
Typing "form heal [target name]" will heal the target selected.

Pick the skill level from the list to the left, or simply scroll to review the entire list.

~Alyx~

CHARM
energy cost: 30
stress factor: None noted
side effect: none
Requires Target
This disc attempts to cause a targeted creature to become an automaton under the Healer's control. Both the chance of success and duration depend upon the skill level of the Healer and the experience level of the creature.
form charm [target] + [action]
Example:
First round command: Form charm [target name]
Second round command: [target name], swap right


Third round command: [target name], get gem In the above example, let's say your target is a Ogre with a hammer AND you manage to charm it on the first try. The first command charms the Ogre, the second command tells the Ogre to swap its hammer to the non-weapon hand, while the third command tells the Ogre to pick up a gem in its weapon hand. Note that "gem" can be changed to bottle, helm, or any other non-combat object.

This disc can be used to disarm, cause crits to attack other crits, or cause crits to drink from bottles (poison, etc) among other things.
