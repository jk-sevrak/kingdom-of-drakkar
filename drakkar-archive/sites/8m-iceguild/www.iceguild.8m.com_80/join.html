Guild Dues

All guild members are asked to donate a small portion of coin to the guild once per week. This due is equal to one hour of coining in any area the member usually coins. There is no minimum and no maximum but it is asked that you do donate as much as you can whenever you can. It is also understood that members need their coin to max/train and we do not ask that you give any unreasonable amounts unless they are truly extra. Dues are collected every Sunday and may be given to Nakago, Lan, Ellowyn or Burt until 12am pacific or 3am eastern. If you cannot donate during Sundays you may ask any of the above to donate for you. Giving your donations to another member to donate for you is done at your own risk and we will not make note of your donation if it is improperly presented.

These dues will be used to max crits as determined their current hps and their max hps for their class. Those closest to maxing their main crit will be at the top of the list, this is the reward for the hard work these people have put into maxing their crits. The dues are split 50%/50% between the council and the regular guild members. That means that if we acquire 3 meg in one week then 1.5 meg will go to one council member and 1.5 meg will go to a guild member. This is in part to the fact that the council donates at least 70% of all funds and also so the council can better donate in the future by maxing their crits and going to Cob to coin. Once the player has maxed their main crit they may choose to go to the bottom of the list and max a secondary crit. Currently, we are working on the last council member. Council member secondary crits are treated the same as all other members' secondary crits.

Secondary crits are required to contribute to the guild too, if you want the guild to help them with maxxing or equipment.

Crime and Punishment

Below is a list of different offenses and their corresponding punishment. Witnesses strengthen the argument of any investigation and Nakago, Ellowyn, or 2 council members determine guilt. Each case will be judged individually, so don't worry if you accidentally kill someone with a fireball when they were hidden or anything like that. It should be noted that no one is above this law, not even me.

Pk'ing: Automatic expulsion from the guild permanently.

Player Theft: All goods must be compensated for if sold or returned if accidentally taken.

Items obviously part of a DP or player exchange will be considered intentional theft.

This also results in automatic expulsion from the guild.

Abandonment: This is willful abandonment of a small crit when in a lair of an eater that results in the death of the crit.

Nodes will not count as willful nor will connection/computer failure.

Minimum sentence of purchase of all hps lost by the eat.

Maximum sentence of guild expulsion.

Kill Stealing: This is defined as waiting for a person to get a lair crit almost dead then entering the lair and delivering the killing blow.

The only sentence for this crime is to pay the victim an amount equal to the experience gained times 2. So if you got 50k from the kill then you pay 100k to the victim. The second offence is expulsion from guild.

Harrassment: Any verbal or physical injury to another player (provoked or not), including but not limited to: Name-calling, debasement of character, rumor spreading.

First offense is a verbal warning and a public apology at a guild meeting; second offense results in expulsion from guild.

Guild Meetings

Guild meetings are held every Saturday starting at 5pm pacific time or 8pm eastern in alt 1 Rat Burrow Tavern. They are not required but are a good way of learning what's new with the guild and meeting any new members. Dues may be collected at this time also but are not required until Sunday at midnight PST. Any questions, concerns, or complaints can be addressed at this time as well.

We also encourage you to join us in Mirc. This is especially important if you wish to join us, then we can get to know you better.. Server 64.57.224.2 Group #ICE
