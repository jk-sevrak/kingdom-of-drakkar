Getting Started in Drakkar

Welcome to an absolutely awesome game. This game can give you years of enjoyment, but can be a little daunting to get started, so here are some recommendations, tips and ideas.

First and foremost - Do the tutorial. There is a ot of useful information in it that is crucial to survival and enjoyment of the game. It is located in the building you first appear in. Go through the door to the west and stand on the gold tiles. Read the Scroll as you do this and practice the things it says. There are 3 more floors of tutorial, so don't stop in first room. You will finish tutorial with fighting your first monster.

~*~

Stats

Try not to get too attached to the first crit you make. Likely you will reroll it not too long after you make it. The usual reasons are poor stats or poor choice in class for you or your crit's stats.

When you reroll, make sure you LOCKER EVERYTHING you want to keep. Don't forget your armour.

Stats are extremely important in this game. The hardest to fix are Luck and Charisma since there are no pots in Nork to do so with. You can fix your other stats with pots, but they are expensive. The following prices are the normal rates at this time (January 2002)

Willpower - 1 to 3 Megs.

Strength - 500K to 1 meg

Intelligence - 150K to 300K

Wisdom - !50K to 300K

Agility - 10K to 40K

Constitution pots up to 15 are 2500 in Steel Flower. Majors are needed to go above that and they  usually run 100K to 150K

You normally would buy these pots from other players, but you may get lucky and find them, especially if you have 18 luck. Usually won't happen till you are big enough to play in N-5.

Note - K = 1000 coins and Meg = 1,000,000 coins

As you can see by this, it is best to roll for Luck, Charisma and Willpower first, then for stat most important for your class.

Ideally, you don't want less than 18 in any of these, but you may have to settle for less in luck and charisma with some races. Only city dwellers get 18 in those 2 stats. These 2 stats affect everything you do in the game, so do try to get the best you can.

Note - You must keep your con at least at 15 at all times. You often lose con points when you die, but if your con is too low, you will also lose hit points and possibly other stats. Your con should be 18 when you rest for maximum Hit point gain.For fighter classes, 18 strength helps too. If you are a ment, you will want your intelligence at 19 and healers need wisdom at 19 for maximum Energy point gain.

Drink yellow pots you find. They give you HP and can be used up to 150 HP. Major con pots will give you extra HP at level 1 too, but are rather expensive. If you are not sure what a pot does, ask someone. There are pots in the game that poison, so be careful.

Do not rest past level 13 until you have maxxed out your hit points at the HP doc. You can buy HP to certain levels, depending on class. If you are a psi class, you will need to max your EP too. This is done by buying EP pots which give you from 1 to 6 points per bottle. They cost 500K. You can use them at any level.

There is more information on how to reroll in the Drakkar help area.

~*~

Gear

The best armour, gear and weapons in this game are tied to your account. This means only your crits can use most of your stuff. Makes PKing less attractive. This means though, that you can't buy decent armour or weapons. You will usually have to find or make them. Armour is generally made by tanning hides of creatures. Notice what creatures need defences against in their environments or from abilities they have. Their hides will generally be good for protection against those.

Once you kill a beast, take the hide to a tanner and type # then the first 3 letters of the tanner's name, a comma and the word tan. eg, #soo, tan. Drop the hide before you hit enter. You must do this quickly as hides rot rather fast. note - the # will not show when you type it. It merely puts you in command mode. You can also just click on the guy with a pen in the command bar, click the tanner's name and then type tan. This is the quicker way to do it, but don't forget to switch back to talk mode.

Note - You cannot take a corpse between alts.

Getting Help

It is okay to ask other players to help you get gear, within reason, but don't beg. That will annoy them. The best method is to simply go to the Steel Flower and ask if anyone minds helping you get gear. If nobody answers, come back later and try again. Do not repeat you request right away and do not randomly choose people you do not know.

This is a highly cooperative game and it is not a good idea to alienate people. Don't forget to thank people if they do help. Most do not expect it, but it makes a nice impression.

Do not be afraid to ask for help retrieving your gear, especially if any of it is special.

Get ICQ. It is the standard messenger in the game and you will not last long without it. Mirc is a good idea too.

~*~

Theft

Don't steal!  Taking things off the ground or counters without permission is theft. This is not a good way to earn your reputation or your livelihood in this game. Once word gets around you are a floor thief, you will never get help getting good gear or retrieving your gear when something kills and strips you.

Some people will kill, strip and pop your gear if they catch you stealing from them too.

~*~

Training

Pay your trainer or you won't gain skill. He is greedy, so be prepared to give him lots of cash. This is where having high Charisma is most useful. It will cost significantly less for each point of char you have.

There are 2 types of training. One is skill training which you do by practicing the skill or weapon. Then you go pay your trainer for that skill. Note - you will not see the percentage rise at the time you pay, but next time you look, if you have practiced, it will be higher.

The other is Class training, which only works at certain intervals when you have learned enough to advance in your chosen profession. You will usually gain some kind of ability when this happens.

Thieves, Healers and Ments skill level is based on class. Fighters, barbs and paladins is based on weapon skill.

ie, A 13/10 healer is level 13 in experience points and level 10 in healing skill.

A 13/10 barb is 13 in experience points and 10 in halberd or whchever weapon he has chosen. Halberd or greatsword the usual.

Because of certain special weapons that are available in the game later on, barbs should skill in halberd and psi classes in staff. Thieves do best with thrown weapons. I would also recommend great sword and long sword for fighters and paladins. Not a bad idea for barbs to skill these too. A nice back up weapon for psi classes is bow for when you run low on ep.

Training in Martial arts up to skill 10 is a good idea for all classes as it helps you avoid being hit and can give extra attacks for fighter classes. Handy if you fumble or lose your weapon too. I also train my crits up to skill 5 in thief. This makes it harder for player thieves to steal from you and you are more likely to see them when they are hiding.

A note on training in thief skills. The best way to do this is to rob all the crits you fight in the dungeons before you kill them.

Healers can injure and heal the same beasties over and over to quickly gain skill in weapon and healing.

Barbs, please read the barbarian safety page.
