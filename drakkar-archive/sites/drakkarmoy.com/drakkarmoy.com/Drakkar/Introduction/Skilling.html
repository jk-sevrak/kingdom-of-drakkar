Skilling your ability

Solo
How much your skill increases is based on a kill per round. so if you are skilling your mentalist you will get more skill using a single target spell and killing slowly, then useing an Area effect spell and killing all at once. If you are skilling your thief skill, all you need to do is try to steal once, then kill.

Group
It doesn't matter if your attacks hit, you are getting skill off any party member kills. so as long as you are trying to use the skill that you want, even if you are "Out of range", you get skill.
As you are in a group the best thing for you to do is stay safe. You get no skill if you are dead. And you are taking skill away from the person who has to res (Cure Critical) you.

Area's
Mostly you are looking for an area that you can survive in, with a steady flow of mobs.
