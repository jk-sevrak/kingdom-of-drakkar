Shadow Wood is a new Master Planned Community  in West Fargo.  It is based on a philosophy referred to as “New Urbanism.”   The concept was derived from the desire to provide a new vision for what neighborhoods used to be.  The primary focus is one of a walkable neighborhood which reduces reliance on automobiles.  It puts focus back on people by building neighborhoods on a more “human” scale. 

You will notice Shadow Wood is a neighborhood built with great care.  At the heart and soul of this community lies thoughtful home positioning, landscaping and careful home design.  Your eyes see front doors and windows that face the street, as opposed to a “wall of garages”.    Shadow Wood is warm and inviting….. so please come in.

Shadow Wood’s land plan focuses on tradition and security.  This is seen through tree-lined streets, clustered greenways, gathering spaces, bicycling paths, ponds and the Sheyenne River.  All this is central to the theme of a unique neighborhood plan. 

If you are looking for something truly special in the streetscape of your life come inquire on how to make Shadow Wood your new neighborhood.
