St. Valentine's Day Event
IF you ever have heard of the story of St. Valentine's, tell it to me someday. hehe.
To me, the day simply means that everyone should take a minute out of the day to acknowledge and give respect to the Love and Companionship of another. This small token will come back to you manyfold. Even barbs have their soft spots.
After receiving a potion from Valentina, trade with someone else to change it's properties. +1 CHR is a nice valentine from someone. Trade with a total stranger or your close friends.


After trading, if you go back to Valentina, repeatively clicking the Heads will give you the message above, However,  if you do it long enough, you will receive a Gem that resembles a human-shaped heart. This will trade for Vampire Gauntlets at the top of the Dark Tower.
