Paladin Island
The Paladin Trainer awaits you on this tiny island between Nork Island and Maeling. He will show you the way of swords and other fighting techniques.
Small N-5 type crits lurk here. Visit the Mausoleum but beware falling into the hole 'lest you become stuck. Best to carry a twig inside this place.
