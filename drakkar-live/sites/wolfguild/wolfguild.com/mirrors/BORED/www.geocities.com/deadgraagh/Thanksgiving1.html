The Thanksgiving Event 1
Thankgiving. A time of peace and rememberance. A time to be grateful for what we have. A time to share our bounty with all. A time to SHOOT TURKEYS! Gobble! Gobble!



+2 Charisma Amulet (Indian Beads) Quest:
Start by talking to Mr. Standish located in the feasting area in front of the Steel Flower. Mr. Standish will tell you to go see Samoset. Samoset is located North of the Prisons along with Sacajawea. Samoset will give you an ear of corn to take back to Mr. Standish. Speaking with Mr. Standish again will allow you to get a Blunderbuss aka Turkey Gun.



The Blunderbuss is classified as a bow weapon. Remember that the Turkey Gun will poof upon being dropped. With your trusty Blunderbuss in hand (beltable), go NorthEast of the Prisons or on the south end of Yeti Island to hunt for a Turkey. Having enough Bow Skill is important if you want to kill a Turkey. When you have scored a prize bird, take it to Samoset. Samoset will pluck the Turkey for you for the dinner. Take the Feathers to Sacajawea and she will trade to you the +3 Charisma Amulet. Amulet will retie to someone else if dropped. Note: This year the Amulet is +3. Last year it was +2. If you have a +2, trade it.

Becky's Cornbread (Temporary OverMax HP/EP) Quest:
Find a lot of White Pearls and take them to Sacajawea North of the Prisons. Sacajawa will trade Corn (not the same as Samoset's Corn) for the White Pearls. Take the Corn to Becky by the Bulletin Boards South of the Steel Flower. Becky will trade the Cornbread for the Corn. Cornbread will temporarily boost HP or EP randomly.

Pilgrim Hat Quest: If you have a CHR Amulet from Events past, you can go to Becky and trade it for a Pilgrim Hat. The Pilgrim Hat is +4 Offense and cuts Psi but it does not have Combat Stun Protection like the PHelm. Still very nice.
