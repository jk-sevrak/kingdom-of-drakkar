The Cobrahn Forest Floor
Map by Genesis__MOY
The Forest Floor of Cobrahn. This is one of my favorite areas to hunt. No psionic attacks, Kobold Mages that Blind, Giants, Rattlers, Egg dropping Spiders, Big Biting Pirahnas and the way of the Flowery Path. Boone will give you +6 HP when you enter Cobrahn but only once. The entrance to the Spectre Lair and the Undead City can be found at the pile of bones in the corner. At the Stump, you can climb up to Tree town. Further North lies Timmy Town and the Tree Ogre Killing Fields which make a tough hitting zoo. East is to the Jungle and Lori Lair.
