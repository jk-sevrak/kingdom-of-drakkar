Homlet
To reach Homlet, you must travel SouthEast from the East gate of Frore. Find the 4 Pillars and follow the river south to a tiny island. There hangs a misty ladder. Climb up to the Homlet Caves and then on to Homlet itself. Skill is fair here and Barbarians do well with Polearms.

A small walled refuge is the town of Homlet. Spartan in nature, you will find your basic needs met here. Hard fighting awaits you beyond the walls. The portal will take you back to Nork. Skill books have been known to drop off the Rockmen of which there are 2 types. One at 11k+ XP, the better one at 28k+ (which is the one that drops those nice books.) Elementals cast AcidBreath so bring protection. Poison is also a problem for all you non-Barb types, hehe.
