Loriapi Lair
Kill Loriapi for her Staff, Ring, Robes, Sash and maybe some Youth Potion. Climb the stairs to go to the Automan's lair. He holds any adventurer's gear that was foolish enough to die at the hands of Loriapi. More than likely, Loriapi will transmute your gear before she TP's it upstairs. Any hits from her Staff while not wearing a Loriapi Robe will take permanent HP from you. Be sure to wear your Ningizidda Helm to protect you. Enter the secret passage leading to a set of locked doors. Wonder whats there?
