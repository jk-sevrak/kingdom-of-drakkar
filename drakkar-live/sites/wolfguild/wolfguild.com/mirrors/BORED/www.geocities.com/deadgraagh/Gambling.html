Gambling Fever
Go to the East end of Nork Island and go into the Casino. There you will meet the Gambler. The Gambler has a simple game for you to play and the reward is  Delicious Coin! Simply throw down your money and pick a number to play from 1 to 10. Click the name of the Gambler and then type Gamble on (your number here). If you lose, your money disappears.  Maximum bet is 500K. For more security, close the Casino doors for private games. Maybe sweep the floor for unwanted invis'd guests hehe. Odds of winning are 1 in 10. Good Luck!
Gamble responsibly.
