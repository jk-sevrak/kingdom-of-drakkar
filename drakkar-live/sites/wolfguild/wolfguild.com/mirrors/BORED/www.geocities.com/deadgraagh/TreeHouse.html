Hemlock /Tree House
Climb up the ladder to get to Hemlock.
After climbing the ladder, Lady Ela awaits you and wonders if you can get Queen Cyresssa's Goblet for her. She will give you her own Goblet in return which will allow you access through the great stump to the Locker and Sellers. If you step on the misty white hex North of the South Seller, you will be transported to a spot just South of Carrena near the SouthEast Caves. Do not put your Cup in your Hemlock Locker and forget it..
