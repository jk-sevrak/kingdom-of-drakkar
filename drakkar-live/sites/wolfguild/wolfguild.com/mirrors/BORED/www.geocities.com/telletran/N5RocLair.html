The Roc Lair and Stores
Back to Nork -5
On Nork -5 in the Haunted area are the Stores and Roc Lair. From the stairs, go east thru the wall and again till you come to a North/South hallway. Go South to get to this area. The Roc Lair is locked, so get a key by trading for it. There is a bank here and a store to sell your gems to. Scroll venders will sell you Succor and other scrolls.
