RatBurrow Quest
The quest for the RatBurrow Sash. The Sash is +2 combat and has limited CritCure capability. You must gather 4 items from N-3.5. The Minotaur's axe, the Dark Guard's axe, Spider Queen's egg and a Sanquin's egg. Talk to the Mayor and he will send you to Mungho. Kill a rat for Mungho and he will reward you with a gem. He will also send you back to the Mayor. The Mayor will send you on a mission to collect the 4 items. Drop all 4 items at the Mayor's feet. Tell the Mayor about each item and he will give you the Sash. The Sash will also let you get in the door to the east of the Mayor's place and to the portal back to the Steel Flower.
