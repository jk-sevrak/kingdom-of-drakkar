Timmy Town
Timmy Town. Named after Timmy of course. That little rascal. You must chase down to tell him to go home in order for you to be atoned. Go to the password girl hidden away in the wall. Trainers and bankers are here to help you. Merton will see to your tailoring needs. See Steve if you die as he will send you to kill a monster and bring it back to get your 2 HP back. Use the express way to get to Tree Town. 2 lockers are here to put your things in. Go west to Timmy Caves and climb down spot.
