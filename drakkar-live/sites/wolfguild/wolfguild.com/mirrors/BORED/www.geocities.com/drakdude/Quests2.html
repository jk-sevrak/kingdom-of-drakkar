Dancer Boots. Find a key on N -5. Find a Wooden Bolt on N -7. Open door with key and trade Wooden Bolt for Dancer Boots. Must be at least 15th level.
Lizard King. Find Sir Halden and say sir, oath. Then find Annalie and say ann, wards. Go to southwest corner of N -2 and stand on hex to teleport to lair. Kill the Lizard King and take Doll and Gold ring. Take Doll to Annalie and say ann, doll. You will receive +1 Con once.
Lighning Hammer. Go to the middle of the Town of Maeling. Hold BNR (Black Ninja Robe) in hand. Trade for Hammer.
Goblin Lord. Find key on N -1. Go to southeast corner of N -1. Open door and kill Goblin Lord. Take key and Purple Gem. Open other locked door. Say Pelagus, proof. Get the ring and go to Genetta. Genetta, pelagus. Get the amulet and go to Hector in Nork. Drop ring and amulet. Say Hector, heirloom. Receive a ring thats 1/1 and level 2 Fire/Ice protection.
Uzi. Go southeast of Frore to hole. Jump down the northeast side of hole. Door in northwest corner, go 6 hexes east then southwest. Kill Lich and take Amulet. Amulet will let you continue to next Lich. Keep killing Liches until you get to Papa Lich. Kill Papa Lich for Uzi. Body of Papa Lich can be carried without Uzi tieing.
+5 Halberd. Go southwest from Frore to the stairs. Go up and enter the lair of the Halberd Giant. Kill the Giant and take his +5 Halberd.
