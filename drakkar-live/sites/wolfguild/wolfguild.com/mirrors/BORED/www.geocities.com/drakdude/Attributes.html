The Attributes
Strength detremines how much damage you inflict when you attack with a weapon and how much weight you can carry before becoming encumbered. Max strength in Nork is 18. Strength can be boosted by: wearing rings, drinking from STR fountain in RatBurrow, berzerking, holding a STR hally and eating Halloween candy.
Intelligence determines how much psionic energy points (EP) a psionic user character initially posseses and how much energy he/she will gain at each experience level. Intelligence is essential for forming psionic disciplines. max intelligence in Nork is 19. Intelligence can be boosted by: wearing rings and eating Halloween candy.
Wisdom measures your character's common sense and is needed to form healing disciplines. Max Wisdom in Nork is 19. Wisdom can be boosted by: wearing rings, holding Slith Healer Staff and eating Halloween candy.
Willpower determines your tenacity and the ability to resist psionic disciplines. Max Willpower in Nork is 18. Willpower can be boosted by: wearing rings and clicking ad.
Agility determines your accuracy to hit and your ability to dodge blows in combat. Agility enhances a Thief's ability to steal, mug and backstab. Max Agility in Nork is 18. Agility can be boosted by: wearing rings, wearing camo cloak, wearing BNR and eating Halloween candy.
Charisma determines how well you get along with others. If you are particulary charismatic, you might get better prices from the shopkeepers and stand a better chance of forming disciplines that influence creatures. Max Charisma in Nork is 18. Charisma can be boosted by: wearing rings and wearing Indian beads from Thanksgiving Event.
Luck will influence the outcome of random events. For instance, the higher your Luck, the better the chances will be of sucessfully forming psionic disciplines or setting/disarming traps. Max Luck in Nork is 18. Luck can be boosted by: wearing rings, wearing Luck Helm, Light ring and wearing Good Tarak Robe.
