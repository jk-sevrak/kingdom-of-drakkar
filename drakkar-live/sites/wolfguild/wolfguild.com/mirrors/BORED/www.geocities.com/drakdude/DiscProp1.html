Discipline Properties 1
Blast (Area Discipline) Psionic Cost: 20 EP

Creates an explosion that burns flesh, shatters small objects and destroys nor-reinforced walls in an 3 by 3 area. Low stress factor.
Absorption   Psionic Cost: 20 EP

Protects against psionic attacks by absorbing psionic energy. You can form this discipline on yourself or others. Low stress factor. Protection Level 2.
Confusion   Psionic Cost: 25

This disciplines attempts to cause a group of creatures to become so enraged that they direct their attacks at random targets, including their companions. The number of creatures affected is based on the skill level of the psionist who forms the disipline and the experience of the target group. In a given group, some members will be affected while others are not. Low stress factor.
Deplete   Psionic Cost: 100 EP

This discipline removes the protection and energy points from the recipient. The amount of energy points taken depends on the skill level of the psionicist that forms the discipline.
Detect   Psionic Cost: 10 EP

Creates a glowing outline around all secret doors within the character's view. You can form this discipline on yourself and others. Low stress factor.
Darkness (Area Discipline)   Psionic Cost: 10 EP

Temporarily creates darkness around the character in a 5 by 5 area. Doesn't work on all creatures as some can see in the dark. Other area disciplines can clear darkness. Low stress factor.

Atone   Psionic Cost: 35 EP

Attempts to draw a character one degree closer to an alignment of true good. Only highly skilled psionicists are able to purify characters, such as Paladins, who want to pursue other professions. The psionicist forming this discipline suffers permanent HP loss as it requires a great deal of concentration to reorient a character's moral compass. Moderate stress factor.
Detox   Psionic Cost: 10 EP

Neutralizes poison. You can form this discipline on yourself or on others. Low stress factor.
CritCure   Psionic Cost: 30 EP

Restores a character to life. The exertion of forming this discipline often damages the healer. Permanent HP damage. High stress factor.
Charm   Psionic Cost: 30 EP

Causes a creature to temporarily operate under the psionicist's control. Click the creature to be controled and then type a function to be performed such as fight orc or take a bottle. Moderate stress factor.
AutoHeal Psionic Cost: 50 EP

Automatically forms the Heal discipline on a character whos current health points are less than one fifth of the maximum health points for that character. High stress factor.
Assault Psionic Cost: 10 EP

Short circuits a creature's brain and nervous system, inflicting considerable damage according to the skill level of the psionicist. Low stress factor.
