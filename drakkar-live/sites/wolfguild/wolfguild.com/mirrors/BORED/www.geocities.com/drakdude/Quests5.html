Mjorin Princess Quest   to get atoned and gain +1 luck.
Buy North Town key.
Go to Orc King's Castle and get a key off of a kill.
Kill Orc King and take armor for later.
Get key for Pumphouse from Gnoll. Find entrance to Gnoll King.
Go up and find key for door or use Door Scroll. Go up and kill Gnoll King.
Take armor and trade for key north of South Town.
Go to Portal Room in Gnoll King dungeon. Trade Orc King armor to King's spy for
key to Portal Room. Jump down hole near South Town. Go east of King in North Town.
Get scroll so Princess will follow. Princess Anne's cell is on 2nd level of Gnoll King dungeon. Use Door Scroll to open wall in Southwest corner. Go North then East through
passage. Kill Archer Orcs behind counters. Open Princess' cell. Go East through bottom
level of Gnoll King dungeon to Portal. Open Portal to King's Throne Room. Simple eh?
Tarak Blast Hammer/Evil Tarak Robe
Kill Slime and take its hammer to the Tarak in the jail in Carrena. Tarak takes hammer and kills you with it. You wake up at the healer with no loss of stats. Go kill Mujaba and take his dagger. Trade dagger to Karzov for Float Bottle on Monkey Island. Go to the whirlpool located in Southwest caves and drink bottle in the shade of the castle. Float up and kill the Tarak for his Blast Hammer and Evil Tarak Robe. Both tie and Evil Tarak Robe can be traded for Good or Neutral Tarak Robes.
Cobrahn HP Shackles Quest: Get shackles for permanent +100 HP.
Kill Kaldor and get his key. Key opens closet east of Kaldor's Lair. Get shackle from prisoner. Go to Tree Town and give shackles to Drizzle for the +100 HP.
To prisoner cell.
Maggio Quest: To shut him up!  hehe
Click heads of Maggio and read what crit to kill by name. Go to Desert Town and find named crit. Go outside town and gather a zoo and drag them in to the named crit. Hang around on crit so zoo eventually kills the crit. Search for ring and hold in right hand and click the heads of Maggio. He swears that he will never gamble again and gives you a Black Pearl. Trade Black Pearl for Helm in Timmy Cave.
Bob Quest: to get 3 Majors.
Start by talking to Bob in Tree Town. He will give you a note to take to a person in Timmy Town. Plans change and that person sends you to another person in Desert Town. The 3rd person takes scroll from you and tells you to go back to Bob for your reward. To reset quest, type bob,reset.
AntiSteve Amulet: So you don't keep losing the same 2 HP over and over.
Go to Desert Town and talk to Murg. Then visit the Psi Tower and kill a Cthon for its Egg. Take the Egg back to Murg and receive the AntiSteve Amulet.
Shalala Quest: To get a miniFate Potion.
Talk to Huddleston in Timmy Town and get a shop number. Travel to Desert Town and tell Shalala the number you were given. Type sha, # to get the bottle.
Cobrahn HP Graagh Halberd Quest: Get Halberd for permanent +100 HP.
Kill Graagh and get his Halberd. Go to lower Graagh Castle and give Halberd to the Duke for the +100 HP.
