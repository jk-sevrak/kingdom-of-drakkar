Alignment
Back to Bits Of Info.
Alignment measures your character's morality. All new characters start with an alignment of Good with Good tendancies. Each non-hostile creature your character kills moves your alignment one step closer to ultimate Evil. Dogs, Deer and Children that are killed drops you -2 levels each.
To check Alignment, type List Stats in Command Mode.
