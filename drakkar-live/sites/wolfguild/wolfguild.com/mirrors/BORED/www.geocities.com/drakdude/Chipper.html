The Chipuda Lair
Upstairs in the caves above the Town of Frore is the Chipuda Lair. Known as Chipper to the locals and is very dangerous, not to be soloed. Chipper is a Mentalist that can cast Acidbreath, Espear and summon a Red Dragon when he gets annoyed. Chipper also will take permanent hits if struck with his staff. You need a power weapon to hit him. Obtain a key to get into the lair by doing the Billy and the Moose quest. Breaking Chipper staff means instant death for anyone around you. Stuns, Sticks and a Ment will make short work of Chipuda properly executed.
