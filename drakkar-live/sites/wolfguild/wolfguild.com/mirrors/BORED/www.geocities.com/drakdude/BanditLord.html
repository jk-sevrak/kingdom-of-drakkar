BanditLord Lair
Gratchit will tell you the password to get into the BanditLord's lair if you say  Gra, promise     Gra,password. You must get a new password if you exit the lair. Gratchit can be found at the end of a very long hallway in the caves to the northwest of the North gate of Carrena.
Kill the BanditLord and get his sword. Darkness can be cast in here. Most NPC's have bow weapons. Tiny will help you if you stand on his hex. BanditLord sword is needed for Evil, Neutral Quest and another one. (hint)
