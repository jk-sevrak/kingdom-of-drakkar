The Healing Touch
A Healer is a mix of Fighters and Mentalists. They use more EP or energy points than Mentalists, so they might run out of energy in a fight. Healers know some of the Mentalist's disciplines as well as some of their own. Healers heal damage of course and can restore a dead corpse to life at higher levels. Healers use their psionic energy as well as weapons such as a staff. Healers main stats are Wisdom, Constitution and Strength. A Mentalist disciplines are of the attacking variety while the Healer disciplines are more of a defensive type.

Dedicate at the Healer Trainer west of the Steel Flower. Healers can heal as well as hurt. Healers get an extra attack every 8 Experience levels.
