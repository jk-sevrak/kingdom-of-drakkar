Cobrahn HP Doctor
In order to use the HP Doc, you must first be enchanted to cross the barrier. Walk East into the deadend to get to the Pillar Room. Run around the room  by standing between the pillars in a clockwise fashion. South - West - North - East then step into the middle of the pillars to arrive outside the building. Continue on to the Doctor.
