Quests 6
Cobrahn HP Ring Quest:  To get +100 HP permanently.
Journey to the Desert Town. Talk to Ionas and get a Ring from him. Travel Southwest to Juntes the Martial Artist. He takes Ring and gives you another Ring to take back to Ionas. Ionas will then take the Ring and will reward you with +100 HP permanently.
