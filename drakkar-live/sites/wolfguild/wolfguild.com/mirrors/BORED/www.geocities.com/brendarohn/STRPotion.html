Strength Potion Trader
Back to Cobrahn.
Find a Silver Nugget that ties to you and bring it to Arminius. He will reward you with a Potion that will increase your Strength by +1. A good place to find Silver Nuggets is in the top of the Timmy Caves. Nuggets are random. Arminius will disappear and you will have to wait for regen if you bring more than one Nugget. Regen is 30 minutes or less.
