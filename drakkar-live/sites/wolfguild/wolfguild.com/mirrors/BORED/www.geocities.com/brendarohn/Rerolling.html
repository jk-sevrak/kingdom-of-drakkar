Rerolling Your Crit
So you decided to reroll your crit eh? Been in the MultiLair one too many times? Been ate by the Twins? Maybe you didn't know any better when you rolled that first crit and want a new one. I dislike losing stats. Rerolling can make an even better character if you click enough times. Remember that Charisma and Luck are stats that have few ways to be enhanced so make sure that they are at 18 as all other stats can be raised by drinking stat potions.

Remember to take off your acquired gear and stow it away in your locker or trade to another of your characters. Any item not in your locker will poof when you reroll. Money left in the bank is safe.
Money.. safe  lol  I made a funny.
When you reroll, half of your experience is transferred to your new character. You will be 1 level less than your last character and all your skill levels will be reset to zero. All money paid in for skill is still paid. Your character will again have to use that skill enough to get back to what it was before and then you will have to start paying on the skill again. No paying for the same amount of skill twice. So Reroll or RR for that perfect crit.
