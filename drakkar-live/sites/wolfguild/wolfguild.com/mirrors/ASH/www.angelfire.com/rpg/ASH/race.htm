Races
 

City Dweller
 

    They are strong and very lucky.  You will find that in drakkar they are the main race used by mostly everyone, they get about average of most stats.  If you are rolling a paladin or barbarian they are a good choice.

Forest Dweller

    Forest Dwellers are agile and extremly charismatic.  As with many of the races they aren't widly used but you do somtimes spot one.  There are more widly used as Thiefs then any other class i can tell.

Mountain Dweller

   Mountain Dwellers are strong and have strong constitutions.   One of the most unpopular races.  They are good for barbarians and somtimes even paladins.

Outcasts
 

 
    Outcasts have great strength but not much luck.  seen rarely in Drakkar nowadays.  Make pretty good barbarians and martial artists i have heard.

Underground Dwellers

    Underground Dwellers are agile and have strong constitutions.  They are probably the most unpopular race in the game.  I have never played one but i'd suspect they would be good thieves.

Woodlands Dwellers

    Woodlands Dwellers are inteligent and have strong willpowers.  Probably the second most common race even tho there numbers are few also.  They make excellent mentalists and exceptional healers.
