Maeling Dungeon: -1

Thanks to Seville's Page for the map and Edge guild for some additional comments.

Green hex is the stairs to the surface. Red hexes take you down to M-2. Fungus bread can be found here occasionally.

You need to have enmis protection here , the tengus will hit a pally for about 20 / turn, so if you get 5 in a room it hurts real bad, If you have enmiss its not to bad just let the lizardmen kill all the crits and then kill the lizardmen :) ,, there is a room to the SW that has some harder npc's and a bit to the NE that has 2-3 M-2 crits. oh and watch out for the trolls they hit hard 100-120 ish.
