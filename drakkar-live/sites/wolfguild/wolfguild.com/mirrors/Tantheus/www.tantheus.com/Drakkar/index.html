Justin S. sends in this tip for updating Alerian g/g e/e n/n quests
hi, your alerian quest information is missing pieces.

before starting either of the g/g e/e n/n quest...you need to go Loranon and say Lor, sever .
you have to break off any existing allegiance or otherwise all the stuff you do will be wasted if you're attached to another allegiance.

also, you're missing something you should do at the beginning of the g/g quest. you need to go to Eldoral (located in town) and say Eld, allegiance. if you do not do this, you're wasting your effort doing anything else.
