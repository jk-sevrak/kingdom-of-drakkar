PSI Cutter Gear:

Referenced : Drakkar forums posted by Ztinktoof

- Direct protection is applied first. Then psicuts. Then class protection.

- Tested in Cob, psicut garments do NOT stack. Thus 2 lori robes do not stack. A lori and a beetle full plate do NOT stack. One garment/armor will stack with a helm, will stack with boots, will stack with a ring.

- Psicuts divide damage by the number of psicutters +1. Thus, one psicutter will divide damage by two. Two psicutters will divide damage by three, etc.

- Psicuts DO affect area discs that are considered psi damage. Thus fire, ice and acid are cut. EC is not affected by psicutters.

- A quick test suggested barbarians take -4 psi damage per level. This is substantial protection in Nork for even small barbs, since it seems to be applied after all other cuts and protection.
