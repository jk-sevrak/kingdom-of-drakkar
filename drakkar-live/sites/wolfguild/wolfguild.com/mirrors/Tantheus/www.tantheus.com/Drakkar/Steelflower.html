Steel Flower Inn:

The Steel Flower Inn ( SF ) is one of the main meeting places for everyone. Here you can use the Nork Lockers, buy Succor scrolls and Instant Healing potions at the hidden Thieves Guild and sell your treasures. You will find items on the ground here, but please ask before taking anything and know before hand that most items are tied to someone already, which means you can't use it or sell it.
