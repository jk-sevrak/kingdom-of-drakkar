Naga Quest Information:

Thanks to Shar's Page

Naga is located near the Prisons area

The Naga is found under the prison, close to the Mino lair.
First you need to find a Statue. Figurines can be found on Tehruh -1 and Prisons.
Then move to the bottom level of the prison and take the NW stairs.
Moving SW in the water you will notice a small cave.
Follow the water way till you can go no farther.
Holding the statue you can now enter the lair of the Naga.
