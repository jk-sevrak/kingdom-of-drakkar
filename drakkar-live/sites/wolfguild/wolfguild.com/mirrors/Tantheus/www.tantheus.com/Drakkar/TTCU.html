Tree Town Climb Up
Climb up to the safety of the Great Tree from the Stump. To the East is the Minor Ogre Field. Follow the Flowery Path to the Spectre Lair and the Log Bridge leading to Boone. Head SouthWest to the SouthWest Caves or go North to Kaldor Castle.
You will need to talk to the man in the Hut during one of the major HP quests. Odie!
