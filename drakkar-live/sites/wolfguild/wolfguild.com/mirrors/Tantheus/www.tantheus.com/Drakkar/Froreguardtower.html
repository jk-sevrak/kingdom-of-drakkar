Frore Guard Tower:

Save the Guard Tower from being overrun by bad guys by helping the local Militia. Head south and go down the stairs to get to the Acid Caves. Heading southeast to get to the Frore Barbarian Trainer. Using Illusion is best but a grenade will do in a pinch. Falling off the edge means climbing the stairs up again. Further beyond the Acid Caves are Evil Nork to the North and Dark Tower to the south.
