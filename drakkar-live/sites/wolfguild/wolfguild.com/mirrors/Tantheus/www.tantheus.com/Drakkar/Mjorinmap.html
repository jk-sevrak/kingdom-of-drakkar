Mjorin Area:

Thanks to Seville's Page

There are 3 levels to Mjorin. One is the area where you first drop in, level 2 is as shown above and level 3 is the Gnoll area leading to to Gnoll King. When you arrive in Mjorin, you are totally stripped. Gold is hard to find. Ments and healers arrive with no disciplines and must relearn them at the trainers. Bow skill is very handy here. The only way to get out is to die normally but there is another way. Entrance to Mjorin is in the Fun House in Nork.
