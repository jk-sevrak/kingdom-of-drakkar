Nork Map: -3.5 Urmak's Boat to Aleria

Thanks to Seville's Page

Drop 1K GP at Urmak's feet and tell him Urmak, pass. He will issue you a pass. Pick it up and hold in hand as you step onto the boat to Carrena. Come Back the same way. I made a macro for this so I don't have to keep typing it in every time.
