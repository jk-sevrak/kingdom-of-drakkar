Maeling Dungeon: -3

Thanks to Seville's Page

Green hex are the stairs that go to M-2. Red hex are stairs that go to M-4. Kill a ninja for a BNR (Black Ninja Robe) and Golems sometimes drop majors.

Recommended Protection needed :
Fire Protection : for protection from Fireball casting Fire Lizards
Infravision : for protection from Darkness casting Golems
Stun Protection : for protection from Stunners
Mentalists can cast Water Illusion and stand in it to be protected from hits, but it can be dispelled by Fireball.
