Healer Gear:

Referenced : Drakkar forums

Here are some of the more easily obtained items a beginning healer might find useful:

Yellow robes are good +4 from timmy0 floor or +5 frpm MrA lair.

Red spec robes might help in psi hvy areas.

Gunt robe, +8 ep regen, until can get loris.

Gold drag armor from surface or psi tower has EC and Fire/ice prot and +1 str (some sources say +7 combat adds too).

Black drag scales from surface or off psi tower basement npc give +2 agil Brown skel leather is +4 and nontying. Ask larger players to watch for one for you.

IV helm until he can get a mama green. Some say nork phelm is not bad until ning helm is gotten.

Steve ammy for basic hit preervation, kaldor ammy for +2 psi skills, or just wear uzi until get other regen items.

Gunt staff gives +2 skill augment until he can get a lori.

Psicrush bracers arent that hard to find in graa castle lvls 0 and 1 until get espear, fs and or combat bracers

Basic floor trash boots and gaunts from timmy0 until can get say, abner boots and juntes gaunts.

Floor combat rings can go up to 5/5, until can get unwanted 8/8s from lair, or psicutter from one of the psi tower floor bosses.

Bubble shield from timmy secret or just basic floor bluegreen sheild until spearman's/Duke can be found in Undead City. Later on titanium, with +100 hits.

+5 curvy longsword together with some LS skill to deal with Timmy0 mace crits and general psi resistance built in to most Cob crits.

Any psi crit that's just achieved Cob size should realized that their fastest skilling will be found in Aleria or Nork for the next several levels, tho the experience gotten in appropriate sized parties in easy or medium cob areas is fairly awesome.
