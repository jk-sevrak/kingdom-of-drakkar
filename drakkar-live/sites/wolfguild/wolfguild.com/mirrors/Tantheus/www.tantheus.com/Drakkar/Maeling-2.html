Maeling Dungeon: -2

Thanks to Seville's Page

Green Hex is the stiars to M -1. Red Hex is the stairs to M -3. Purple hexes are Secret Passage. Break OgreLord Scales here for 1K+ each. Bring Infravision scrolls or disipline to see on M -3.


Edge Guild comments:

Bulk coin to be made here , the pearls can go for up to 13 k , so pick them all up , you can find acid bracers and +3/+3 rings here , also a good place for pots as well .

You have to have some Lightning protection if you can , and some ice protection , Watch out for the mongrels they will blind and the hardest hits come form the Ogre Lords and the ogres with the +3 katanas, the other ogres are easy meat.

I tend to try and make a zoo and let the Kinzo and sanquin kill all the others then kill them , but you have to have good def to do this effectively. Another good starting ploy is to run to the rooms with water in them and then use a range weapon to kill the crits off , this was Kenshins first M-2 strategy and it did me well for a while :)
