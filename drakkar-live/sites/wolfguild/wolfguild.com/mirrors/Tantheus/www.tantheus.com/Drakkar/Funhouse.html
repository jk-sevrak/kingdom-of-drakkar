Fun House: Ground Floor

Thanks to Seville's Page

Note: You must be Level 15 to enter !, have an ivory arrow from N -5 secret area also known as Haunted area and bring 25k gold to buy Key from Jeremy. ** no twig can be set in Funhouse **
With Ivory arrow and 25k go to the NE corner of Nork Town, find the Inn and walk upstairs. If you don't have a Find Friendly scroll or can't cast the spell then you need to bring someone with you. It takes 2 people in the area 3 room below in order to teleport to the next room.

Fun House: Area 1

Buy a key from Jeremy. Step on X tile to continue.
Fun House: Area 2

Step on X tile to continue.

Fun House: Area 3

Cast Find Friendly spell and continue to the west. If you don't have a Find Friendly scroll or can't cast spell then you need to bring someone with you. It takes 2 people in the room in order to teleport to the next room. Before you go, shout to next room to see if anyone is in there or someone must die.
Fun House: Area 4

Step on X tiles to continue. Get out of room fast or one must die.
Fun House: Area 5

Step to the southwest to continue.
Fun House: Area 6

Trade ivory arrow to dog for key to Kaxximillion room.

WARNING * If you die in KAXX room he WILL strip your crit. If your gear lands on special hexes it will be lost forever *

Kill Kaxximillion to get key for final door to continue to the stairs or run past Kaxx if you already have a key.
Stairs on top go to Mjorin. WARNING Going down Majorin stairs will strip you
Stairs in Middle go to Funhouse Store
Stairs on bottom go to Nork.

Fun House: Area 7

Barker: Will embroider items with the words "I survived the Fun House!" for 10K.
Etiyet: Will handle all your banking needs.
Alex: Will sell you things including EP Permanent Potions for 500k.
