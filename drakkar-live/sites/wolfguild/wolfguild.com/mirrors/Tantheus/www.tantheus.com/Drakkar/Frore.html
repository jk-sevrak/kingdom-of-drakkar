Nork Frore Town:

Thanks to Seville's Page

The town of Frore, a walled in city where Red Dragon and the occasional Fire Giant roam. Betty's father Artimus waits for the return of his daughter. Billy has lost his Moose again and it's up to you to lead it home again. The full hospitality of a prosperous town waits to serve you here. Adopt a puppy from the kennels and seek guildance from the Trainers here.
