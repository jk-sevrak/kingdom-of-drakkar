As I changed my gear for this hunt my friend told me that he was getting slapped around quite a bit and needed some help badly. So rushing along I accidentally put on my ring of lesser protection instead of greater protection (I put on a 2/2 ring instead of my
0/5). I rushed there as fast as I could and as the final approach to his lair came to foot a dreaded feeling crossed over me. I knew this fear to be Dragon fear being that the Levi is actually a Dragon Turtle though popular rumors in town say it was a leviathan that attacked their ships. Doping off a few sacks of Instant Healths in a pretty alcove I made a short swim over to where the fight was. The battle was going smoothly for the parties part until the Levi dived under water and swam from sight. Unfortunately this meant the barbarian in the group lost his target and found my head to me a good one and charged me
knocking me prone right into the Leviathan's mouth where he chewed me up and spit out my dead corpse! Revenge was not mine and calling upon Restore I ran back to his lair. I was shocked to see the beast so critically wounded and managed to shoot off a few Energy spears before Gregus struck the final blow. Recouping all my lost gear I realized that my vengeance was not complete and that I would need to become even more powerful still if I expected to receive the cool taste of vengeance any time soon.
