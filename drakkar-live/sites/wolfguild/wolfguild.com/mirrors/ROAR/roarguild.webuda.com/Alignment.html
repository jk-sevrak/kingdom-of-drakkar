Alignment can effect your choise in class, can effect which items you can and cannot wear, aswell as how neutral ncps react to your presence.

How you can lose alignment is simple, you must 1 shot neutral (grey tagged) ncps or players with a disc, 1 shot hitting with a weapon will not effect your alignment.

To fix any loss of alignment, you will require an 1 atone scroll for each step of alignment, atone scrolls can only take you back to G/n.
If you are for example, N/e, you will require 2 atone scroll charges to be g/n.
There are two quests you can use to get back to G/g alignment. Betty in frore and Timmy in Cob.

Atone scrolls are found on the corpse of Minotaurs, in either of the two lairs in nork or the minotaurs in Kingmino dungeons.
