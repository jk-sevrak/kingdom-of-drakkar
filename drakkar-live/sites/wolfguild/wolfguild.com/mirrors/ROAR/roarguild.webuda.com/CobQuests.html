Pillar dance Step in the north hex. then east west hex, then south hex, then east hex, finally the middle hex.
Steve Amulet Go to the desert town, talk to Murg. Visit the psi tower and kill a cthon for its egg, take this egg back to murg to recieve an amulet.
Elance Disc / Elance robe. Collect all 4 amulets from the psi tower, Strength, Agility, Ep, Intelligence, hold them in hand 1 at a time and step on hexes around teleporter on 4th floor untill you "hear a click".
one done with all 4 amulets, step on teleporter hex.
Head west to find elance crit.
Trade robe for elance or keep robe for protection.
Titanium Shield Search Pillars, giant keep till you loot a wooden bolt.
Head to Spectre dungeon (place before going into ud1) head to the southwest corner untill you find igli, trade the bolt to him for a sash.

Take the sash to Sasha in the Northeast corner and trade it for a ring.

Head into the spectre lair to trade the ring for the moonshield.

Take the moonshield to ud2, where you find stairs in a building leading to titanium shield trader, trade the moonshield for the titanium shield.

(lvl 25 req)		 Take a titanium shield and deathsasquatch armor to the trader located above the titanium shield trader.
Guard Replacement
(+100 hp)		 Go to treetown, find drizzle and say replacement. (Driz, Replacement)
Once you get the name of the guard, climb back down, head just north and there will be a guard house, go inside and say replacement is "name you got before" (GuysNameInGuardHouse, Replacement is "Name")
Graagh's hally +100 hp Obtain a Graagh's hally, go to the first floor of graagh's castle,
look for a well in the center, climb down the well. head east.
Go see Duke Moreal, click his name, and click talk button.
Juntes +100 hp Go to the desert village, find Ionas and click his talk button, he will give you a ring for his brother.
Head to juntes who is southwest from the desert village. hold the ring in hand and click him, he will give you another ring inplace for the ring you gave him.
Return to Ionas and hold the ring, click him.
Shackle +100 hp Go to kaldor castle and kill kaldor for a key to the jail cell. once you have the key, open the cell, click the prisonner inside and he will give you a pair of shackles.
Go to drizzle in treetown with the shackle in hand, click him.
Maggio Iv helm Find maggio, click his name and click talk button, see what crit to kill by the name he gives. head to desert town, search for this crit.
Drag a zoo of crits to him and wait for him to die, once dead, search his corpse for a ring.
return to maggio and click him, he gives you an Iv helm which can be padded.
