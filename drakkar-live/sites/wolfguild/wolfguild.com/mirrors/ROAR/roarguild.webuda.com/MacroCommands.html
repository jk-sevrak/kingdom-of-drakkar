Command Function

Party related commands
Party create "name" Allows you to create a party
Party join "name" Allows you to join a created party aslong as you see the creator
Party give "name" Allows you to transfer leadership to another player in party if you are the current leader.
Party break Dispands the party only if you are the creator
Party leave Leaves the current party
Party eject "name" Kicks a player from the party only if you are the creator
Party list Lists the party creator along with all members

Commands related to a target/crit
Attack Attacks your target
Fight Attacks your target
Throw "item" at "name" Throws specified item at a specified target.
Chase "target" Moves you towards your target

Commands related to guilds.
Guild tag "name" Allows you to tag someone into your guild if you have tagging rights
Guild eject "name" Will remove a guild member from the guild if you have tagging rights
Guild display Shows all your guild members in your viewing area
Guild transfer "name" If you have tagging rights, this will transfer those rights to another player

System based commands.
Cmds Places the typed command into the scrolling region as they are being acted upon.
Example: On a mentalist, typing form energyshield will show in the scrolling region as >form energyshield.
Dash At the end of every round, dashes appear in the scrolling region.
Echo Enables or disables character echo. not sure what its used for.
Redraw Tells to game to re-render the game window.
Paint Has the same effect as redraw.
Critsort Enable
Critsort Disable		 Displays crits on your hex at the top of the list always.
Helpful for zerking barbs.
Rest When your health is full, will advance to the next level if you have enough experience.
Also you can recover from damage at a rate directly proportional to your current constitution. This regenerative ability takes effect during both REST commands and normal time-bases regeneration.

Commands related to items.
Look

Look at "name"
Look at "name" "item"		 Looks list items on the current hex

Looks at the target, listing what they are wearing.
Looks at a specific item your target is using/wearing.
Get "item name" Will pick up the item from the ground
Put Puts the item in your hand to where you specified.
Example: Put shield in belt
Drop "item" Drops the specified item
Break "item" Allows you to break the item named if you are holding it
Take "item" Takes a specified item from a specified place.
example: Take gem from sack
Wield "weapon" Takes your weapon out from your belt
Example: Wield stiletto
Draw "weapon" Draws specified weapon
Example: Draw staff
Belt "weapon" Moves specified weapon to your belt
Example: Belt staff
Drink Drinks a Health pot in your sack.
Trade enable Allows you to trade items with other players.
Count "item" Determines the exact number of a givin item currently on the ground below you.

Commands that don't waste a round
*Rtb Right to belt
Weapon in your right hand goes to belt
*Drink Drinks an instant heal
*Btr Belt to right
First weapon in your belt goes to right hand

Ncp related commands
Tan Tells ncp to tan a creatures corpse.
only works if ncp is a tanner.
Example: eld, tan
Make armor Same effect as tan.
Example: eld, make armor
Appraise "item" Asks trader ncp to appraise the specified item.
Example: Darwin, appraise helm.
Deposit Asks ncp to deposit specific aount of coins
Only works if ncp is a banker.
Example: Har, Deposit 50000
Withdraw Asks ncp to withdrawt specific aount of coins
Only works if ncp is a banker.
Example: Har, withdraw 50000
