Quest Name What to do
Progression 1 Kill a Hatchling to obtain his scales. Visit Botanist in the Introduction area
Progression 2 Speak to X in the Escapee area then kill mercenary's untill you collect 10 Merc twigs.
Trade enable and trade them to Lunches that walk around the Escapee area.
Progression 3 Kill golems untill you obtain an essence, Obtain a beholder staff from beholders. Hold both in hand and right click the staff. Get a party to kill Medusa to obtain her gem.
Once you have her gem, Go visit X in the golem area.
Progression 4 aka Gdh2 Gather a party to kill FoodGuardian and collect a Climbing rope twig from him.
When this twig is held in your hand, You can climb up to the next area.
Combat earring Find and kill a hatchling, tan its corpse and pick up the scales.
Find Barnalot in the third area of the green dragon hatcheries to trade for it.
Farmers robe Kill gatherers untill one drops a feather, take the feather and click farmer to trade for a robe.
Vine sash Kill Militia who are red untill you loot a canteen, collect 20 canteens and trade them to Captain for the sash by saying "captain, Help"
Vine shield Visit the official, he will ask you to save 10 lostfarmers, locate lostfarmers and ask them to follow you. after saving 10 of them, he will reward you with the shield.
Improved thermature ring Obtain 10 bear meets from killing cave bears, visit Smokie to the NorthEast of the barbarian fields, give him the 10 meats and he will give you some jerky.
Visit near danfur in green dragon hatcheries 2, with both thermature ring and jerky in hand, click him to get the improved ring.
Marton robe Obtain 1 milky gem and 1 red robe. (both gained by brewing)
Trade both to marton in Gdh2 for the robe.
