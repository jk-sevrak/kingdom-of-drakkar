Quest Name What to do
Akronium Block Collect small ore's and large ore's untill you reach 100lbs of ore.
Jabroon in Nameless town holds onto your ore untill you reach 100lbs then he gives you a block.
Akronium Coins Visit Emerax in Nameless town, bring him a coin mould and a block of akronium to get 100 akronium coins.
Coin molds Head over to Arnes in Nameless town after you have obtained an Apple sapling and Dryad parts.
Progress past level 50 Once you reach level 50, dedicate to the primal quest, obtain 200 Akronium coins and bring them to Emergon on the Nameless land surface.
Once complete you will be able to gain exp untill level 55 in certain areas.
Primal - Progression past level 55 Depending on your alignment, visit Halgroth in graine village if you are good, Primas in nameless town if you are neutral, Necro in homlet if you are evil, to start the primal quest.

When visiting the ncp depending on your alignment, you ask them about primal, then say path to them.
Example: Hal, primal (He tells you a short tale)
Hal, Path (sets you on the primal path)

Obtain the 5 primal ingots, once done you will be able to advance to level 70, skill 34.
Nameless lord Once you have completed the primal quest aswell as reached level 60, you must summon all of the dukes, giving you 600 Nameless land only hp and allowing you to speak to Lord summoner.
Which lets you summon the Nameless lord. Once you have slain the nameless lord, you will be able to advance up to level 75, skill 35.
 
Bandit lord lair access Take an Improved merkon short sword and visit Yomin in Graine town to trade for an Outer door key
Head over to the Ice floes of nameless land, Kill crits untill you find an inner door key
With both keys you can access the BanditLord's lair.
Bandit lord ring Gather a party to slay the Banditlord, search his corpse and take his head, go to Jelterban and trade the head for a ring.
Jelterban requires you to be factioned to trade with him.
 
Enchanted terror blood Head over to Alchemix in the Decayed land caves after you have obtained a terror blood aswell as a Perfect oak, Alchemix will then create an Enchanted terror blood.
Breath capture vials Obtain a Swamp bat wing and an Enchanted terror blood, head over to Kurrashna in Decayed land caves to create a Breath capture vial.
Breath of a titan While holding a breath capture vial in hand, finish off either Albron or a titan
Titan annealed Akronium Obtain an Akronium block and breath capture vial with a breath inside and go see Encorma in the Forges to trade.
 
Defence staff Have a Akronium block enchanted to +6, then visit Jinx in Nameless town who gives you an Akronium ring.
Then go visit Jeblaxin in Homlet to trade for a defence staff.
Upgraded Defence staff Obtain some Volcanic ore and a normal defence staff, then visit Irabob in Graine town to upgrade the defence staff.

Keep access To gain access to keep 1, you need the crest amulet from keep 0, when found take it Almora who resides in homlet.

To gain access to keep 2, you need the crest armor from keep 1, when found take it Almora who resides in homlet.

To gain access to keep 3, you need the crest ring from keep 2, when found take it Almora who resides in homlet.

To gain access to keep 4, you need to be partied with a thief who can mug Almora.
Great hall access First you require to be factioned with either nameless or graine (13000 points)
Talk to the crypt keeper for your faction, he will ask for an Akronium block, some forgotten dust and a dragon heart, deliver these to him and you gain access to the great halls.

Duke marvin Go visit Shimok in Nameless town, he will tell you to Fill the shimok book of pages, Kill Wolves on the lands surface untill you find all pages.
Give shimok the completed book, then head over to the first duke summoner, he will require some impure volcanic ore.
give him the ore and you will be able to summon Duke marvin.
Duke Xuladan Head to Nameless town's maze. Bring 20 Akronium coins per try at the maze which changes each attempt. Locate the judge at the end of the maze.
Return to duke summoner who will then ask for a packet of mandrake root, obtain one and bring it to him and you will be able to summon Duke xuladan.
Duke Merntax Head down to mormar 15 to kill foreman and obtain the mormar book, Seach for a page on each of the 25 levels of mormar to complete the book.
Once completed bring it back to the duke summoner which will allow you to summon Duke merntax.
