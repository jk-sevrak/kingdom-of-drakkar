Tarak Hammer Locate tarak in his prison cell in town, click his name and click the talk button. he'll inform you about a slimebeast

Locate the slimebeasts lair and kill it, loot the corpse for a hammer.

Take the hammer back to tarak in his cell, drop it on his hex and tell him hammer (Tarak, Hammer) he then kill you and escapes.


Go to the jail guard outside the jail, tell him tarak (Guardsname, Tarak)

Go visit the castle and look for Kiara, tell her blessing (Kiara, Blessing)
Then repeat the words she tells you.

Go and slay Mujaba and take his dagger.
Bring the dagger to Karazov on the island south of Mujaba's tent.

Drop the dagger on his hex and say (Kara, Kukri)
He will drop a special levitation pot for each dagger you drop, pickup the potion.

Head to the southern caves and go past the whirlpool and search for a clearing, once under taraks castle, you will see a note saying you are below the castle, drink the levitation pot to float up to his lair.

Once you kill and search him, take his hammer and robe
the robe can be traded to Kiara for either a Neutral or good aligned robe depending on your alignment. say Kiara, bounty to trade. (Kiara, Bounty)
Light ring Locate the goblin king in his lair, slay him and search his corpse for an axe, take his axe and head to the Dwarf mine village and trade the ring with King Borav. Drop the axe on his hex and tell him goblinking. (King, Goblinking)
Poison amulet Go visit the Seldari forest, locate the statues within the forest.

Read all of them by standing on them, head east to Elshea, Drop the light ring on her hex and repeat the words from the statues to recieve the poison amulet.

Tell koron, hello. (Kor, hello)
then tell him mission. (Kor, mission)
