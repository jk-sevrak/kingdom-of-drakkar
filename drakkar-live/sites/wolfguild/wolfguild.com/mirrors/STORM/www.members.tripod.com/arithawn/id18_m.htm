Guild Guidelines


We don't have a whole lot of rules and regulations here really we rely mostly on human character to judge what is right and what is wrong. As with all humans we are not perfect so we do need a few rules and regulations though I hate to admit it. With that in mind here is a list of current rules and regulations for The Guild.


1)Player killing is not tolerated period. We have sysops to deal with those that break the rules of the game we are not vigilantes or a posse.


2)Abusive play in any form is not tolerated .


3)Be respectful of all those around you. The tag you wear reflects on all those in the Storm Family.


4)When it is time for a guild hunt that means guild hunt. Not sorry am coining or I need to do this or that. It means just what it says if you are online you need to attend.(Certain exceptions to this rule for size or availability to be determined by hunt master and guild leaders). There are exceptions to this rule but we need to know if you cannot attend. It is a simple courtesy to all of us.


5)Be courteous,kind and friendly.I would expect everyone to remember what it was like to be smaller or in need. Please find time to help others. (Unless they fail Rule #11) Honestly i wanted to point some examples out here but I am sure we all know who fails and passes this rule.


6)Warnings for improper play or abusive play can be given and with repeated instances will be cause for removal. This guild has a good solid reputation and there is never an exception to rude or abusive play. We are adults here use your head.


7)New prospects if referred by a guild-mate should be directed to Fin or myself so we can set a time to meet and hunt a bit to see abit about them and them about us.


8)Please do not give out ICQ numbers of guild members or anyone else for that matter without asking first.


9)Currently we are asking for new applicants to be in cob or almost to Cob most of us hunt in cob and are rarely in nork there are however exceptions to this based on each individual person. Don't assume it is No without at least trying we are pretty flexible. New Applicants should contact a current member and have them get in touch with the guild leaders, New Member Coordinator or the hunt master's.


10) DO NOT ask for new membership if all you are after is gear. We are not here to serve the gear needs of any one crit we are here because we love this game and love to hunt and learn new things and eventually gear up along the way.


11)Talas has a no-F#$k-Face rule if this is you don't even bother. We all adhere to the rule and have grown to love it. (It Keeps us from beating the Tar out of some people).

12)Jester's Rule: We Zerk therefore We Are!!

13) Smack 'em hard, Smack 'em often and Dance on their bloody corpses...(Really not much more needs said here )


14)Last but probably the most important rule this guild has: HAVE FUN!!!!

STORM Guild Chat Room Guidelines and Rules

These rules will be adhered to by all guild mates and guests while in the STORM public chat room and will be enforced by all guild mates.

Foul language will be kept at a minimum.(OK so this one is a little flexible. Lord knows i am ringleader of the docker worker for kids 101.)  We have children in our chat room that do not need to hear foul language in every other post made.


With the number of people in our chat, guild mates & guests alike, there is a lot of room for misunderstandings and bruised feelings. If a guild mate asks somebody to stop something, i.e. name calling, arguing, suggestive comments etc. it should be stopped immediately. If you have a problem with the request, go to side chat and discuss.


Arguments will not be conducted in the public chat room. If you have a problem with somebody in the room, go to side chat to discuss.



We are Looking for a few fresh faces to add to the Storm Family. See rule # 11 if that is not you feel free to contact Fin, Talas or Myself.
