Kingdom of Drakkar Forum Discussion - "Past Lives" Part 2
==========================================================

Thread Overview
---------------
This forum thread discusses memories of Legends of Kesmai (LoK) and Isle of Kesmai
(IoK), predecessor games to The Kingdom of Drakkar (KoD).

Game Mechanics & Exploits
--------------------------
Players debated moonstone rings, HP gain mechanics, and various bugs. A significant
exploit involved a duplication hex in Leng Hell that GM-only areas contained for
legitimate item creation. When players discovered access to this area, some flooded
the market with rare items before permanent lockouts were issued.

Notable Characters
------------------
- Ironuzak (player Brem): Early famous Martial Artist who discovered Iron Lich Boots
  with a wizard character. Known as an independent player outside guild politics.
- Mord: Described as the first Ninja (MA/Thief hybrid), never joining any guild
- Frugle: Exploiter who created a webpage bragging about accessing restricted areas

Game History
------------
Toasticle asked about player permanence in Axe Glacier to prevent server crashes.
Ztinktoof explained a "xeroxing bug" requiring the last player to exit to trigger
it -- a long-standing exploit that took over a year of player reports before
developers addressed it.

Creator's Statement - Brad Lineberger
---------------------------------------
Brad (KoD Creator) clarified: "I am the sole creator of Realm which became
Drakkar...The game was developed on a PDP11, and later ported to unix."

He distinguished KoD from IoK, noting superficial similarities but fundamentally
different development paths, influenced by Rogue, Moria, and IoK.
