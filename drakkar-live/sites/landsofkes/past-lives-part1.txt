Kingdom of Drakkar Forum Discussion - "Past Lives" Part 1
==========================================================

Main Topic
----------
A player named purg2 initiated a discussion seeking information about the historical
versions of Kingdom of Drakkar to document the game's evolution on a webpage.

Island of Kesmai (IOK) Background
----------------------------------
- Originated on CompuServe in the early-to-mid 1980s as "Dungeons of Kesmai"
- Evolved through versions: Island of Kesmai -> Legends of Kesmai (mid-90s)
- Featured text-based ASCII maps with symbols representing game elements
- Eventually closed when EA acquired GameStorm in 1999

Kesmai Game Features
--------------------
- Five character classes: Paladins, Thaumaturges, Wizards, Thieves, and Martial Artists
- Complex spell system requiring chanting before casting
- Demon summoning mechanics with seven demons (only six summonable)
- Advanced Segment (AG) as end-game content
- Resurrection system with underworld quests
- Guild-based secrecy regarding tactics and rare items

Drakkar's Relationship to IOK
-------------------------------
Contributors noted that while not directly related, Drakkar was created by former IOK
programmers. Brad (identified as having played IOK under names like "Soyer" and "Cure")
is credited as Drakkar's primary creator.

Notable Differences Between Games
-----------------------------------
- Round time: IOK (15 seconds) vs. Drakkar (5 seconds)
- Screen view: IOK (3x3) vs. Drakkar (4x4)
- Map scale: IOK (~80x80 or 100x100) vs. Drakkar (200x200 hexes across multiple layers)

The thread demonstrates extensive nostalgia among veteran players sharing memories
spanning decades.
