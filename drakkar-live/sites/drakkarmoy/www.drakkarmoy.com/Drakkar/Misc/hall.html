Guild Halls

Basic Guild Halls for $4.99 contain.
  • Trainer up to skill 20, with a 10% discount. Trainer will also tell you what percentage you have paid up to. Also you can use the adds to give you an additional discount. i.e. Adds enabled + adds clicked + hall discount = 30% discount.
    (No barb or Paladin trainer though).
  • 3 Locker Accesses (Nork, a thief locker, and guild hall locker)
  • Portals - (Nork, Maeling, VT, Nork-4, and Maeling-1).
  • Minor Con pot per day. (Max 15, 30K per pot and drink in fountain)
  • A person who will tie non-tied items to your account.
  • A Minor EP (100 eps) Restore potion free per day. (Tied to your account)
  • A Twig Holder. Who will hold one twig while you adventure around, die get striped and when you get back will give it to you.
  • Stores and Bank here are cheaper than the Thief store in Nork Locker area or Sash Store in Rat town

Deluxe Guild Halls for $7.49 contain.

Since this is upstairs from the Basic Guild Hall. It has everying mentioned above plus.

  • Trainers up to skill 35, with a 20% discount. (still no Barb or Paladin trainer though). You can use the adds to give you added discounts. i.e. Adds enabled + adds clicked + hall discount = 40% discount.
  • 4 More Lockers (Aleria, VT, +2 new ones).
  • Portals - (Frore, Nork-6, Evil Nork, King Mino -2, and Aleria) < Note, if using this portal your twigs are still active and good>.
  • 1 Zap free per day. (Tied to your account).
  • 1 Youth Pot per Month for 150K.
  • 1 Minor Con pot per day (Max 15, cost 20K and drink in fountain).
  • 1 Con pot per Day (Max 18, cost 45K and drink in fountain).
  • 1 More Twig Holder.
  • Store and Bank here are cheaper than in the Basic Guild Hall.

-Short Note-
Guild Halls are enabled for one character, not your whole account. If you only play one main character and several minor ones, then only the one character you choose to get access to the guild hall may enter. Unless you buy the guild hall for your other character's also. The cost is billed initially when you sign up. and then it is added to your monthly bill from MPGN.
