ReRolling?

When ReRolling you should put all the items you wish to save into your locker, and all your money into the bank. When you ReRoll you will loose 1/2 your EXP, and you will loose your skill. but your skill is still paid for up to one level below where you were at. If you accept the roll you have 1/2 your exp, and a 0 skill till you skill. Your aligment is reset to good/good. All the quests you have done that are one time quests, you still have done (means you cant do the lizard king quest again). If you reroll again you will have no skill unless you practiced in that skill you had.
