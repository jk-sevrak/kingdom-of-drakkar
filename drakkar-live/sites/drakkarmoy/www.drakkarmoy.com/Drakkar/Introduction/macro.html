How to write a Macro:
I would put general instuctions on how to write a macro in here..scroll down to the empty slot and click on it, hit ok. A new screen will pop up ect....

General:
To have an attack macro that is throwing
throw right at also click target lock

To picking up coins, but i add a third command which is invalid so i know the coins are in my sack for example i have a macro which reads as
take coins;put coins in sack;i love beer also click auto return
what this does is in combat message screen i get the message invalid command,please rephrase, so i have a visible message the coins HAVE been placed in my sack (submitted by Termin)

To help keep you alive there is a nice little one where you stand next to a door and click button.. say you stand above a door to the south and you need to hide..
S S ; close N also click auto return
This will move you south into the door, south on the other side of the door and then close the door with the monsters on the other side. Really good way to keep yourself alive. This could be made up into 4 macros.. one for each direction. (submitted by Genesis)
