Lizard King, Nork -2


      Talk to Lord Talbert in Nork Town and tell him oath. Then talk to Annalie and tell her Wards. Then travel down to the sw corner of -2 and stand on the square and you will be teleported into the Lair. In the lair you kill the Lizard king and take his doll. You can also take his golden ring which is a level 4 fire/ice ring. You take the doll to Annalie and tell her doll. You get a +1 con point the first time you do the quest
