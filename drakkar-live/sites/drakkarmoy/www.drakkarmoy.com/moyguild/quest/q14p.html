Levi

This is an easy quest. Very short and fairly easy with a good party. The Levi is a large turtle found SW of the west gate in Frore. When you hit a wall follow it along till you come to an opening south. This is the Levi Lair. Kill her and take her egg. Take the egg to the man holding the Full Plate Armor east of Frore. Hold the egg in your right hand, click on his name then click on the talk button. You now have Full Plate. Warning: you must be level 15 to do this quest.
