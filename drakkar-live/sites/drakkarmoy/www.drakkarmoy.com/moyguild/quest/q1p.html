Goblin Lord



      You need to get a key off a dead Goblin, it is a random treasure in Nork -1. You go to the South East area of Nork -1 and open the locked door with key. You kill the Goblin Lord, and take his key and purple gem. You take the purple gem to the other locked door in the room and show it to Pelagus and tell him proof. He will give you a ring. Take the ring and the purple gem to gennetta and tell her Pelagus, she will give you an amulet. Take ring and amulet to Hector. Drop them on him and tell him to make "heirloom" and he will give you a 1/1 ring with level 2 fire and ice protect.
