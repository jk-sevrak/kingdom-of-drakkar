Baskie, Valcano Town



Talk to Kuron in the basement of VT and tell him "Kuron, secret". He will tell you what creature he wants. You kill that creature and bring it back and tell him "Kuron, Secret". He will then tell you to find the lair. You can now enter the Lair. You kill the beast then tan him and take the armor to the man holding the Baskie Sheild. Click on him, then click on the talk button and he will give you A Baskie Sheild.
