Muzi, King Mino -2

To do this quest you need 2 Silver Bows from the VT Fishery archers, 1 Door Pearl from Nork -3.5 Ghost and the key from Maeling -2. To get the bows and pearl you just kill the monsters holding them. To get the key you have to trade the NPC a Perfect Black Pearl (mostly found on m-3). On KM-2 you will find the trader "PeeWee". First you hold the pearl in your right hand, click on him then click the talk button 5 times. You can now put the pearl away. Drop a silver bow and type "Pee, Trade". Repeat this step once more and you will get the +2 Muzi.
