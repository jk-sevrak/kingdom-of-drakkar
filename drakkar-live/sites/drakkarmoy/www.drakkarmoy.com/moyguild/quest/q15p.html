Betty, Frore

Betty is found West of Frore. You have to blow some walls behind the Frore Mino to find her. There are lots of Harpies and Skeletons around to kill this 1 hp little red headed girl. You hold the doll in your hand and she will follow you anywhere (including her death). Her father is in the West end of Frore. When you get her to Art (her father) tell him "Art, Betty". You will get atoned (up to Good/Good) and you will get some random pots (including 1 zap and 2 20K lair diamonds).
