Dancers, Nork -7

To start this quest you hunt the Nork -5 Secret Area. There you will find a key. The key is used to open the door to the dancer seller. On -7 you can find bolts. Take a bolt and hold it in your right hand while standing on the dancer seller. Click on his name, then on the talk button. Warning: you must be level 15 to do this quest.
