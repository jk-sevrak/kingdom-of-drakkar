Lightening Hammer, Maeling Town

This quest is started when you kill a BNR ninja in Maeling -3. There is a man in the center of Maeling Town holding a Lightening Hammer. He will trade it for the BNR. Hold the BNR in your right hand, while standing on the Trader. Click on his name, then on the talk button.
