Poison Bow, Nork -5

To start this quest you need to get an arrow from the -5 haunted area, it is a Random treasure there. You trade the arrow for the key to the Roc lair. The man to trade with is just inside the secret area. The Roc Lair is also just south of the man holding the key . You kill the Roc and take her egg. Then go back to the haunted area and put the egg in your hand. Click on the man, then click on the talk button and he will give you the Poison Bow. Warning: you must be level 13 to do this quest.
