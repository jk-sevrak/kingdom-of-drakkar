Armor Stats

This chart was made by going down to -4, wearing nothing but each type of armor, The character was a 13 level Fighter, with no MA skills, or any other blocking ability. Letting a thumper take 100 swings. So the lower on the chart you go, the better the armor is for physical protection.

Hits is the Percent of times the character was hit.
Blocks is how often the armor blocked the Thumpers attack.
Misses is the percent of misses
Deflects is the percent of Deflects.
Misc is the Fire, Ice, and other Protection the armor might have.
