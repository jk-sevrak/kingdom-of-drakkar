## Original Post

The poster shared a link to a "Game Archaeologist" article about Kingdom of Drakkar
and added personal recollections:

- They played the game "way before UO or EQ ... probably in 1991 when the game was
  first hosted in MPG-NET."
- They described it as "a very much hack-n-slash loot game with NO animation!!"
- They pointed out two inaccuracies in the article, noting that the cost of playing
  Drakkar on MPG-net was "$2 per hour, NOT $3-$5."
- They recalled that the best gear, like "the chipper staff for the mentalist,"
  dropped from named mobs, though there was no camping issue because there weren't
  enough players to need to take turns.
- They also noted that "$2 an hour was *cheap* then ... GENie was $6 per hour," and
  recalled using GENie for forum discussions.
