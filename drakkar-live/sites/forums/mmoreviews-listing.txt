# Kingdom of Drakkar - MMO Reviews Listing

## Game Overview
"Kingdom of Drakkar is a 2D traditional fantasy MMO featuring dungeons, cities, and an extremely vast game area" developed by Drakkar for Windows. The game is free-to-play with optional subscription choices.

## Setting & Lore
The game's backstory involves "an ancient evil released long ago that consumed the Earth, leaving it flooded and cultures segregated." Four primary cities exist: Nork, Volcano Town, Province of Maeling, and the City-State of Frore, connected by portals.

## Game World
The Kingdom spans "over 250,000 hexes of playing area," with diverse terrain including ice fields, deserts, volcanoes, and oceans. The review notes that "New areas, game items, and quests are constantly being developed."

## Playable Races
Six races are available: City Dwellers, Forest Dwellers, Mountain Dwellers, Outcasts, Underground Dwellers, and Woodlands Dwellers—each with distinct stat advantages.

## Classes
Seven character classes are offered: Fighter/Paladin, Barbarian, Martial Artist, Mentalist, Healer, Thief, and Fighter/Mentalist.

## Rating
The game received a 2.80 out of 5 stars based on 5 votes on the review site.

## System Requirements
Original specs listed: "486DX/66; SVGA; 8MB RAM/40MB hard disk space"
