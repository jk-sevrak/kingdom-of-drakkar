


==Information==
[[File:Mormar Stairs.png|thumb|228x228px]]

A 25 level dungeon with the [[Rift Guardian]] at the bottom (26th floor).

Each level drops more gold, has higher value gems, and increases experience yields. The monsters grow more and more difficult as you progress.

Lower Tiers and Nork Stat Pots are abundant here.

==How to Access or Get to==
[[File:Mormar Access.png|thumb|229x229px]]
Accessed through the [[Decaying Lands Bog]], of the [[Nameless Lands]]. You'll find 4 rocks surrounding an empty space. '''Climb down''' to enter.

Requires completing the [[Mormar Entry]] Quest to access consecutive floors.
