# Martial Artist Class Guide
Source: https://areyekuwe.wordpress.com/2015/11/11/martial-artist-class-guide/
Date: November 11, 2015

This comprehensive guide for Kingdom of Drakkar's Martial Artist class was authored by Snake-Eyes and originally published on the MoY Guild website around 2000.

## Core Class Information

**Special Attacks by Skill Level:**
The guide details progression from skill 6 (Jumpkick) through skill 29 (Deathtouch). Key abilities include Sweep at skill 8, Chi Sweep at 17, Shield at 23, and Power Sweep at 24.

## Equipment Recommendations

**Armor:** MAs perform best in Griffin or Bruce Griffin armor, which provide fire/ice protection. Black Ninja Robes are highly valued for their "+2 agility" bonus per robe worn.

**Gauntlets:** The guide ranks equipment damage potential, listing Red Dragon gauntlets as beginner options, progressing to Alerian gripclaws, Katsumo gauntlets, and Vampire Gripclaws as top-tier choices. "The Kings Quest Vampire Gripclaws are totally badass grips" offering excellent damage and blocking capabilities.

## Party Hunting Tips

Essential guidance includes positioning beside teammates rather than on their hex, maintaining healer proximity, and prioritizing spell-casting NPCs as targets.

## Advanced Hunting Strategies

The guide recommends soloing Griffins for skill development and identifies optimal farming locations including double griffin lairs, Minotaurs, and high-level zones like KM4 and KM5. It emphasizes wearing proper attire for each location and maintaining full healing status during dangerous encounters.
