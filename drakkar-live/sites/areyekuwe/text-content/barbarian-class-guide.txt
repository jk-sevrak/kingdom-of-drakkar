# Barbarian Class Guide - Drakkar
Source: https://areyekuwe.wordpress.com/2015/11/11/barbarian-class-guide/
Date: November 11, 2015

## Overview
This guide, authored by Thor from the MoY Guild (circa 2000), details why players might choose a Barbarian character in the Kingdom of Drakkar MMORPG.

## Top 10 Reasons to Play a Barbarian

**Combat Advantages:**
- Highest natural hit points (580 at base)
- Four extra attacks at full berserk level 20
- Additional hit points gained when entering berserk at level 18
- Extra strength point per berserk level starting at level 16
- Extra attack per round at level 21

**Defensive Benefits:**
- Natural resistance to psionic disciplines like poison and lightning
- Less gear-dependent for surviving different areas
- Party value: barbarians are sought after to absorb damage

**Versatility:**
- Can equip evil-aligned gear unavailable to other classes
- Capable of solo hunting in certain areas
- Can heal with full hands via "autobalm"

**Gameplay:**
- Considered easiest class to play
- Unique skill progression tied to experience gains

## Berserk Mechanic

The guide emphasizes berserk as central to barbarian gameplay, progressing through four levels indicated by messages like "You are agitated" through "You are frothing at the mouth."

**Key Training Note:** "Practice with berserk once you've reached exp level 5 or so in areas where there are few players."

## Autobalm System

Autobalm triggers automatically when berserked and out of control, healing at one-third health or below, even with weapons equipped.

## Critical Warnings

The guide strongly cautions against solo hunting dangerous lair creatures like Red Dragons or Griffons, as death results in permanent experience and skill loss ("being eaten").

## Character Development

Recommended high attributes: Strength, Constitution, Will, Luck, and Charisma. The first three improve through potions; the latter two are rarely available.
