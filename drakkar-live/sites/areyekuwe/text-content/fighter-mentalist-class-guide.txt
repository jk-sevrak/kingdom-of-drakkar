# Fighter/Mentalist Class Guide
Source: https://areyekuwe.wordpress.com/2015/11/11/fighter-mentalist-class-guide/
Date: November 11, 2015

This guide, authored by Genesis of the MoY Guild (circa 2000), provides foundational information about the Fighter/Mentalist class in Kingdom of Drakkar.

## Location and Dedication

The Fighter/Mentalist trainer is located "South West of Frore" in an isolated area. Players can reach this trainer by climbing toward hill giants, then jumping off a cliff south of the stairs (requiring a succor twig to escape). Mentalists can alternatively use illusion magic to traverse water barriers.

## Prerequisites and Warnings

The guide strongly cautions against dedicating to this hybrid class early in gameplay. Instead, the author recommends: "you need the power one way or the other" through extensive leveling as either a pure Mentalist or high-level Paladin with multiple specializations first.

## Class Limitations

Once dedicated to Fighter/Mentalist, players face significant trade-offs compared to pure class specialization:

- "Fighter Mentalist skill gain in Mentalism about half as fast as normal"
- Players cannot access all Mentalist spells
- Weapon specialization becomes unavailable
- Advancement in either discipline becomes slower

## Conversion Paths

Players may dedicate from Merchant, Mentalist, or Fighter classes. However, "you cannot become a Barbarian or Paladin once you dedicate to Fighter / Mentalist," creating permanent restrictions on future class choices.
