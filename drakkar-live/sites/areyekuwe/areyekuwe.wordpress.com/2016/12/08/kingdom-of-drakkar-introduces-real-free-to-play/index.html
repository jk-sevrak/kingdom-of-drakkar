Kingdom of Drakkar introduces real free to play

The e-mail that landed in many people’s inboxes on 12/07/2016:

The biggest key point to these news is that formerly pay-per-play accounts that could not go back to free-to-play now can

COME PLAY DRAKKAR WITHOUT A PAID SUBSCRIPTION

Your level character wants to tell you about new happenings in the lands of the Drakkar!

Major Update to Subscription Status
The Aleria and Cobrahn expansions are now available to FREE PLAYERS!
Previous subscribers may now access Nork,Aleria and Cobrahn without repurchasing a paid subscription!
No more play lockouts with reverting to FREE status (when not carrying a balance)
Nameless land subscribers will have launch time access to the new Invader’s Refuge Scenario
Additional Game Improvements Include
Level advancedment up to level 100
A new classification of quirks
A new ability tree, “Arcane Abilities”
Game with your higher level friends through the new SIDEKICK party style (coming soon)
