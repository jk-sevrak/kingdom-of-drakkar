**Main Content:**

"Cobrahn (formerly known as The Pit) is a scenario that can be accessed upon reaching level 18."

The page explains that a portal behind the Slicer lair in N-7 provides entry to the forest. Death penalties in standard play are minimal: "you lose 2 hp" upon dying.

To access Crazy Cob, players should "go to Timmy Town and tell any blue npc `xxx, gocrazy`." This variant provides enhanced progression: "XP and Skill gains are doubled here."
