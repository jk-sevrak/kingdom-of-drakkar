**Main Content:**

"Nork is the scenario you start in. It has the school, lots of towns, deep dungeons, and plenty of world to explore."

The page describes that Nork features a portal room connecting to the Guild Hall and an enhanced Alt 2 version. Holiday events occur in Alt 2 Nork through paid subscription.

A challenging "Primal" Nork variant appears periodically as difficult endgame content where gear resets and items decay over time with randomized statistics.

**Additional Information:**
