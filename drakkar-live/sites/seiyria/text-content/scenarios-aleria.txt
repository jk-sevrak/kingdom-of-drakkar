**Main Content:**

"Aleria is a scenario that can be accessed at any level, provided you have Upper Guild Hall or a Ratburrow Sash."

The page explains that Urmak the Boatman, located north of Ratburrow town in N3.5, trades 1,000 gold for a boat pass to reach Aleria.

Key features noted include: alignment-based mechanics, expedited leveling toward level 18, and "Alerian Rare" items as collectibles. Common farming targets mentioned are Duke Talinar, the Seldari Queen and her lair, and Koss.

To access Crazy Aleria, players should visit Kiara and issue the command "kia, gocrazy" to gain primal skill.

The document warns: "Beware the raids - sometimes the Duchy of Carrena will get raided by hundreds of goblins, ogres, etc."
