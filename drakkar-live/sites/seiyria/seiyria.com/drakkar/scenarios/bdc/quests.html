    Crypt Access [Back to top]

    To activate the Archaeologist quest, you must first do the Trapper climbup in GDH2. Talk with him in the BDC forest and kill the 4 spectres in BDC castle. Take their statues to the Archaeologist to go down to the crypt.

    Hermit Skill Progression [Back to top]

    Kill the Hermit in the BDC forest and take his scroll (read with right click) to advance to skill 39.

    Tengu Skill Progression [Back to top]

    Gather 10 Tengu Glands and take them to Caniblas. Drink the Ink Pot to advance to skill 39.
