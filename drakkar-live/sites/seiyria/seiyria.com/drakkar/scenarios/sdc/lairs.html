    Corosonous [Back to top]

    Found At: Lava City

    Finish Dark Knowledge before doing this fight. Breaks bottles. Bring Acid prot (2 AcidronAlcor robes). Can choose a Broom at the end of the fight, or improving any of the 3 SDC quirks (Tree Hugger, Air Head, Cavern Creeper). Drops random Dark Knowledge Pages (1-35), 4 random stat flowers and 4 Essence Twigs.

    Drops:
    Broom Dark Knowledge Page

    OzonousMax [Back to top]

    Found At: Lava City

    Sucks stats. Breaks bottles. Mean lightning attack. Drops random Dark Knowledge Pages (1-35), 4 random stat flowers, and 4 Essence Twigs.

    Drops:
    Dark Knowledge Page Ozonous Armor Ozonous Bracers (Armor) Ozonous Bracers (Regeneration) Ozonous Bracers (Strike)
