Cobrahn Haunts lvl 1

There are two different areas to the haunts , the top part which we have mapped is not to bad , Stunners , apparitions, fiends etc 

Its good xp and not bad coin, you get to her by climbing down at the pile of bones in the swamp west of Juntes hut.

The lower part , we have not fully mapped , is very nasty , stunning NPC's and wraiths and wandering specters , plus there seem to be crits we have not seen yet , we found a lich archer at one stage.

There are step's here up to the Top level , I think this may be the way to Arun's Ruins ( you can see the steps on this map in between the two secret doors far south of map. They steps go up to a locked door.  ( picture )
