Getting started in Mjorin

If you are not going over with someone who has the cash build up for you to by gear hear is what I think is the best way to get started.

  • You will appear to the east of south town, first step to make it safe, is to set your healer recall spot so you don’t die and go back to Nork. Run to west and a little bit north. Inside the town you will find a healer trainer. Stand on the healer and say his "name,recall" ie ,, john,recall. This will make it so that if you die you will not be shot back to Nork.
  • Ok now for some gear and cash,, you need to get to north town first and see if the door is open, if it is set the healer there as your recall spot. If it is locked up you will need to get 10 gp by selling a long sword to by the key and get in. After you have done this you will probably need to rest.
  • TIP : create a macro drink;drink;drink and stand in the water just at the end of the road into north town you will get 6 hps / turn this way
  • Now you will need to head to the prisons and open a few doors , the idea is to get the prisoners to fight the guards. You can then pick up some basic gear .
  • TIP : some of the spear use short sword skill , look at them on the ground you can tell

Pick up as many shields as you can this is the basic way to get cash

  • Now head off to the LS trader and trade the shields for a LS ,, you can sell the LS for 10 gp each
  • After you get a little bit of gold you can them start buying better armor from south town and some healing pots,, then its time to go of looking for purple gems ……

 

Making Cash

Look in the Denaba caves for purple gems , the crits will fight each other and there will gems just lying on the ground. Take the gems to the trader with the red gem and trade for it. Take the red gem to the armor trader and trade for this ,, u can then sell this for 250 gp.

If you cant get into the Denaba caves yet you can collect the shields and trade them for long swords that you can sell for 10 gp

Other than this most items in the north map sell for 1 gp , some of the spears sell for 2 gp
