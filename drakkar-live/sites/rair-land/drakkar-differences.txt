Drakkar Differences: Land of the Rair
=======================================

How Land of the Rair diverges from Kingdom of Drakkar through numerous mechanical
and interface improvements:

User Interface & Quality of Life
---------------------------------
The game features "an entirely rearrangeable (sometimes resizable)" UI that saves
positions automatically. Message logging includes filters for environmental, chat,
and combat messages, while visual indicators show gear damage status and gem
encrustation details.

Combat & Status Effects
------------------------
An active target display shows "accurate percentage of their HP, an HP bar, their
alignment towards you and their direction, and what buffs/debuffs they have."
Elemental spell casting builds effects (burns, chills), with "recently" mechanics
preventing chain-stun situations.

Character Progression
----------------------
Character creation uses "fixed stats, no roller." Players level at trainers rather
than via rest commands. Skill trees enable customization across four base classes
with three specialization paths each, replacing seven distinct classes.

Inventory & Equipment
----------------------
A dedicated potion slot prioritizes healing. Equipment slots indicate gear types.
Dragging gear displays compatible drop locations. Banks are account-wide rather
than per-character, with accessible regional wardrobe systems.

Gameplay Mechanics
-------------------
True instanced dungeons eliminate wait times. Nourishment requirements impose stat
penalties without food/water. Teleportation lacks distance limits. Lairs deposit
stripped gear in safe zones rather than danger areas. Daily quests and RNG boxes
provide regular engagement opportunities.

Additional Systems
-------------------
Enhanced GM tools, obtainable resources, star-rated item quality indicators, and
improved identification systems round out major improvements over Kingdom of
Drakkar's design.
