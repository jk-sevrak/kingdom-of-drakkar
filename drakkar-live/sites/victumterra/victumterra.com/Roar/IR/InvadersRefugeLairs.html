Santa

Winter holiday event
Find Santa who is roaming around in the IR dungeon
He hits with his weapon (bisento or halberd)
He can stun you
He can use a ranged %hp attack similar to banshees

He hits very hard (20k or more) and is difficult to hit until you bring him enough of his lost gifts (found randomly in the ir dungeon when slaying enemies)

Hold the gifts in hand and Santa will randomly take them
(Alternately you can hand them in to SantasHelper in the ir town but it is less effective)

If he kills you, he will strip you
