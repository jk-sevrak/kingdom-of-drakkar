Sdc Gnoll area quests
Progress past 78/39 (to 85/40) Collect 64 gold nuggets by killing ElderGnomes or picking up nuggets from the cave floor.

Head to the smelting pot, open the brew window and brew 8 nuggets at a time to make a fist sized nugget.
Brew 8 fist sized nuggets to make a large nugget.
Hand in the large gold nugget to Armon to be able to reach level 85 and skill 40.
Improve Killer Weapons Collect 8 leathers and 8 platemails by killing Gnomes, ElderGnomes, Crushers.

Take the 8 crystalline leathers to the smelting pot to smelt a crystalline extract, enchant it to +6

Take the 8 platemails to the smelting pot to smelt a silver extract.

Combine the +6 crystalline extract, the silver extract, a swamp water and a small gold nugget in the smelting pot to receive a refined fluidic crystal
Note: description of the swamp water
A bottle containing a putrid acidic fluid

Enchant the refined fluidic crystal to +6.
Bring it and your killer weapon to Alex to have the damage and blocking ability improved.
Key to Miner49er Collect 64 gold nuggets by killing ElderGnomes or picking up nuggets from the cave floor.

Head to the smelting pot, open the brew window and brew 8 nuggets at a time to make a fist sized nugget.
Brew 8 fist sized nuggets to make a large nugget.
Bring the large nugget to Modax to receive the key, hold the key in your hand, step on the hex in the southeast corner of gnoll cave.
