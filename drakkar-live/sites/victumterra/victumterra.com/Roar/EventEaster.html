To start the Easter event, head to nork steel flower and look for Lilly.


Lilly will ask you to find 4 eggs.


Head to nork-4 or nork-5 dungeons and kill orcs until you find 1 of each of the 4 eggs.
You can also head to maeling forest and kill wolves for the eggs.


***WARNING: Belt or locker your weapon before giving in the eggs, there is a chance you can have your weapon disappear, I just lost my killer halberd this way***

After you collect the 4 eggs, head back to Lilly.
Give the 4 eggs in to receive a jeweled easter egg.


Bring this egg to Mayleen to trade for an easter robe.


You can also eat the egg for various possible outcomes.
1) A gem can drop off the egg, sells for about 380k
2) If below 23 agility, the egg can give +1 to +3 agility
3) Takes your hp to 2 and poisons you
