Beetle:
Claws you, uses a %hp attack, casts Sw.
Kill and tan for his scales.
Location: down a hole in the swamps.
Notes: Steals exp when he claws you.
Death Sasquatch:
Hits with a club.
in his lair there are many psi crits around.
Drops: Possible water scroll, possible 10/0 ring, key to vani lair.
Tan him for great barb armor or trade armor for a king shield.
Location: on ud-5, up some steps.
Notes: Many psi crits are in lair, Sassy can hit twice in a round, has hit me for 2800.
AutoMoton:

Location: In lori's lair.
Loriapi:
Hits with a staff, casts espear, elance, shockwave, deplete.
Drops: Lori robe, Red robe, Lori staff, 5/5 sash, 8/8 ring, possible Yp or Statue (Always drops 1 of the 2).
In a hut around the southeast area of the jungle, her lair is up the stairs in the hut.
Note: automoton is also in lair.
Notes: Her staff hits will take 2 hp each if not wearing a lori robe.
