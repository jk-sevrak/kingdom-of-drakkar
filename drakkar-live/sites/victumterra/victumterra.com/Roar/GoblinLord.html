The Goblin Lord's lair is hidden in the south part of the first level of the Nork Dungeon, recognizable because the door is locked.  To enter, you must have a key, a random find on a goblin on that level. 
The key will open the door to the lair.  Kill the Goblin Lord and take an amythest and another key from him. 
This key opens a second door inside the lair. 
Inside that room, you will find the Goblin Lord's prisoner, Pelagis.  Show Pelagis the gem.  Tell him, "Pelagis, proof."  Pelagis will give you a ring.

Next, you must find Genetta on that level of the dungeon.  Hold both the amythest and the ring from Pelagis, and tell her, "Genetta, Pelagis."  She will give you the amulet she is holding.

Then, you must go to to Hector's square.  He is in the room adjacent to the stairs to Nork -1, directly south of the Steel Flower.  Drop both the amulet and the amythest on Hector's square and tell him, "Hector, heirloom."  You will receive a 1/1 ring with level 2 fire and ice protection


Goblin Lord: In actual play, it is difficult to almost impossible to find Genetta. She vanishes for a while after someone completes the quest. Also, neither she nor Hector acknowledge the player's presence, nor do they tell the player what reward he has received. The players should have consistent feedback.
