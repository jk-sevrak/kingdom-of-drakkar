The Mummy King's lair is hidden behind a set of locked double doors in the southeast area of N-3. To enter, you must have a key, a random drop on N-3. Open the double doors, and enter. The key will not vanish, because there is another set of locked double doors that must be opened with both the "Open Door" macro and the key, which now vanishes. The Mummy King, armed with a great sword, is inside. The doors will close behind you and lock themselves but this is a zone where twigs work. Kill the Mummy King and take him to be tanned. You will receive a tattered cloak.

Take this cloak to Wartberg, the NPC who stands behind the west Bank of Nork. Drop the cloak and 1999 coins, and tell him, "War, sell." He will take the cloak and give you a Protection from Stun amulet that operates at level 10. It has a random number of charges and will eventually fall to dust

Mummy King: In actual play, the Mummy King cannot be identified as such, as there is nothing to distinguish him from the usual run-of-the-mill mummies.
