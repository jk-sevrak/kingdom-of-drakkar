To start the winter event head to the norken inn sign and step on it.


Inside you will find Frosty, Maury and Santa.


Santa will ask you to find his reindeers, head over to Maury to collect walnuts.
You will need 9 walnuts (A minute wait between each walnut)

With the walnuts head out to search for the 9 reindeer.

Dashing is east of prisons
Cupid is southwest of maeling
Comet northeast of maeling along the cliff walls
Dancer is on the east side of yeti caves (on the outside)
Rudolf is near nork griffy climb up
Blixen is east of billy in frore
Prancer is behind nork hp doc building
Vixen is east of the cave leading out after the steps in nork rd lair
Donder is in the southwest corner of frore town

Hand each of them 1 walnut by holding it in hand and clicking them.
Return to santa to be rewarded with a Gingerbread man, 16 use instantheal.


Santa will then request that you recover the stolen gifts.
Head to nork-1 dungeon and kill until you find 1 of each of the 5 gifts.

Return to santa with the gifts, when holding them in right hand, tell santa presents.
Santa, Presents, after all 5 are given in, he will give you a glass of eggnog


Go see Frosty, he will ask you to find his lost charms.
Head to nork-1 dungeons and kill to find the 6 charms.

Lump of coal (Listed as a rock)
Pipe (Listed under gems)
Scarf (Listed as an amulet)
Carrot (Listed as a food)
Button (Listed under gems)
Hat (Listed as a helm)

Hold each one in right hand, click on frosty to turn them in, turn in the hat last.
Frosty will reward you with an atone (Positive alignment change)

If you are the first player to complete frosty's quest (Hint: his name is simply 'snowman' he will give you a +1 charisma pot) otherwise if his name is Frosty, you're too late




A newer addition to the winter event comes in the form of SantaMars.
Speak to him near the steel flower in nork to hear about a group of Sysops who've hit the eggnog a little to hard and have now turned into snowmen.

Your task is to find them since they have wondered off!

Tell SantaMars 'Lost' in order to learn what you can do to help

Santa mars will give you some hot cocoa in order to help the lost sysops find their way back

Tell SantaMars 'Cocoa' to gain some hot cocoa, you'll only need one but take two

With the hot cocoa in hand it is time to run around looking for them, you'll probably want to keep track of which ones you find as you find them.

When you find one of the missing sysops, click them with the hot cocoa in hand and they will take a sip and return home,

if you are lucky they will decide to keep the hot cocoa and in return give you a fancy tier!
