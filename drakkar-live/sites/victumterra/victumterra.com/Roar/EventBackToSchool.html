To start the back to school event, you must first travel to nork-1.
Kill anything that moves until you get a golden nugget
(Searches as a rock)
Pickup the nugget, head to nork-2.


Once on nork-2, start killing all the orc's you see, continue until you find a plaque (Searches under gems)
Pickup the plaque, head to nork-3.


Once on nork-3, start killing all the trolls you see, continue until you find a blue bottle
(Searches under bottles)
Pickup the bottle, head back to nork.


One in nork, make your way to the nork school building.


Go look for Mrs.Maeling, when you see her, hold the nugget in your right hand, click her.


Continue forward until you see Mr.Mjorin, hold the plaque in your right hand and click him.


Continue by going back the way you came until you see Mrs.Homlet, hold the blue bottle in your right hand and click her.


Return to Mr.Drakkarian for your reward.


You can do the event twice per character.
First reward: 10% to your main skill if the skill isn't capped.
Second reward: +1 permanent int.
(My ment has 26 base int now)
