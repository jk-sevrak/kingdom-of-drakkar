Additional information:
In an area of n-5, called the fireroom, there is a teleport hex that takes you to the nork dungeon 5 red dragon lair.
To get in, you need to have a protfire amulet equipped. (warning, merk attack amulet works as well and will vanish)
You can also use door, blast, grenades ect to get to lair in the southwest area of nork dungeons 5

N-5 secret area is located just north of the steps to n-4, behind a secret wall.

N-5 haunt area is located inside the n-5 secret area behind the northeast wall
