To start the Thanksgiving event, head south of the nork steel flower and look for Standish in the tent.


Standish will ask if you are interested in a turkey hunt.
Say Standish, yes
He will send you over to Samoset east of nork.

Head over to Samoset east of nork.
Click samoset, he will ask you if you want to help his people.
say Samoset, yes
He will give you an ear of corn
(Note, you can keep collecting corn from him, so get your luck to 18!)


Take the corn back to Standish.
With corn in hand, tell standish blunderbuss
Standish, Blunderbuss.
He will give you a blunderbuss for the corn.


With blunderbuss in hand, go slay turkeys!

Head to the areas indicated by the turkey icons in order to find turkeys! boom boom East of samoset

Once you have slain a turkey, take it to Samoset
Tell samoset turkey
Samoset, Turkey
He will give you some feathers



Go speak to Sacajewa, she will ask if you have seen any turkey feathers.
With feathers in hand, click her to receive a charisma amulet.
Note: There is a chance that you'll receive a special turkey tier instead.


You can also continue to say yes to Samoset to receive more corn, eating the corn gives +temp hp and has a chance to give +1 permanent luck up to 18.
